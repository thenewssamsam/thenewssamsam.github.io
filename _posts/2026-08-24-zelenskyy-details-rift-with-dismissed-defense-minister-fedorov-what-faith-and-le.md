  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The Fallout Behind Fedorov's Dismissal</h2>
    <p>During a candid two-hour meeting with journalists in Kyiv, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy addressed the political turmoil following the dismissal of Mykhailo Fedorov, a popular minister widely credited with modernizing Ukraine's armed forces and accelerating drone warfare technology. Fedorov's departure last month sparked weeks of mass protests and subsequent calls for wartime elections—a move Zelenskyy firmly rejected, warning that an election would act as a political tsunami capable of splitting the nation apart.</p>
    
    <p>Zelenskyy disclosed that Fedorov rejected multiple alternative government positions offered to him after his exit. Beyond political disagreements, the Ukrainian leader pointed to defense budget pressures, including a reported $27 billion shortfall, and an awkward diplomatic encounter with U.S. President Donald Trump. According to Zelenskyy, inaccurate Defense Ministry briefings led him to assure Washington that SpaceX had approved Starlink usage over Russian territory for deep strikes—a proposal Elon Musk later rejected, leaving the Ukrainian president to shoulder accusations of misrepresentation.</p>
