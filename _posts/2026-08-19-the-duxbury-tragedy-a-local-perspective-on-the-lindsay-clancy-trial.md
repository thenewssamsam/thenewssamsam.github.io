    <p>The quiet, coastal town of Duxbury, Massachusetts, remains forever changed by the events of early 2023. As the prosecution rests its case in the murder trial of Lindsay Clancy, the community is forced to revisit the harrowing details of a tragedy that claimed the lives of three young children: Cora, Dawson, and Callan. For local residents, this trial is not just a high-profile legal proceeding; it is a painful reckoning with the limits of our medical systems and the hidden struggles occurring behind suburban doors.</p>

    <p>After 14 days of testimony, the prosecution has concluded its case, presenting a narrative of deliberate intent. Prosecutors argue that Lindsay Clancy took the lives of her children before attempting to end her own life. The defense, led by attorney Kevin Reddington, maintains that Clancy was not criminally responsible due to severe mental health struggles, including postpartum issues and the impact of prescribed medications.</p>
