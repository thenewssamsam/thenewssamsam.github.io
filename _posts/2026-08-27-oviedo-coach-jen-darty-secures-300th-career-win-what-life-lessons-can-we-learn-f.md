  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened: A Milestone for Oviedo Volleyball</h2>
    <p>According to regional sports coverage, Coach Jen Darty reached her 300th career victory while coaching at Oviedo High School, a program where she herself once played as a student-athlete. Hitting the 300-win mark is no small feat in competitive high school athletics; it requires years of consistent performance, adaptability through shifting rosters of teenage athletes, and an unwavering stamina that lasts far beyond a single stellar season.</p>
    <p>While the box scores and match highlights capture the immediate excitement of the milestone, the deeper story lies in what it takes to sustain excellence at a single institution over a span of many years. In an era defined by rapid turnover and the constant search for the next quick fix, Darty's tenure at her alma mater stands as a rare and grounding example of community-rooted leadership.</p>
