<h2>What Happened</h2>
<p>On Thursday, North Korea launched a suspected ballistic missile toward the sea, according to reports from Japanese and South Korean officials. This provocative move came less than 24 hours after Pyongyang publicly brushed off a United States decision to scale back joint military exercises with South Korea.</p>
<p class="ncg-p">The diplomatic friction began when U.S. President Donald Trump ordered the Pentagon to substantially reduce the 11-day Ulchi Freedom Shield exercises. Trump pointed to a purported good relationship with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un and voiced frustration over South Korea's diplomatic positioning regarding the conflict in Iran. However, Kim Yo Jong—the influential sister of the North Korean leader—swiftly dismissed the gesture. She declared the move unworthy of comment, asserting that the aggressive nature of the drills remained unchanged despite the trimmed size and duration.</p>
