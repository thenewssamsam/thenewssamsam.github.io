    <h2>What Happened: The AFT Target Boycott</h2>
    <p>When the American Federation of Teachers (AFT) launched a nationwide back-to-school boycott against retail giant Target, it instantly turned aisles of notebooks and backpacks into a frontline for corporate and political activism. Led by AFT President Randi Weingarten, the union urged its 1.8 million members and families to skip Target for their seasonal shopping over the retailer's perceived silence regarding immigration enforcement (ICE) operations in its home state of Minnesota.</p>
