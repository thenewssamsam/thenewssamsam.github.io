<h2>What Happened</h2>
<p>South Korea's annual joint military drills with the United States, known as the 2026 Ulchi Freedom Shield exercise, have been abruptly shortened by six days following a direct request from Washington. The defense ministry in Seoul announced that both the period and scale of the exercises have been adjusted, setting a new conclusion date of August 21 instead of the originally planned August 27.</p>

President Donald Trump publicly linked the reduction to South Korea's decision not to participate in a military operation involving Iran. The unexpected policy pivot has instantly reshaped the operational landscape for tens of thousands of service members stationed in the region.</p>
