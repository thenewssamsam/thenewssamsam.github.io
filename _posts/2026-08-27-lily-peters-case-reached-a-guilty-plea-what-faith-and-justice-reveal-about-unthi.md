Chippewa County Judge Steven Gibbs accepted the plea, convicting Peters-Berger and sentencing him to life in prison with the possibility of extended supervision after 25 years. Additional charges of sexual assault were dismissed but read into the record.

Trials, Newell explained, carry immense risks regarding jury reactions and often inflict secondary trauma on grieving families. By accepting a guilty plea, the family is spared the harrowing ordeal of a trial, allowing them to turn their focus entirely toward healing and honoring Lily’s memory.
