  <p class="ncg-lead">When presidential envoy Jared Kushner sat down with Israel's prime minister to revive a stalled Gaza agreement, he stepped into one of the most stubborn diplomatic mazes in modern history. As former U.S. ambassador Daniel Shapiro recently observed on NPR, navigating the gap between optimistic diplomatic roadmaps and entrenched political realities is a delicate, high-stakes balancing act.</p>

  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in Jerusalem</h2>
  <p>Following high-profile discussions in Cairo and Washington, Jared Kushner traveled to Jerusalem to press forward with President Trump's 15-point plan aimed at ending the war in Gaza. On the surface, signals appear mixed. Hamas has indicated a willingness to engage on the framework, while Prime Minister Netanyahu has maintained a firm stance, insisting on full disarmament before any complete Israeli withdrawal.</p>
  
  <p>Speaking on Fox News after the meeting, Kushner highlighted the potential milestone of eliminating a major security threat if weapons and tunnels are surrendered over the coming months. However, seasoned observers like former Ambassador Daniel Shapiro point out that beneath the public statements lies a complex web of domestic politics, deep-seated mistrust, and strategic maneuvering by all involved parties.</p>
