<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the East Sea</h2>
<p>On Thursday, regional authorities in Japan and South Korea reported a sudden escalation as North Korea launched an unidentified projectile off its east coast. The Japanese prime minister’s office confirmed the detection of a suspected ballistic missile, though initial details remained sparse as defense analysts scrambled to track telemetry and trajectory data.</p>

<p>This launch did not happen in a vacuum. It arrived barely twenty-four hours after the United States and South Korea announced they were scaling back their 11-day Ulchi Freedom Shield military exercises. That drawdown followed instructions from U.S. President Donald Trump, who cited a desire to reshape relations and pointed to South Korea’s lack of reciprocal support regarding broader foreign policy friction points, such as the conflict in Iran.</p>
