  <section class="ncg-section">
    <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Memory Market?</h2>
    <p>In the fast-moving world of semiconductor manufacturing, few announcements carry the gravitational pull of an Nvidia earnings report. On Wednesday, August 27, 2026, Nvidia Corp. delivered another headline-grabbing quarterly performance, reporting that revenue more than doubled to $96.2 billion and posting adjusted net income of $2.22 per share, easily topping Wall Street consensus expectations.</p>
    
    <p>However, the real financial earthquake was buried deeper in the balance sheet disclosures and executive commentary. Nvidia CFO Colette Kress revealed that the company's future procurement commitments skyrocketed from $119 billion in the previous quarter to a staggering $279 billion. CEO Jensen Huang explained the driving force behind this massive capital allocation: memory chips have officially become the primary bottleneck constraining Nvidia's long-term growth outlook.</p>
