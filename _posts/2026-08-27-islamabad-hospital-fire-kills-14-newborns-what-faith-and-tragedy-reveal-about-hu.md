  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened at PIMS Hospital</h2>
  <p>
    The fire broke out around 7 a.m. local time inside the maternity ward nursery at PIMS, the largest government-run medical facility in Pakistan's capital. According to rescue sources, an air conditioning compressor explosion inside the nursery triggered the deadly blaze. Out of 16 infants in the unit, rescue workers managed to save only one child.
  </p>
  <p>
    The aftermath quickly exposed chaotic emergency conditions. Two sources at Islamabad's Capital Development Authority reported that hospital security closed the facility's main gates after the fire began—allegedly to prevent crowds from rushing the complex—which severely delayed first responders. While hospital spokespersons and officials have disputed these accounts, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has ordered an immediate investigation and suspended a top health department official as public outrage mounts over chronic underfunding, aging infrastructure, and institutional negligence in public healthcare.
  </p>
