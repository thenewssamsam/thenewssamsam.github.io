    <h2 style="font-size: 1.6rem; color: #2c3e50; border-bottom: 1px solid #eaeaea; padding-bottom: 8px; margin-top: 0;">What Happened in Billings</h2>
    <p>On a peaceful Sunday evening in Billings, Montana, a family gathering meant for sharing a meal and fellowship transformed into a horrific crime scene. According to reporting by The Associated Press, a man shot and killed eight of his own relatives—including four children—before taking his own life. Responding police officers arriving at the residence heard gunshots from the back just as the home became completely engulfed in flames.</p>
    
    <p>Family spokesperson Jennifer Mercer, who established an online fundraiser to assist with funeral costs, shared the heartbreaking scope of the loss. The victims spanned four distinct generations of a blended family, ranging from a great-grandmother in her nineties down to a seven-year-old girl. Neighbors like David Hiller initially mistook the terrifying sounds for fireworks, only to rush outside and find tactical police units taking cover as black plumes of smoke choked the Montana cul-de-sac.</p>
