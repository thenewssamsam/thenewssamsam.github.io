  <section class="ncg-section" style="margin-bottom: 35px;">
    <p>When leadership changes at the nation’s highest regulatory bodies, the ripple effects are felt across millions of kitchen tables, doctor's offices, and pharmacy counters. President Donald Trump’s announcement nominating Dr. Heidi Overton as the next commissioner of the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is no exception. As an agency responsible for overseeing everything from the food supply chain to life-saving therapeutics and complex reproductive medicines, the FDA occupies a uniquely influential space in modern governance.</p>
    
    <p>Yet, beyond the political maneuvering and headlines, this nomination opens up a deeper philosophical and moral discussion. In a society fractured by debates over medical autonomy, vaccine safety, and the sanctity of human life, how are believers to view institutions of power? And what does genuine stewardship look like when science, politics, and faith intersect?</p>
