<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in Moscow?</h2>
<p>During the bilateral meeting held at the Foreign Ministry's representative office in Moscow, Archbishop Gallagher and Sergey Lavrov discussed several areas of convergence on international issues. Accompanied by the Apostolic Nuncio, Archbishop Giovanni d'Aniello, the Vatican delegation spent more than an hour in discussions that touched on humanitarian initiatives, the welfare of the Catholic community in Russia, and pathways toward future peace.</p>

<p>Speaking to the press after the meeting, Archbishop Gallagher reiterated the Holy See's unwavering stance: the conflict cannot be resolved through military means alone. Instead, he stressed the urgent necessity of creating conditions for genuine political and diplomatic processes. He called upon both Russia and Ukraine to demonstrate openness, goodwill, and strict adherence to international and humanitarian law—particularly regarding the protection of civilians, vital infrastructure, the wounded, and prisoners of war.</p>
