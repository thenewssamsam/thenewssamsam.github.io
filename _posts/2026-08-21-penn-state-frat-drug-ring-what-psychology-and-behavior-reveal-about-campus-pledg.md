    <h2>What Happened: The Network and the Charges</h2>
    <p>According to court documents released by the Pennsylvania Attorney General's office, a sprawling drug distribution network transported cocaine from New York and Philadelphia directly to State College. Investigators allege that the operation was orchestrated by senior fraternity members, including Thomas Robinson and Agostino Abbatiello, with drugs being cut, packaged, and distributed from within the Delta Upsilon and Sigma Chi fraternity houses.</p>

    <h3>The Mechanics of Indoctrination</h3>
    <p>Investigators stated that the cutting and packaging of cocaine was specifically used to indoctrinate fraternity pledges. In psychological terms, this is a severe escalation of hazing tactics. When a group forces a new recruit to participate in illicit activity as a rite of passage, they leverage <em>cognitive dissonance</em> and <em>sunk cost fallacy</em>. The pledge thinks, "I have already risked too much to walk away now," binding their personal identity to the survival and secrecy of the group.</p>

This illustrates an extreme form of <em>normalcy bias</em>—an inability to process a catastrophic reality because it violates the insular, invulnerable bubble the fraternity had constructed for itself.</p>
