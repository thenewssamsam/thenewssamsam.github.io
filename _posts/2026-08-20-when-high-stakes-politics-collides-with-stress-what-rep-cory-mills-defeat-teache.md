<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in Florida</h2>

<p>President Donald Trump's endorsement is often seen as an unyielding shield in Republican primaries. Yet on Tuesday night, that backing proved insufficient to save Rep. Cory Mills from a stunning upset defeat against former Orlando-area news anchor Ryan Elijah. Mills, seeking a third term representing Florida's 7th Congressional District, found his campaign weighed down by an ongoing House Ethics Committee investigation and unconfirmed allegations of a federal probe.</p>
