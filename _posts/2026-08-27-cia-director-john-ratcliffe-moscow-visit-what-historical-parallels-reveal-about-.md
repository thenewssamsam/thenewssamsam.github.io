<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: The Secret Moscow Mission</h2>
<p>The details emerging from Washington, London, and European capitals paint a picture of extraordinary diplomatic friction. CIA Director John Ratcliffe flew into Moscow aboard a U.S. military transport originating from Latvia. The trip, initially unacknowledged by the White House, immediately sparked rampant speculation across the international community.</p>

<p>According to sources familiar with the discussions, Ratcliffe’s primary objective was to deliver a blunt warning: Moscow must refrain from any escalatory military action or hybrid warfare tactics against NATO’s frontline members—specifically Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania. With the war in Ukraine deadlocked and drone strikes crossing borders regularly, Washington felt compelled to draw a definitive line regarding the Baltic states.</p>
