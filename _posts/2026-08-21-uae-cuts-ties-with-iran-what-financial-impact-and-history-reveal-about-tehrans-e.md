Now, with that $21 billion lifeline severed following a ballistic missile scare near maritime traffic, Iran faces an unprecedented economic squeeze as deadlock persists with Washington.</p>

The UAE government stated that all commercial exchanges and financial ties with Iran would cease "until further notice." The catalyst? Emirati officials reported that two ballistic missiles launched from Iran fell into the sea, directly targeting maritime navigation lanes.</p>
