<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: The Economic D-Day Announcement</h2>

<p>Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is scheduled to reveal details of a sweeping new financial pressure campaign against Iran in a Washington news conference. Writing in a <em>Financial Times</em> op-ed, Bessent warned that “we are entering the endgame” and promised the “single greatest financial offensive ever marshalled against an adversary.”</p>

<p>The announcement arrives amid a grinding six-month conflict that has drained Iranian resources, damaged its infrastructure, and strained domestic leadership. Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian recently acknowledged the severe domestic toll, stating in state media that the nation “cannot continue with war forever,” even as hardline elements within Tehran's security apparatus vow retaliation and threaten to choke off transit through the vital Strait of Hormuz.</p>

<p>At the same time, the economic shockwaves are crossing oceans. In the United States, elevated gas prices persist, and Federal Reserve officials have cautioned that inflation targets remain out of reach until the Middle East conflict finds resolution.</p>
