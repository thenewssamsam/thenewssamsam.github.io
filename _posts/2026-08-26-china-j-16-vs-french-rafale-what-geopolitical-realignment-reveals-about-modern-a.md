<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Skies Over Egypt?</h2>

<p>According to reports from regional defense outlets and state news services, Chinese PLA Air Force pilots deployed their heavy twin-seat J-16 fighters to Egypt for high-stakes, one-on-one combat drills. These exercises tested medium- and close-range maneuvering under realistic battlefield conditions against Egypt’s established fleet of French-designed Rafale jets.</p>

<p>Egypt operates roughly 55 Rafales under contracts with Dassault Aviation, making the North African nation one of the largest non-European operators of the French aircraft. By inviting the Chinese J-16 into its airspace, Cairo is signaling a remarkably diversified defense strategy—one that balances Western, Russian, and now increasingly prominent Chinese hardware.</p>
