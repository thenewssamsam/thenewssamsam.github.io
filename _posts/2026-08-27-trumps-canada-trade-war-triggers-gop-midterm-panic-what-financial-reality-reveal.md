    <h2>What Happened: A Trade War on the Northern Border</h2>
    <p>Donald Trump is facing growing alarm from within his own party after a sudden collapse in trade talks with Canada led to sweeping 50 percent tariffs on roughly $20 billion worth of Canadian goods. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney quickly retaliated by doubling existing tariffs on American steel and aluminum while targeting a broad range of additional U.S. exports, bringing the total value of affected American goods to an estimated $20 billion.</p>
    <p>Rather than backing down, the administration has doubled down on Truth Social, even jokingly floating names like "Lake America" for Lake Ontario and calling Canada "the most difficult and unreasonable" trading partner. But while the rhetoric plays out online, the downstream economic effects are hitting home in crucial battleground states just weeks before the midterms, turning political strategy into a high-stakes scramble over household budgets and grocery bills.</p>
