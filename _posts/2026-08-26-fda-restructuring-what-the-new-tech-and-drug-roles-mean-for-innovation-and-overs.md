  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The Proposed FDA Overhaul</h2>
    <p>According to reports, HHS is moving to create two specialized deputy commissioner roles reporting directly to Heidi Overton, President Donald Trump's pick for FDA commissioner who awaits Senate confirmation. One position will center on technology, explicitly bridging health and artificial intelligence, while the second will focus entirely on drugs.</p>
    
    <p>Sources indicate that Jared Seehafer—a senior advisor to the FDA, life sciences compliance software co-founder, and former tech CEO—is the leading candidate for the technology leadership post. Meanwhile, candidates for the drugs deputy commissioner role have not yet been publicly identified. No formal announcements have been finalized, and HHS has declined to comment on the developing plans.</p>

    <div class="ncg-quote-box">
      <p>"The new technology deputy commissioner would oversee both the agency's internal use of AI and its evaluation of external AI-enabled medical products."</p>
