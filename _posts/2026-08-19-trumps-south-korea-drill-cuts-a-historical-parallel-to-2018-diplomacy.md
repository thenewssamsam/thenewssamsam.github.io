    <h2>The Current Situation</h2>
    <p>The annual "Ulchi Freedom Shield" exercises, involving approximately 18,000 South Korean personnel, began this Monday. However, the U.S. presence has been curtailed following President Trump's directive to Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. Trump justified the move by pointing to his "very good" relationship with Kim Jong Un and expressed frustration that Seoul has not provided sufficient support in the U.S. conflict with Iran.</p>
  </section>
