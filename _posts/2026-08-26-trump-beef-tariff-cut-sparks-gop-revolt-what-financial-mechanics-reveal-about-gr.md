<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened: The Tariff Pause Explained</h2>
<p>The White House announced a temporary suspension of tariffs on foreign beef imports, aiming to introduce 300,000 metric tons of foreign beef into the American supply chain at 25% below current market prices. Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins defended the policy as a necessary short-term fix to ease sticker shock at the supermarket checkout line. However, the announcement caught senior congressional Republicans off guard.</p>

<p>Prominent GOP lawmakers—including Wyoming Senator John Barrasso, Nebraska Senator Pete Ricketts, Montana Senator Tim Sheehy, and Iowa congressional candidate Ashley Hinson—pushed back hard. Their primary concern is that cheap imports will disincentivize domestic ranchers from rebuilding depleted herds, ultimately harming the domestic agricultural economy.</p>
