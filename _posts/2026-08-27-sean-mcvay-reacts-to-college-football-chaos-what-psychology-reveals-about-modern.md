<h2>The Psychology of Athletic Overload and Normalcy Bias</h2>
<p>Sean McVay’s humorous reaction captures more than just exasperation; it highlights a profound psychological state known as cognitive overload paired with normalcy bias. For decades, the boundaries between amateur collegiate sports and professional leagues were rigidly defined. Fans, coaches, and players relied on mental models that assumed a one-way street: you play in college, you enter the draft, and you turn pro.</p>
