Counterintelligence agencies are no longer just tracking foreign operatives; they are monitoring petty criminals, disaffected citizens, and radicalized loners activated instantly via digital wallets and encrypted messaging. The result is a persistent state of low-intensity friction that wears down public confidence and strains democratic resilience.</p>
