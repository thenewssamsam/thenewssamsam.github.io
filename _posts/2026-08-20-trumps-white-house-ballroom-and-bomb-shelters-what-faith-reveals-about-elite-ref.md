    <p>
      When headlines focus on elite bunkers and political battles over historical landmarks, followers of Christ are invited to respond with discernment rather than anxiety or cynicism:
    </p>
    <ul style="padding-left: 20px; color: #4a5568;">
      <li style="margin-bottom: 10px;"><strong>Resist the spirit of fear:</strong> Headlines detailing potential threats and underground shelters can easily stoke anxiety.
