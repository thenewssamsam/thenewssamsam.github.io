    <h2 class="ncg-h2">What Happened to the Swift Rescue Mission?</h2>
    <p>Launched originally in 2004, the Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory has spent more than two decades peering into the cosmos, tracking gamma-ray bursts and far-flung cosmic phenomena far beyond its original mission expectations. However, orbiting roughly 347 kilometers above Earth, Swift's altitude has been shrinking due to atmospheric drag, accelerated recently by increased solar activity.</p>
