As the Sunshine State prepares for a campaign that will test both Donald Trump’s influence and the Democratic Party's relevance, it is worth looking back at the cycles of change that have defined Florida’s political geography.</p>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Results at a Glance</h2>
    <p>In a decisive primary night, Byron Donalds secured the Republican nomination for governor, while former Republican-turned-Democrat David Jolly claimed the Democratic nod. The general election will see these two figures vie to replace outgoing Governor Ron DeSantis. Meanwhile, in a surprise Senate outcome, progressive state Rep. Angie Nixon defeated Army veteran Alexander Vindman to challenge Republican Sen. Ashley Moody, who was appointed to fill Marco Rubio’s seat.</p>
  </section>

Much like the transition periods of the mid-20th century, where the state began its slow migration from a Solid South Democratic stronghold to a competitive battleground, we are witnessing a new realignment today.</p>
