  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The Summer After the Shot</h2>
    <p>For the New York Knicks, the summer of 2026 has been an unending whirlwind. After snapping a grueling 53-year championship hex with an improbable comeback against the San Antonio Spurs, the team has been treated to non-stop parades, late-night television appearances, and key-to-the-city ceremonies. Yet, amid the chaotic ticker-tape adulation, forward OG Anunoby has remained remarkably detached.</p>

    </div>
    <p>Interviewed on a brown leather sofa in August, wearing a black hoodie and sporting his signature deadpan demeanor, Anunoby reflected on his famous Game 4 buzzer-beating tip-in—now affectionately dubbed the "Hand of God" by breathless fans. When asked about moving past the viral fame, Anunoby clarified that while he appreciates the moment, he is already looking forward. "You have to move forward," he noted simply, signaling a rare emotional groundedness in a sports culture that often demands endless self-celebration.</p>

  <section id="the-weight-of-glory">
    <h2>The Weight of Glory and Human Pride</h2>
    <p>Human beings are naturally wired to chase validation. Whether it is a championship ring in Madison Square Garden, a promotion at work, or societal applause, we love to bask in the warmth of achievement. Yet, Scripture consistently warns us about the seductive, fleeting nature of worldly glory.</p>

    <h2>A Scripture Perspective on Success and Tomorrow</h2>
    <p>When I read about athletes grappling with the sudden onset of fame and the immediate pressure to defend yesterday’s achievements, it makes me think about how easily our hearts anchor themselves to temporary victories. We pour so much energy into scaling our own personal mountains, only to discover that the summit is just a starting line for the next exhausting climb.</p>

    <h2>A Prayer for Wisdom and Hope</h2>
    <p>Heavenly Father, we thank You for the blessings, victories, and milestones You place in our lives. Help us to celebrate them with joyful hearts, but guard us against the snare of pride and the heavy burden of seeking our worth in fleeting applause. Give us the wisdom to stay levelheaded, to release yesterday’s triumphs, and to faithfully steward the opportunities You give us today. May our deepest identity always be found in Your unchanging love rather than the changing opinions of the world. In Jesus' name, Amen.</p>
