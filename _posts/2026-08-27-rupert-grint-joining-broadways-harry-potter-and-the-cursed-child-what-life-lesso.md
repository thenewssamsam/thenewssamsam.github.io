  <section class="ncg-section" id="what-happened">
    <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened?</h2>
    <p>Nearly two decades after the cinematic journey of the wizarding world concluded, another core member of Hogwarts royalty is stepping back onto the stage. Rupert Grint—who famously portrayed the loyal, brave, and deeply human Ron Weasley across all eight <em>Harry Potter</em> films—is officially heading to Broadway. Grint will take over the role of adult Ron in the hit stage production <em>Harry Potter and the Cursed Child</em> at New York City's Lyric Theatre.</p>
    <p>According to reports from Deadline, Grint is slated for a 17-week limited engagement running from Tuesday, February 2, 2027, through Sunday, May 30, 2027. He follows in the footsteps of former co-star Tom Felton, the original cinematic Draco Malfoy, who currently anchors the Broadway production before his scheduled departure in January 2027. While Grint and Felton won't share the stage simultaneously, their consecutive runs mark a monumental nostalgic moment for a generation of fans who grew up alongside them.</p>
    <p>Producers Sonia Friedman and Colin Callender highlighted the emotional resonance of Grint's return, noting that he is not merely revisiting his past, but carrying forward a journey that began when he was just eleven years old. For Grint, who is now a father, stepping back into Ron Weasley's robes offers a unique mirror to his own personal evolution.</p>
