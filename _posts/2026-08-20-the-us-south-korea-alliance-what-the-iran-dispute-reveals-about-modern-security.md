The result was a dramatic truncation of exercises from 11 days to five, catching South Korean officials completely off guard.</p>

However, just as the US-UK relationship faced friction during the Suez Crisis of 1956—where differing strategic priorities led to a public rupture—we are seeing a similar realignment today.</p>
