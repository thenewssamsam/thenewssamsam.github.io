    <h2>What Happened in the Latest Polls</h2>
    <p>President Donald Trump’s second-term approval rating remains mired at a record low, according to the latest Reuters/Ipsos survey released in August 2026. Holding steady at 33%—with disapproval ticking up to 65%—the numbers reflect a compounding voter discontent driven primarily by foreign policy and economic friction. Most notably, public support for U.S. military action in Iran has slumped to just 31%, down from 37% earlier in the year.</p>
