    <h2>What Happened in Tuesday's Primaries</h2>
    <p>Tuesday night's primary elections across South Carolina, Oklahoma, and Georgia delivered a mixed bag of results, but the crown jewel for the White House was undoubtedly the Palmetto State. Darline Graham, who had no prior political experience and famously admitted during a debate that national security was "not my thing," claimed the GOP nomination after a fierce runoff against Rep. Ralph Norman.</p>

    <p>The path to victory was far from guaranteed. After Gov. Henry McMaster initially appointed her to fill the remaining months of her late brother's term in July, Trump aggressively lobbied her to run for a full six-year term. When polling showed her struggling against Norman—who had previously campaigned with former rival Nikki Haley—Trump intervened directly, holding a rally in Myrtle Beach and deploying over $827,000 via his super PAC, MAGA Inc. The narrow win provides a vital morale boost just over two months before the November midterms.</p>
