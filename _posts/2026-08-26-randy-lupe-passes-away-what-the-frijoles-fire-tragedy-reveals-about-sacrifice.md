<h2 id="what-happened" class="ncg-section-heading">What Happened</h2>
<p class="ncg-paragraph">
The wildland fire community is mourning the loss of Randy Lupe, a 62-year-old support crew member from the San Carlos Apache Indian Reservation in Arizona. According to updates from KOB and the Department of the Interior, Lupe suffered a fatal medical emergency on Sunday while providing vital temporary emergency support at the San Carlos camp during the Frijoles Fire response.
</p>
<p class="ncg-paragraph">
U.S. Wildland Fire Service Chief Brian Fennessy expressed deep sorrow over the event, noting that every member of the wildland fire workforce plays an indispensable role in safeguarding our communities. Out of respect for his family and loved ones, further medical details are not being publicly released, though support systems have been mobilized by the Interior Department to comfort his grieving colleagues and family members.
</p>
