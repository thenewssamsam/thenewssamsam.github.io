  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Current Investigation</h2>
    <p>On August 17, 2026, the House Ethics Committee announced a formal probe into whether Rep. Jimmy Gomez violated conduct rules regarding sexual misconduct. While Gomez has admitted to "personal mistakes" in his marriage, he maintains that these interactions were consensual. Conversely, attorneys for an accuser have disputed this characterization, emphasizing a lack of consent. The investigation remains ongoing, and Chairman Michael Guest and ranking member Mark DeSaulnier have stressed that the existence of a probe does not constitute proof of guilt.</p>

    <h2>The Psychology of Power and Misconduct</h2>
    <p>From a behavioral perspective, the repeated allegations against members of Congress—regardless of party—suggest the presence of "power-induced disinhibition." Psychological research indicates that individuals in positions of high authority often experience a diminished sense of empathy and an increased belief that they are exempt from the social norms that govern others.</p>
