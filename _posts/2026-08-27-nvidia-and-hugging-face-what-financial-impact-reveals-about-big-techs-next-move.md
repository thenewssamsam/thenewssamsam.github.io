What started as minor venture capital participation has quickly escalated into an all-out corporate consolidation of the infrastructure powering modern machine learning.</p>

<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Nvidia-Hugging Face Deal?</h2>
<p>According to reports from <em>The Information</em> and <em>Business Insider</em>, Nvidia is finalizing a deal to purchase Hugging Face at a valuation topping $13 billion. The transaction is still being finalized and could theoretically face hurdles, but the trajectory shows a rapid escalation from previous negotiations.</p>

<p>Back in 2023, Nvidia participated in Hugging Face's Series D funding round alongside giants like Google and Amazon, valuing the repository at $4.5 billion. However, Nvidia wanted a deeper foothold. Sometime toward the end of last year, Nvidia attempted a massive $500 million direct investment that would have valued the platform at $7 billion. Hugging Face balked at the offer, fearing a single investor would hold too much sway over corporate decision-making.</p>
