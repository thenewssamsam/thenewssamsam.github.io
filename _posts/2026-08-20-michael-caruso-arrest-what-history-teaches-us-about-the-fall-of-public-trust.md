  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Palm Beach County Case</h2>
  <p>On Tuesday morning, Palm Beach County Clerk of Court Michael Caruso, 67, was arrested and immediately suspended from office by Florida Governor Ron DeSantis. Caruso faces five severe felony charges, including kidnapping, lewd or lascivious molestation, lewd or lascivious exhibition, luring or enticing a child, and child abuse causing mental injury.</p>

  <p>According to court documents filed in Orange County, the charges stem from an extensive investigation by the Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE). The victim detailed a disturbing timeline of abuse beginning in November 2024—when Caruso allegedly targeted the child during a private fishing trip—culminating in severe abuse aboard a seven-day Royal Caribbean cruise in August 2025. Investigators noted that when family members confronted Caruso in December 2025, he made partial admissions alongside explicit threats to avoid police involvement.</p>

  <p>Following his arrest, state officials moved swiftly to protect public integrity. Shannon Ramsey-Chessman was sworn in as clerk ad interim to ensure continuity of operations for the 650 employees and residents relying on the office. Special prosecutors in the Ninth Judicial Circuit are handling the ongoing case, and Caruso is currently held without bond in the Orange County Jail.</p>
