  <section id="news-hook">
    <p>In a stunning political upset, Florida state Representative Angie Nixon has defeated former National Security Council aide Alex Vindman in the Democratic primary for the U.S. Senate. Despite being significantly outspent, Nixon’s grassroots campaign successfully challenged the establishment-backed candidate, setting the stage for a high-stakes general election against Republican incumbent Ashley Moody.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Primary Results</h2>
    <p>Nixon, a member of the Democratic Socialists of America, secured the nomination with a platform centered on Medicare for all, universal child care, and a critique of current U.S. foreign policy. Alex Vindman, who rose to national prominence during the 2019 impeachment hearings, had the backing of the Democratic Senatorial Campaign Committee (DSCC) and raised over $16 million. Nixon’s victory, achieved with less than $1 million, marks a significant shift in Florida’s Democratic landscape.</p>
