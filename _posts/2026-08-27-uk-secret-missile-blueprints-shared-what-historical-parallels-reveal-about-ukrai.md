<h2 id="what-happened" class="ncg-section-heading">What Happened: The SCALP Transfer</h2>

<p>During his inaugural visit to Kyiv, newly appointed U.K. Prime Minister Andy Burnham announced that London is providing Ukraine with sensitive technical blueprints for British-made components of the SCALP cruise missile—known as the Storm Shadow in its British variant. This follows France granting a production license for the weapon back in July, aligning Western allies in an effort to empower Ukraine's domestic defense capabilities.</p>
