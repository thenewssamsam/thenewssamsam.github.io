    <h2 id="what-happened" style="font-size: 1.5rem; color: #2d3748; margin-top: 30px; margin-bottom: 15px;">What Happened at the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences?</h2>
    
    <p>Early Wednesday morning, a catastrophic tragedy unfolded inside the gynecology ward nursery at the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS) in Islamabad. According to hospital officials and emergency responders, a sudden explosion of an air-conditioning unit compressor triggered a fast-moving fire within the enclosed unit.</p>
    
    <p>At the time of the blast, at least 15 newborns were receiving intensive care in the nursery. Firefighters quickly arrived at the scene and extinguished the flames, managing to rescue just one infant. Tragically, 14 newborns lost their lives just days after entering the world. Senior nurse Noreen Khan described the harrowing scene to reporters, noting that the infants had barely opened their eyes to life before the tragedy struck.</p>
