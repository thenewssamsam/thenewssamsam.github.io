<h2 id="what-happened" style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; color: #1a202c; margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 1rem;">What Happened in Dearborn?</h2>
<p>On Tuesday, a regularly scheduled Dearborn City Council meeting turned into a massive security operation. Anticipating friction, city officials had already relocated the meeting from its usual venue to the Henry Ford Centennial Library. Led by conservative activist Jake Lang, demonstrators gathered for the "Christian Crusader March," drawing hundreds of participants to the surrounding area, including Detroit pastor Lorenzo Sewell and out-of-state arrivals from as far away as New York and Tennessee.</p>

<p>According to Dearborn Police Chief Issa Shahin, officers faced a "challenging and rapidly evolving situation." While the majority of the demonstrators remained peaceful, several incidents necessitated police intervention. In total, 22 people ranging in age from 18 to 64 were arrested on misdemeanor charges including breach of peace and disorderly conduct. Mutual aid partners, including the Michigan State Police and Wayne County Sheriff's Office, assisted local police in keeping the peace without any reported injuries.</p>
