  <section class="ncg-section" id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened in Worcester</h2>
    <p>A community is pausing this week to say a final farewell to a man who spent more than three decades wearing a badge. A funeral mass is set for Wednesday morning at Our Lady of the Angels Church in Worcester for veteran Police Officer Kurt Solomon, who was found dead inside his Wildwood Avenue home on August 18 at the age of 57.</p>
    <p>The sorrow surrounding his passing runs deep, compounded by a multi-agency search that ended when authorities discovered the body of 58-year-old Karen Solomon—identified in court records as his wife—in a wooded area behind a local rehabilitation hospital. Investigators are now treating the heartbreaking case as a murder-suicide, leaving family, friends, and fellow officers grappling with a profound void.</p>
    <p>Officer Solomon was a father of two, a U.S. Army veteran, and a 31-year veteran of the Worcester Police Department. In statements following the recovery, his family expressed deep gratitude to law enforcement agencies for their unwavering support, describing themselves as "heartbroken and rudderless" yet finding flickers of light amidst the darkness thanks to the empathy of those around them. Worcester Mayor Joe Petty and local police unions echoed these sentiments, remembering Solomon's warm smile and caring personality at City Council meetings.</p>
