  <section class="ncg-section" id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened in the Markets</h2>
    <p>On Wednesday, Wall Street witnessed an extraordinary session as shares of Moderna Inc. (MRNA), Merck & Co. Inc. (MRK), and Target Corp. (TGT) all surged to new 52-week highs. The catalyst for the healthcare giants was fundamentally scientific, while the retail giant's jump was driven by robust earnings reports and subsequent Wall Street price target upgrades.</p>
    
    <p>Moderna experienced its steepest climb on record, with its stock rocketing nearly 177% to close at $176.66. Its partner in the venture, pharmaceutical titan Merck, saw its stock jump over 12% to an annual high of $153.50—its strongest single-session gain since March 2009. Meanwhile, retail heavyweight Target closed up more than 4% at $161.98 following a strong second-quarter earnings report that beat analyst consensus estimates.</p>
