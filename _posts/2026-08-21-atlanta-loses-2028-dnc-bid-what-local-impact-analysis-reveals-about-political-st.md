<section id="what-happened">
  <h2>What Happened</h2>
  <p>According to local reporting from WSB-TV Atlanta and related regional updates, the Democratic National Committee (DNC) will not bring its 2028 national convention to Atlanta. For a city that has increasingly positioned itself as a pivotal political battleground and a powerhouse for business, culture, and tourism in the American Southeast, the news came as a significant moment of reassessment.</p>
  <p>While convention bids involve intricate calculations regarding venue capacity, fundraising potential, security logistics, and broader electoral maps, missing out on a convention leaves a tangible footprint on local expectations. City leaders and hospitality stakeholders who had anticipated a massive influx of visitors and economic activity are now left evaluating alternative pathways for growth and civic promotion.</p>
