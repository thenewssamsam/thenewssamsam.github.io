    <h2>What Happened in Billings</h2>
    <p>News reports out of Billings, Montana, describe an unfathomable tragedy that unfolded quietly on a Sunday evening outside city limits. Authorities confirmed that eight members of a single extended family—spanning four generations—were shot and killed inside a family home on White Star Circle. The victims included the elderly homeowners, Dana and Barbara Smith (ages 73 and 72), the family matriarch Evelyn Smith (92), Megan Ireland-Ghekiere (39), and four children: Owen Ireland (13), Chloe Ireland (7), Landon Ghekiere (15), and Charlotte Ghekiere (12).</p>
