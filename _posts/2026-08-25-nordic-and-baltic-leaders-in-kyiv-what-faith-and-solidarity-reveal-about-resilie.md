  <section id="what-happened" style="margin-bottom: 2.5rem;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; color: #1a202c; border-bottom: 2px solid #3182ce; padding-bottom: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 1rem;">What Happened: A Gathering at the Railway Station</h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      On August 23, 2026, the Kyiv Railway Station welcomed leaders and senior representatives from Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Latvia, Lithuania, and Norway. Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha greeted the visiting dignitaries, capturing the emotional weight of the moment with a declaration shared widely on social media: "We feel we are not alone. Our allies are with us."
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      This visit precedes Ukraine's National Flag Day and Independence Day celebrations, coming exactly four and a half years after the onset of the devastating war. Lithuanian President Gitanas Nauseda emphasized his commitment, stating that he would participate in upcoming discussions with Nordic-Baltic counterparts and the broader Coalition of the Willing meetings. For a nation enduring relentless bombardment and missile terror, the physical presence of international friends serves as a vital reminder that steadfast alliances remain unbroken.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section id="defense-and-diplomacy" style="margin-bottom: 2.5rem;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; color: #1a202c; border-bottom: 2px solid #3182ce; padding-bottom: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 1rem;">Defense, Industry, and Practical Cooperation</h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      Diplomacy during wartime requires more than symbolic handshakes; it demands tangible collaboration to protect civilian lives. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy utilized the visit to advance concrete industrial partnerships. During a roundtable on Ukrainian-Finnish defense cooperation alongside Finnish President Alexander Stubb, vital agreements were sealed.
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      Finnish defense firms—including Insta, Boomeranger, and Patria—signed direct agreements with Ukrainian counterparts. These pacts focus on the joint development and production of drones in Finland tailored for Ukraine and broader European security, the expansion of uncrewed naval systems, and the establishment of manufacturing facilities for ground-based unmanned vehicles (UGVs). President Stubb noted that both nations stand as mutual beneficiaries in this industrial defense integration.
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      Furthermore, Zelenskyy met with former U.S. Vice President Mike Pence during Kyiv's National Prayer Breakfast, pressing the urgent need for enhanced air defense capabilities, specifically the licensing and provision of Patriot interceptors to counter ongoing ballistic missile terror ahead of the winter months.
    </p>
  </section>
