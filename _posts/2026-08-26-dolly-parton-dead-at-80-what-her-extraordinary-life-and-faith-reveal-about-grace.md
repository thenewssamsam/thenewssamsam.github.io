  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The Passing of an Icon</h2>
    <p>According to family statements and her official team, Dolly Rebecca Parton Dean died peacefully on Tuesday surrounded by her loved ones. Her nephew, Brian Seaver, who served as her head of security for over two decades, shared the somber news in a video posted to Instagram. Seaver noted that his aunt had faced a brief battle with cancer in her final days, but reassured fans that she ultimately "lived in the light and is in the arms of Jesus."</p>
    
    <p>President Donald Trump ordered U.S. flags to be lowered to half-staff nationwide for one week, while tributes flooded in from entertainment legends, world leaders, and millions of ordinary fans whose lives she touched. Her passing follows a challenging period that included the death of her beloved husband of nearly 60 years, Carl Dean, in March 2025, and her own ongoing recovery from kidney stones and immune system fatigue.</p>
  </section>

Her father paid for medical services with a sack of meal; her mother sang traditional Appalachian ballads that sparked a lifelong musical fire. By age 13, she was performing on local radio and stepping onto the hallowed stage of the Grand Ole Opry.</p>

As Paul wrote in <strong>Colossians 3:12</strong> (ESV): <em>"Put on then, as God's chosen ones, holy and beloved, compassionate hearts, kindness, humility, meekness, and patience."</em></p>
