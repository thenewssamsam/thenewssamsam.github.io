    <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Florida GOP Primary?</h2>
    <p>On Tuesday, Representative Byron Donalds decisively won the Republican nomination in Florida's governor's race, capturing 47.8 percent of the vote with 99 percent reporting, according to Associated Press figures. He outpaced Lieutenant Governor Jay Collins, who secured 25.2 percent, and investor James Fishback at 10.5 percent. Donalds, who has represented Florida's 19th District in Congress since 2021 and earned an endorsement from President Donald Trump, will now face David Jolly in November.</p>
    
    <p>As the campaign moves forward, public interest has naturally extended to Donalds' family life. Married for over two decades, Byron and Erika Donalds have raised three sons—Damon, Darin, and Mason—while navigating both corporate careers and state-level political activism. Erika Donalds has been a prominent figure in conservative educational reform, founding what is now known as the Education Freedom Foundation and serving as a chair for the America First Policy Institute.</p>
