<p>When elections shake up local representation, the ripple effects hit communities long before the dust settles in Washington. In Central Florida's 7th Congressional District, voters made a definitive choice on Tuesday, Aug. 18, 2026, ending embattled incumbent Republican Rep. Cory Mills's bid for a third term and choosing former local television news anchor Ryan Elijah in the GOP primary.</p>

<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened in the 7th District?</h2>
<p>According to race calls by The Associated Press, Ryan Elijah secured victory over Rep. Cory Mills by roughly 12 percentage points. The primary race unfolded against a backdrop of intense scrutiny surrounding Mills, who has faced an active House Ethics Committee investigation over allegations of domestic abuse and campaign finance violations. Mills has consistently denied any wrongdoing.</p>
