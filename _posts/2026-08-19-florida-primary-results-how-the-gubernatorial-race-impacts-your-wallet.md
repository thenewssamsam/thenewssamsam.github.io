<p>The dust has settled on Florida’s primary elections, setting the stage for a high-stakes gubernatorial showdown between Republican U.S. Rep. Byron Donalds and Democratic nominee David Jolly. While political pundits are focused on party control and national implications, the most pressing question for everyday Floridians concerns the state’s financial trajectory. <section id="what-happened">
<h2>The Path to November</h2>
<p>Byron Donalds secured the Republican nomination with a platform centered on traditional conservative economic pillars, while former Republican-turned-Democrat David Jolly emerged as a centrist alternative. As Florida faces unique pressures—most notably a volatile property insurance market and rising costs of essential services—the upcoming general election is effectively a referendum on how the state should manage its economic resources over the next four years.</p>
