  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The Door Closes on LIV Returns</h2>
    <p>Professional golf's turbulent civil war just reached another definitive milestone. Speaking to reporters on Tuesday, PGA Tour commissioner Brian Rolapp delivered a blunt assessment regarding players who left the established circuit for the upstart, Saudi-backed LIV Golf league: there are currently no plans to offer another "Returning Member Program" allowing defectors a pathway back to the PGA Tour.</p>
    
    <p>"No, currently there's no current plans for [the] Returning Member Program like you saw earlier this year," Rolapp stated clearly. This announcement slams the door shut for any stars hoping to re-enter the PGA ecosystem ahead of the 2027 season, leaving the professional golf landscape fractured as LIV grapples with its own funding uncertainties and recent schedule disruptions.</p>
  </section>
