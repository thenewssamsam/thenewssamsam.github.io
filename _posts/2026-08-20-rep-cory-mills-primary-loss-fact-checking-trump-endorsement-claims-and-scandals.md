<h2>What Happened in Florida&apos;s 7th District?</h2>
<p>When incumbent politicians face intense public scrutiny, the narrative surrounding their re-election bids often splits into two entirely different realities. That dynamic played out in stark relief during Tuesday night&apos;s primary election in Florida&apos;s 7th Congressional District, where Republican Rep. Cory Mills lost his bid for a third term.</p>

<p>Mills was unseated by Ryan Elijah, a former Orlando-area television news anchor and NFL sideline reporter. The upset came despite Mills holding a commanding lead in early polling and securing what is widely considered the most potent symbol of political gravity in modern GOP primaries: an endorsement from former President Donald Trump.</p>
