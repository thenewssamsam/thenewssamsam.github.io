  <section id="financial-impact">
    <h2>The Economic Ripple Effect of Campus Scandals</h2>
    <p>When a fraternity faces severe charges, the financial fallout extends far beyond individual legal fees. Universities often see a sharp increase in insurance premiums as liability risk assessments rise. For the institution, the cost of increased monitoring, legal counsel, and potential settlements can reach millions of dollars.</p>

      <p><strong>Did you know?</strong> Universities often maintain "umbrella" liability policies that are directly affected by the frequency of campus incidents, meaning one organization's misconduct can theoretically impact the insurance overhead for the entire student body.</p>
