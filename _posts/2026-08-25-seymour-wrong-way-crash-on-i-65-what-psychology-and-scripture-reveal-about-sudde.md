      What Happened on I-65 Near Seymour
    </h2>
    <p>
      According to reports from the Indiana State Police, a horrifying sequence of events unfolded around 2:30 a.m. on a Sunday morning along I-65 south in Jackson County, just a mile outside of Seymour, Indiana. 
    </p>
    <p>
      Authorities stated that 51-year-old Theosco Middlebrooks of Louisville, Kentucky, was driving northbound while traveling in the southbound lanes of the interstate. While driving the wrong way, Middlebrooks' vehicle collided head-on with an SUV driven by 31-year-old Alyssa Osborn of Scottsburg, Indiana. Both drivers sustained fatal injuries and were pronounced dead at the scene.
    </p>
    <p>
      Tragically, Osborn's one-year-old son, Waylon—who was securely restrained in the backseat—suffered critical injuries. He was rushed to a children's hospital for emergency medical intervention, but despite the efforts of medical professionals, he later succumbed to his injuries. Initial statements from the Indiana State Police indicate that alcohol consumption by Middlebrooks likely contributed to the crash, with a toxicology report pending.
