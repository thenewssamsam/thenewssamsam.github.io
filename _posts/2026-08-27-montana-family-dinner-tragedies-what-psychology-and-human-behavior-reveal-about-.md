    </h2>
    <p>
      According to reports from The Associated Press, law enforcement responded on Sunday evening to a residential property outside Billings, Montana, after receiving reports of gunfire. As officers arrived, they heard shots from the back of the property just moments before the house was entirely engulfed in flames. 
    </p>
    <p>
      Inside the home, members of a blended, four-generation family had gathered for a routine Sunday dinner. Investigators later confirmed eight victims lost their lives, ranging from a great-grandmother in her nineties using a wheelchair to a seven-year-old girl. The suspect, Alan Smith, who had reportedly been asked by his parents to move out just weeks prior, suffered a self-inflicted gunshot wound and died at the scene. Among the victims were Smith's elderly mother, grandmother, sister, parents, and four children, including 12-year-old Charlotte Ghekiere, who bravely dialed 911 in an attempt to save her family.
