  <h2 id="the-financial-impact">The Financial Mechanics: Turning Compute Into an Investable Asset</h2>
  <p>
    From a financial impact perspective, Jensen Huang's recent moves represent a masterclass in market creation. Historically, hardware manufacturers rely on customers having existing capital expenditure (CapEx) budgets to purchase equipment. When those budgets get tight, hardware sales stall. 
  </p>
  <p>
    By establishing memoranda of understanding (MOUs) with private equity and banking titans, Nvidia is effectively bypassing traditional corporate budget constraints. As Huang succinctly put it: <em>"In AI, compute is revenue. Nvidia compute is uniquely suited for this role."</em>
  </p>
  <p>
    By transforming raw computing power into a marketable, investable asset class—often referred to as "AI factories"—Nvidia is actively engineering the ecosystem required to absorb its own supply. Financial firms like BlackRock and KKR see predictable long-term yield in digital infrastructure, while tech companies gain structured financing options to acquire high-end GPUs without instantly crushing their free cash flow.
  </p>
