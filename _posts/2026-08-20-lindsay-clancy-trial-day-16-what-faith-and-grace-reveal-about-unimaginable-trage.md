  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened on Day 16 of the Lindsay Clancy Trial</h2>
    <p>Wednesday's proceedings in the Massachusetts courtroom were unexpectedly cut short following an "unforeseen circumstance" announced by Judge William Sullivan, dismissing the jury for the day after a dramatic morning of testimony. The trial centers on Lindsay Clancy, who has pleaded not guilty to first-degree murder in the January 2023 deaths of her three young children—Cora, Dawson, and Callan—while her defense maintains she was suffering from extreme postpartum psychosis and was overmedicated.</p>
    
    <p>Earlier in the day, forensic psychologist Dr. Paul Zeizel concluded his cross-examination by the prosecution, defending his extensive care and professional evaluation of Clancy. Tense moments arose as prosecutor Shanan Buckingham questioned Dr. Zeizel about his deep empathy and unusual level of personal involvement with the defendant. Meanwhile, the judge ruled that a TikTok user who previously worked at McLean Hospital would not be permitted to testify before the jury, keeping the focus strictly on the medical records and clinical evaluations of the defense.</p>
