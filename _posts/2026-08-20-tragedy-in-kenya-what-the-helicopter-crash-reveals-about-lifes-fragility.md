  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Facts: A Devastating Loss</h2>
    <p>On Wednesday, August 19, 2026, a tragic helicopter crash near Mount Ololokwe in Samburu County, Kenya, claimed the lives of seven individuals. The flight, operated by Lady Lori Helicopters, was transporting guests from the &Beyond luxury tour agency when it went down in difficult, mountainous terrain. Among the victims were five American citizens, as well as Michele Sensi-Contugi, the director general of Ecuador’s intelligence agency, and his wife, fashion designer Stephany Sensi. The cause of the crash remains under investigation by the Kenyan Transport Ministry.</p>
  </section>
