    <h2 style="font-size: 1.6rem; color: #1a202c; border-bottom: 1px solid #edf2f7; padding-bottom: 8px; margin-top: 0;">
      What Happened: The Curtailment of Ulchi Freedom Shield
    </h2>
    <p>
      The joint military exercises between the United States and South Korea, known as the annual Ulchi Freedom Shield (UFS), were abruptly cut in half following a direct directive from Washington. Originally scheduled to run for 10 grueling days, the exercises were curtailed to conclude early after President Donald Trump called for their reduction. Citing a personal relationship with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un, the administration argued that large-scale field exercises send an inappropriate and hostile signal to Pyongyang.
    </p>
    <p>
      According to statements released by the Pentagon and military officials from both nations, the Department of Defense substantially reduced the operational scope of the exercises, converting several live-training events into simulations while maintaining core tactical capabilities. While South Korean President Lee Jae Myung expressed understanding and support for creating diplomatic conditions on the Korean Peninsula, the decision instantly renewed discussions concerning the legal bounds of international military cooperation, alliance structures, and executive decision-making.
    </p>
  </section>
