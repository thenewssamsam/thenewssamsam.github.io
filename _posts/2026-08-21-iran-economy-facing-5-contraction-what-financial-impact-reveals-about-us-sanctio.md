  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: Operation Economic Fury and the UAE Trade Severance</h2>
    <p>Following the collapse of critical ceasefire talks, the U.S. administration announced a massive escalation of its pressure campaign, dubbed "Operation Economic Fury." The strategy aims to cut off all remaining global revenue streams, oil smuggling routes, currency swap lines, and front companies linked to Tehran.</p>

    <p>Yet, the immediate pressure was turbocharged by an unexpected regional actor. The United Arab Emirates—historically Iran's largest source of imports and its primary financial conduit—announced it was halting all trade and financial ties with Tehran. This bold move came in the wake of alleged ballistic missile firings toward the Gulf state, closing off the vital economic oxygen pipeline that Iranian businesses relied upon to stay afloat.</p>

    <h2>Closing Takeaway</h2>
    <p>The situation in Iran demonstrates that modern economic warfare is a blunt and unpredictable instrument. True economic stability remains one of the most fragile and precious foundations of human well-being.</p>
