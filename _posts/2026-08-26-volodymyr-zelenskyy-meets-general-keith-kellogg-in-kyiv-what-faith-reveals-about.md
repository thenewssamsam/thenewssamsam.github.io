    <h2 id="what-happened" class="ncg-section-h2">What Happened in Kyiv?</h2>
    <p class="ncg-p">On Saturday, August 22, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy met with US General Keith Kellogg in Kyiv. The encounter followed General Kellogg's initial stop in Odesa, marking a deliberate journey that brought the former US Special Representative for Ukraine directly into communities bearing the heavy burdens of the conflict. Kellogg traveled to the capital to participate in the Veterans Forum, an event honoring those who have served on the front lines.</p>

President Zelenskyy highlighted that while Ukraine continues collaborating with European partners to develop broader anti-ballistic initiatives, the immediate procurement of Patriot interceptors remains irreplaceable for enduring the colder months.</p>
