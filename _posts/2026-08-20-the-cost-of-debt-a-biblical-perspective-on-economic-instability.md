    <section id="introduction">
        <p>In the quiet corners of the financial world, a shift is occurring that threatens the stability of our daily lives. As the cost of borrowing—the "cost of money"—surges, we are witnessing a tightening grip on household budgets, small businesses, and the national economy. With 30-year Treasury bond rates climbing above 5.3 percent, it is time to look past the complex jargon of Wall Street and consider what this means for our families and our future.</p>
    </section>

    <section id="what-happened">
        <h2>The Mechanics of Rising Rates</h2>
        <p>The government is currently paying more to finance the public debt than at any point since 2007. This rise is driven by a combination of factors: ballooning federal deficits, the massive borrowing appetite of the AI industry, and the inflationary pressures resulting from global geopolitical conflict. When the government and large tech entities compete for capital, they "crowd out" other borrowers, effectively raising interest costs for everyone.</p>

    <section id="scripture-reflection">
        <h2>A Call to Stewardship</h2>
        <p>Reflecting on our current economic landscape, I am reminded of the parable of the talents. We are called to be faithful stewards of the resources entrusted to us, whether they are many or few. When we see a system built on the assumption of endless expansion through debt, we are challenged to ask: Are we building our lives on sand or rock?</p>

    <section id="practical-response">
        <h2>How Should Christians Respond?</h2>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Prioritize Debt Reduction:</strong> If you carry high-interest debt, make a strategic plan to pay it down. Reducing your exposure to lenders increases your personal freedom.</li>
            <li><strong>Practice Contentment:</strong> With inflation impacting the price of goods, now is the time to evaluate needs versus wants. Cultivating a grateful heart protects us from the fear-driven spending that often characterizes volatile markets.</li>
            <li><strong>Seek Wise Counsel:</strong> Financial decisions should be made with prayer and, when necessary, the advice of those who prioritize long-term stability over short-term gains.</li>
        </ul>
    </section>

        <p>Lord, we lift up our nation and our families to You in this season of economic uncertainty. We ask for wisdom for our leaders and for ourselves, that we might be good stewards of the resources You have provided. Help us to find our security not in the stability of markets, but in Your unchanging character. When the world is shaken, let our hearts be anchored in the peace that passes all understanding. Grant us the discipline to live wisely and the grace to care for those who are struggling under the weight of these economic burdens. In Jesus' name, Amen.</p>
