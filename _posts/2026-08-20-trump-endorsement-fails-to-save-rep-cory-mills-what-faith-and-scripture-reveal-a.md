    <h2>What Happened inelsk the Florida Primary?</h2>
    <p>Tuesday night marked a pivotal turning point in Florida politics. Rep. Cory Mills, who entered primary night with a comfortable lead in multiple local polls and the backing of the former president, was unseated by challenger Ryan Elijah, a former Orlando-area news anchor and NFL sideline reporter. Elijah, who resides in Seminole County with his family, capitalized on voter weariness surrounding ongoing investigations into the incumbent.</p>
