<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened on Day 15 of the Lindsay Clancy Trial?</h2>

<p>According to reports from NBC Boston, Day 15 of the high-profile trial brought jurors back to the harrowing events of January 24, 2023. Prosecutors maintain that Lindsay Clancy strangled her three young children—Cora, 5, Dawson, 3, and Callan, 8 months—in their Duxbury home before jumping from a second-story window, an act that left her paralyzed. The defense argues that Lindsay was in the absolute grip of severe bipolar disorder and postpartum psychosis, rendering her legally not criminally responsible for her actions.</p>
