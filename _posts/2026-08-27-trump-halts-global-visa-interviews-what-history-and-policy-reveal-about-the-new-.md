<section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The Global Visa Freeze</h2>
    <p>In a sweeping administrative move that immediately reverberated across international borders, the Trump administration ordered a complete halt to all U.S. visa applications worldwide and canceled scheduled interviews at embassies and consulates. According to reporting by Forbes, the sudden freeze is designed to facilitate an intensive "global training initiative" for consular employees. The stated goal is to ensure that officials rigorously evaluate applicants to screen out individuals who might eventually become a financial burden on government assistance programs.</p>
    
    <p>This development follows a recent federal court ruling against an earlier attempt by the administration to suspend immigration applications from 75 predominantly poorer nations. Alongside the interview pause, the administration has also advanced plans to revoke up to 200,000 visas held by asylum seekers who originally entered the country for business or tourism, marking what could be the largest mass pulling of visas in U.S. history.</p>
</section>
