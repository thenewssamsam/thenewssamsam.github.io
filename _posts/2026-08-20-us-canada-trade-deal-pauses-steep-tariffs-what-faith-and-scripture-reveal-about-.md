    <section id="introduction" style="margin-bottom: 35px;">
        <p>In the relentless world of international trade, brinkmanship has become the default operating system. Just hours before a sweeping 50 percent duty was set to slam into billions of dollars worth of Canadian exports, a last-minute diplomatic breakthrough altered the course of history. United States President Donald Trump and Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney pulled their nations back from the brink, agreeing to a temporary three-day pause while trade teams finalize documents.</p>
        <p>For ordinary citizens on both sides of the border, the immediate economic relief is palpable. Yet beneath the ticker tapes, policy memos, and strategic chess moves involving the resurrected Keystone XL Pipeline lies a timeless human drama. When nations, leaders, and neighbors lock horns over pride, sovereignty, and economic survival, the underlying tensions reveal much about the fragile nature of human cooperation—and why ancient wisdom places such a high premium on humility and peacemaking.</p>
    </section>
