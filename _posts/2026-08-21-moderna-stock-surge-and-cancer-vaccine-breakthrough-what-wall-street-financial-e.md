<section>
    <p>Financial markets experienced one of the most remarkable healthcare valuation swings of recent years when <strong>Moderna stock</strong> skyrocketed 177% in a single session, only to pull back roughly 20% in early trading the following day. The massive catalyst behind this volatility was a triumphant late-stage Phase 3 clinical trial announcement for a personalized mRNA cancer vaccine called intismeran, developed jointly with pharmaceutical giant Merck.</p>
    
    <p>For investors watching biotech equities, the event marks a watershed moment. Beyond infectious disease vaccines, the clinical success signals a major commercial pivot. But as the initial euphoria settles and profit-taking takes hold, market participants are asking a critical financial question: What are the actual economic mechanics, valuation adjustments, and long-term earnings catalysts that Wall Street analysts foresee next?</p>
