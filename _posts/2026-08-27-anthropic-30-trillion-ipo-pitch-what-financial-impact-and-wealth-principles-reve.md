<h2>What Happened: The $30 Trillion Question</h2>
<p>The public markets are experiencing one of their most electrifying stretches in recent memory. Following high-profile debuts from heavyweights like SpaceX, Cerebras, and Jersey Mike's earlier this year, the IPO pipeline is heating up for what could be a historic fall season. But no prospective listing is turning heads quite like artificial intelligence pioneer Anthropic.</p>

<p>According to a recent report from the <em>Wall Street Journal</em>, the creator of the Claude AI assistant has confidentially filed for a potential public offering as soon as October. Even more eye-catching than the timeline is the pitch itself: Anthropic may present investors with potential revenue opportunities scaling upward of $30 trillion. Hot on its heels, competitor OpenAI is widely expected to follow suit with equally audacious future revenue projections, fresh off a summer executive shuffle.</p>
