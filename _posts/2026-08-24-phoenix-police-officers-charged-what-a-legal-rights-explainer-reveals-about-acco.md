<p>From a legal standpoint, this case touches upon some of the most fundamental protections guaranteed under the United States Constitution. Every citizen possesses specific rights when interacting with law enforcement during a traffic stop, and understanding these boundaries is crucial for personal safety and accountability.</p>

<p>First, under the Fourth Amendment, police officers must have reasonable suspicion of a traffic violation or criminal activity to legally pull over a vehicle. Punching restrained individuals, deploying stun guns without legal justification, and making death threats far exceed any lawful scope of police power.</p>
