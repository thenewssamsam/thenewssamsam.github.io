    <h2>What Happened in the 2026 Primaries?</h2>
    <p>In recent primary contests, several high-profile Democrats closely linked to past presidential impeachments and anti-Trump investigations suffered stunning defeats. Retired U.S. Army Lt. Col. Alexander Vindman—who raised more than $16 million leveraging his prominent role in Trump's first impeachment—lost his Florida Senate primary to DSA member and state Representative Angie Nixon. Similar upsets struck veteran lawmakers and figures like Representative Diana DeGette in Colorado, Representative Shri Thanedar in Michigan, and Representative Dan Goldman in New York.</p>
