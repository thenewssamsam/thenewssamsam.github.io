<section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened at the 2026 NYS Fair?</h2>
    <p>On Wednesday, syracuse.com reporters and editors descended upon the Geddes fairgrounds for the opening of the 2026 New York State Fair. Their mission was clear: sample dozens of items across the expansive fairgrounds to compile the ultimate super guide to food, drinks, and midway thrills. The findings were as eclectic as they were indulgent.</p>
    
    <p>Among the standout discoveries were budget-friendly $4 sugar waffles, gooey NY Skillet mac & cheese loaded with fresh broccoli, and inventive creations like the caramel apple potato—which swaps traditional savory toppings for Beak & Skiff apple chutney, whipped cream, and caramel. Drink enthusiasts explored Brooklyn Lavender Collins cocktails, while thrill-seekers tested the legendary Superman ride on the Midway. Yet, for all the excitement, the sheer volume of high-calorie, highly processed treats highlights a familiar seasonal dilemma: managing our health when surrounded by irresistible temptations.</p>
