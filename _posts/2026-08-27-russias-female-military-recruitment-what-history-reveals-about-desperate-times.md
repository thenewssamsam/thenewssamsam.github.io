<h2>What Happened: The Kremlin's Shift Toward Female Recruitment</h2>
<p>Vladimir Putin's administration has intensified efforts to recruit women into military contracts across Russian regions following a critical rule change in the spring. Driven by mounting personnel deficits and an aggressive Ukrainian summer drone offensive that has severely strained the nation's infrastructure and e-commerce giants, the Kremlin's recruitment apparatus is reaching deeper into society.</p>

<p>According to reports from Polish investigative publication <em>Vot Tak</em> and independent Russian media outlet <em>Mediazona</em>, confirmed Russian military deaths have reached nearly 240,000 since February 2022, with actual totals believed to be much higher. To replenish these staggering losses, regional authorities have deployed aggressive outreach strategies. From unsolicited phone calls to female citizens shortly after registering children for kindergarten, to closed recruitment meetings at technical colleges in cities like St. Petersburg, Samara, and Krasnoyarsk, the net is being cast far wider than before.</p>
