    <p>In Florida, Democratic socialist Angie Nixon secured a victory in the Senate primary over Alex Vindman, setting the stage for a general election clash against GOP Sen. Ashley Moody. Cory Mills in his GOP primary bid, and in Wyoming, state Sen. Eric Barlow defeated the candidate preferred by former President Donald Trump.</p>
