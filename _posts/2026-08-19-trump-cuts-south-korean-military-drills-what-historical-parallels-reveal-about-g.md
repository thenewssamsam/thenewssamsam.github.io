<h2>What Happened</h2>
<p>South Korean President Lee Jae Myung announced that the nation must brace for a "worst-case scenario" after U.S. President Donald Trump ordered Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to substantially reduce joint military exercises. The directive regarding the ongoing Ulchi Freedom Shield drills arrived via a Truth Social post, with Trump citing his amicable relationship with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un while criticizing Seoul for its lack of support in a U.S. military operation against Iran.</p>
<p>The announcement immediately ignited security concerns across the Korean Peninsula. While about 18,000 South Korean military personnel continue their defensive maneuvers through August 27, Pyongyang's foreign ministry quickly labeled the exercises an aggressive rehearsal for war. Meanwhile, analysts like Victor Cha suggest that the timing is a calculated diplomatic maneuver designed to entice Kim Jong Un back to the negotiating table while shifting focus away from Middle Eastern entanglements.</p>
