<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened: The Shift to Retail Infrastructure</h2>
<p>According to reports from NBC News, Ukrainian drone operations have officially broadened their scope, striking deep into the supply chains and fulfillment centers of Russia's booming online retail sector. This weekend's strike on a second major e-commerce titan signals a deliberate strategy: bypassing heavily fortified frontlines to inflict economic chaos and domestic pressure right in the heart of Russian consumer life.</p>

<p>For months, the conflict has been defined by artillery duels, trench warfare, and strikes on traditional energy infrastructure like refineries and power grids. By pivoting toward consumer retail and logistics networks, the strategy aims to fracture the illusion of normalcy for ordinary Russian citizens who might otherwise feel insulated from the war raging miles away.</p>
