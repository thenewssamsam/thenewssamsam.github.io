<h2>The Normalcy Bias and Our Invisible Infrastructure</h2>
<p>When we slide behind the wheel and merge onto a major interstate like I-65, our brains operate on what psychologists call the <strong>normalcy bias</strong>. This cognitive bias causes people to disbelieve or minimize threat warnings. We expect yesterday's conditions to replicate themselves today. The bridge has always stood; therefore, the bridge will stand today.</p>
