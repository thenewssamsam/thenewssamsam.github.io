  <h2 id="what-happened" style="color: #1a1a1a; margin-top: 35px; border-bottom: 1px solid #eee; padding-bottom: 8px;">What Happened: A Massive Overnight Aerial Assault</h2>
  
  <p>
    According to reports from the Air Force of the Armed Forces of Ukraine via Ukrinform, Russian forces launched a massive coordinated aerial assault starting at 18:00 on August 25. The attack involved a staggering 162 Shahed-type attack UAVs, including advanced jet-powered variants, alongside Gerbera drones and Parody-type decoy drones designed to overwhelm defensive radar networks.
  </p>

  <p>
    The projectiles were launched from multiple staging areas, including Kursk, Orel, and Primorsko-Akhtarsk in Russia, as well as temporarily occupied territories in Donetsk and Hvardiiske in Crimea. In response, a complex web of Ukrainian aviation, anti-aircraft missile forces, electronic warfare units, unmanned systems units, and mobile fire groups mobilized to repel the barrage.
  </p>

  <p>
    By 08:00 on Wednesday, August 26, preliminary data indicated that Ukrainian air defenses had successfully shot down or suppressed 134 drones across the northern, southern, and eastern regions of the country. However, hits were recorded at 18 distinct locations, and falling debris created hazards in three additional areas. Tragically, the human toll was not zero: a Russian drone strike in Chernihiv claimed the lives of a woman and a four-year-old child, while leaving a 14-year-old injured.
  </p>
