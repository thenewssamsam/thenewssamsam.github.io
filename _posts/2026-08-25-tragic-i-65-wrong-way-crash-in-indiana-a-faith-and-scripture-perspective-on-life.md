    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; color: #2d3748; margin-bottom: 15px; margin-top: 0;">What Happened on I-65: The Reported Facts</h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      According to the Indiana State Police, the fatal incident occurred just after 2:30 a.m. on Sunday on southbound Interstate 65 near the 48-mile marker, approximately one mile south of Seymour, Indiana in Jackson County. 
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      Investigators report that a Jeep, driven by 51-year-old Theosco Middlebrooks of Louisville, was traveling northbound in the southbound lanes for reasons still under official investigation. The vehicle collided head-on with an SUV driven by 31-year-old Alyssa Osborn of Scottsburg. 
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      Both drivers died at the scene. Tragically, Alyssa Osborn's one-year-old son, Waylon Osborn, who was properly secured in a rear car seat, sustained critical injuries and later passed away at Norton Children's Hospital. Preliminary findings from state police indicate that alcohol use likely played a contributing role in the tragedy, though investigations remain ongoing.
