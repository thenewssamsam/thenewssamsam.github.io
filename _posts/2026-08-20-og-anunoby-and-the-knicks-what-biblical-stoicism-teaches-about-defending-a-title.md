<h2>What Happened: The Summer After the Shot</h2>
<p>For the New York Knicks, the summer of 2026 has been an unending blur of talk shows, parades, and hardware. After snapping a grueling 53-year championship drought with an improbable postseason comeback &mdash; capped off by forward OG Anunoby's iconic, buzzer-beating tip-in during the NBA Finals &mdash; the franchise has found itself thrust into an unfamiliar realm: the spotlight of reigning champions.</p>

<p>Yet, while fans and media outlets continue to dissect the dramatic series against the San Antonio Spurs, Anunoby himself has struck a notably different chord. Sitting in a black hoodie during a promotional interview circuit, the 10-year veteran admitted that while he appreciates the memories, he is already "over" his legendary moment. His eyes are firmly fixed on the horizon, ready for the grueling task of training camp and the defense of the crown.</p>

<p>This unemotional, almost monk-like detachment from his own fame has become a signature trait for Anunoby and a defining characteristic of a veteran Knicks roster that refuses to get intoxicated by its own success.</p>
