<section id="the-news">
<p>The recent announcement by Pennsylvania Attorney General Dave Sunday regarding an alleged cocaine-trafficking operation at Penn State University has sent shockwaves through the academic and Greek life communities. With 14 individuals charged—including current and former students—the case highlights a disturbing intersection of illicit activity and organizational hierarchy within two specific fraternities, Delta Upsilon and Sigma Chi.</p>

<section id="what-happened">
<h2>The Allegations: A Coordinated Operation</h2>
<p>Authorities describe a highly profitable, structured operation led by senior fraternity members. According to the investigation, the illicit enterprise spanned from 2023 to 2024, involving regular supply runs to Philadelphia and New York. Perhaps most concerning is the allegation that the packaging and distribution of narcotics were integrated into the "indoctrination" process for pledges, effectively grooming new members into the criminal enterprise.</p>
