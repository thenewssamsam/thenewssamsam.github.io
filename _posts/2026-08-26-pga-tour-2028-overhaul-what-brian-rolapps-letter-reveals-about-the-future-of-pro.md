    <h2>What Happened: The 2028 Vision</h2>
    <p>Professional golf is bracing for a seismic shift. In an open letter to fans released ahead of the TOUR Championship, PGA Tour CEO Brian Rolapp outlined sweeping changes to the tour’s competitive model, culminating in a brand-new season-ending structure set to debut in 2028. According to Rolapp, the current postseason format has failed to deliver a conclusion worthy of the grueling season required to reach it. Consequently, the organization is stepping away from the traditional model to institute a two-week, high-stakes match-play championship hosted across rotating elite venues.</p>

    <p>Why do major sports leagues feel such intense pressure to completely redesign their formats every few years? When a system relies heavily on convoluted starting-stroke advantages or mathematical handicaps, viewers experience cognitive load. Instead of simply watching the best athletes battle head-to-head for glory, fans have to mentally calculate how many shots back a player starts. Over time, that friction breeds detachment.</p>
