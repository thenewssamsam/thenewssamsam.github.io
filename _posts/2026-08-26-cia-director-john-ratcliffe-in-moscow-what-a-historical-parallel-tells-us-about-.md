However, reports confirmed that ahead of Ratcliffe's arrival, the U.S. informed Ukraine of the impending senior delegation visit, requesting a temporary suspension of strikes until the delegation departed.</p>
<p>This journey marks Ratcliffe's first known trip to Russia as CIA director, though he has maintained communication with Russian intelligence counterparts. The visit unfolds against a tense geopolitical backdrop.
