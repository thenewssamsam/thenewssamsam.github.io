<h2>A Financial Perspective on Inflation and Volatility</h2>
<p>When major indexes like the Dow slip due to sticky inflation, it triggers a cascade of psychological and financial reactions. From a financial impact perspective, stubborn inflation acts as a silent tax on everyday consumers and corporate margins alike. When the cost of goods remains elevated, central banks face restricted room to lower interest rates, keeping borrowing costs high for businesses and households.</p>
