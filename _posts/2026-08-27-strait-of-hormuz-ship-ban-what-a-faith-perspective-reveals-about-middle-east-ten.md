    <h2>What Happened in the Strait of Hormuz</h2>
    <p>Nearly six months into the ongoing conflict involving Iran, regional waterways remain heavily disrupted, carrying profound consequences for global commerce and international relations. Recently, Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi announced that Iran and Oman are discussing a potential temporary agreement to manage commercial shipping traffic through the vital Strait of Hormuz. Under the evolving framework, commercial vessels would transit specific designated corridors passing partly through Iranian and Omani territorial waters.</p>
