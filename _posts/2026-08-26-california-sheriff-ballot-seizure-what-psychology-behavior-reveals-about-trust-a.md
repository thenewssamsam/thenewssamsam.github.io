<h2>What Happened in the California Ballot Dispute?</h2>

However, following a complaint, Riverside County Sheriff Chad Bianco initiated an investigation and seized approximately 650,000 ballots.</p>

<p>Most citizens operate under a psychological framework known as normalcy bias—the default assumption that foundational systems, such as election tabulation machines and state administrative processes, function correctly because they have always done so. When an authority figure like Sheriff Bianco publicly challenges that normalcy by claiming machine counts diverged from physical evidence, it creates massive cognitive dissonance for the public.</p>
