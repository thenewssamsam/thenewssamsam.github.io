<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened</h2>
<p>The House Ethics Committee officially announced Monday that it is investigating Rep. Jimmy Gomez, a five-term congressman representing California&#39;s 34th District, over allegations of inappropriate sexual contact with a House staffer. The inquiry follows initial reporting by <em>The New York Post</em> in April regarding a 2023 incident, alongside subsequent information uncovered by committee investigators.</p>
<p>In response to the probe, Gomez acknowledged making &quot;personal mistakes outside my marriage&quot; while maintaining that his actions were consensual and did not violate House rules or the law. He stated his intention to cooperate fully with the committee. The bipartisan panel&#39;s announcement emphasized that opening an investigation does not automatically indicate a rule violation has occurred, but it sets the stage for a formal, impartial review of the facts.</p>
