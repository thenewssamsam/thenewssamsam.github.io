    <p>On Tuesday night, political analysts witnessed a major primary upset when Republican challenger Ryan Elijah defeated incumbent Rep. Cory Mills. Mills, serving his second term representing Florida's 7th Congressional District—which includes Seminole County and parts of Orange County—faced a wave of political headwinds stemming from a House Ethics Committee investigation and unconfirmed allegations of a federal probe. Mills consistently denied the allegations, attributing them to political maneuvering by primary opponents, and frequently pointed to his military background and Donald Trump's endorsement as he campaigned.</p>
    
    <p>However, the local backlash proved insurmountable. High-profile Republicans, including Rep.
