<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Florida Primary?</h2>
<p>The primary election results on Tuesday night caught political observers off guard. Alexander Vindman, a retired army lieutenant colonel and national security whistleblower who gained national prominence during Donald Trump's first impeachment, entered the race with a staggering  million war chest. Yet, it was Nixon—a Jacksonville-born state representative and member of the Democratic Socialists of America—who captured the nomination.</p>

<p>Vindman focused much of his campaign on the ultimate general election opponent, Republican Ashley Moody, and notably declined requests to debate Nixon. Nixon, meanwhile, ran a grassroots campaign highlighting her nearly two-decade history of fighting legislative policies alongside local residents. Following the race call, Vindman conceded gracefully, praising Nixon's campaign and pledging his support for the general election fight against Moody.</p>
