<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened at Boston Scientific?</h2>
<p>In the fast-moving world of medical technology, a reliable supply chain is quite literally a matter of life and health. On Wednesday, major medical device maker <strong>Boston Scientific</strong> disclosed in an SEC filing that it had been hit by a severe cyberattack. The security breach has severely disrupted access to critical company information systems and applications—most notably halting vital product shipment services.</p>

<p>For a $72 billion healthcare titan responsible for manufacturing life-saving cardiac devices like pacemakers and stents, any operational downtime is deeply unsettling. The disclosure immediately rattled investors, sending shares down roughly 4.5% in morning trading. This fresh blow compounds an already bruising year for the stock, which has faced mounting pressure following a soft profit forecast earlier in the first quarter.</p>
