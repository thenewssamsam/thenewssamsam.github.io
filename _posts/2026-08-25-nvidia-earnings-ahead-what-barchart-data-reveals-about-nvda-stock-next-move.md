    <h3>Understanding Market Volatility and Options Flow</h3>
    <p>Barchart metrics frequently highlight implied volatility (IV) spikes ahead of major earnings announcements. When IV rises, options premiums become more expensive, signaling that the options market is pricing in a massive move in either direction. For retail investors, this environment requires a disciplined understanding of risk. Chasing high-premium options without a clear risk management strategy often leads to capital erosion.</p>
