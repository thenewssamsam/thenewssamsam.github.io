  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened: The Ozon Warehouse Attacks</h2>
    <p>The conflict between Russia and Ukraine has officially entered a new commercial battleground. According to reports from CNBC and international think tanks, Ukraine’s ongoing drone campaign has shifted its primary focus from Wildberries—Russia's leading Amazon-style online retailer—to Ozon, the nation's second-largest e-commerce marketplace.</p>
    <p>Over a span of just three days, at least six logistics facilities belonging to Ozon came under drone attack. Incidents were documented at major hubs in Makhachkala in Dagestan and the town of Enem near Krasnodar. Meanwhile, a major warehouse in the Samara region suffered a severe fire after a drone strike on Saturday, forcing the rapid evacuation of more than 500 employees.</p>
    <p>The market reaction was swift and unforgiving. Shares of Ozon plunged nearly 30% on the Moscow Exchange on Monday. The Institute for the Study of War confirmed that Ukrainian forces have intensified their systematic targeting of storage centers holding dual-use goods, effectively pivoting away from the 15 Wildberries hubs struck since mid-July.</p>
