<p>According to reports from the Daily Memphian, Memphis Light, Gas and Water (MLGW) has deployed a staggering 600 employees and contractors to tackle widespread power outages following damaging storms. Utility officials have worked around the clock to repair downed lines, clear debris, and restore electrical service to affected neighborhoods.</p>

<p>Despite the massive deployment of personnel, MLGW officials have issued realistic warnings to the public: full restoration may take until Thursday for the hardest-hit areas. In situations like this, patience wears thin as refrigerators warm up and daily routines are completely upended.</p>

Every repaired transformer and lifted tree branch is an act of restoring order to a chaotic world.</p>
