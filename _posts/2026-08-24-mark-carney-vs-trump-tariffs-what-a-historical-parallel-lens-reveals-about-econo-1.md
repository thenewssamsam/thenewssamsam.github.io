<h2>What Happened: The Breakdown in US-Canada Talks</h2>
<p>The confrontation escalated late Friday when Prime Minister Mark Carney concluded that Washington's final-hour demands crossed a red line. According to Ottawa, the United States introduced language that would have severely restricted Canada's autonomy to forge independent trade agreements with other nations. Calling the demand unacceptable, Carney's government chose to absorb immediate economic pain rather than yield.</p>

<p>In response, the U.S. implemented 50 percent duties on roughly $20 billion in Canadian goods, prompting Carney to announce dollar-for-dollar retaliatory measures beginning September 8. British Columbia Premier David Eby warned that accepting Washington's terms would have reduced Canada to the "economic equivalent of the 51st state"—a concept President Trump has frequently referenced.</p>
