        <p>The Associated Press projected Ryan Elijah as the winner on Tuesday, bringing an end to a tumultuous campaign cycle for Cory Mills. Mills’ tenure had been marked by a series of controversies, ranging from a restraining order issued against him involving a former girlfriend to ongoing investigations by the House Ethics Committee and reports of a Department of Justice inquiry—all of which he has consistently denied.</p>
