    <h2 class="dp-h2" id="the-power-of-looking-forward">The Psychology of Early Anticipation</h2>
    <p class="dp-p">Why do announcements about December festivities bring us a sense of comfort even when we are sweating through the dog days of summer? Psychologists often point to the power of anticipation. Simply knowing that a positive, community-oriented event is on the horizon can act as a mental anchor. It shifts our perspective away from immediate stressors and offers a gentle reminder that seasons change.</p>

    <p class="dp-p">Life can easily feel like an endless treadmill of daily obligations. When venues like the Dr. Phillips Center plant a flag for a holiday festival months in advance, they aren't just selling tickets—they are offering a psychological waypoint. They invite us to lift our eyes from the immediate moment and remember what celebration feels like.</p>
