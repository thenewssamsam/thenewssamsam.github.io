<h2>What Happened in Kenya</h2>
<p>On a Wednesday that began with ordinary routines across Florida and the globe, devastating news broke from northeastern Kenya. José Alberto Suárez, a respected media executive and president and general manager of Telemundo stations 31, 49, and Ft. Myers–Naples, was killed in a helicopter crash in Samburu County.</p>

Over 18 combined years with Telemundo and NBC stations, he led markets from Fresno and Las Vegas to San Antonio, Utah, and Florida.</p>
