  <section id="the-news-context">
    <h2>Understanding the Recent Military Investigations</h2>
    <p>The military's announcement addresses five specific cases out of 150 reviewed incidents. While these investigations mark a shift in policy, they have been met with skepticism from international observers and human rights organizations. The cases include the killing of Hind Rajab, who was trapped in a vehicle while pleading for help, and the deaths of 15 emergency responders whose bodies were later found in a mass grave. Conversely, the military stated it would not pursue criminal charges regarding strikes that killed aid workers from World Central Kitchen and Doctors Without Borders, maintaining that those actions did not reach the threshold of criminal misconduct.</p>
