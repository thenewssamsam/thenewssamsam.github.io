<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened: The Record July Numbers</h2>
<p>According to recently released government data provided by ICE to the Deportation Data Project based at U.C. Berkeley and UCLA, immigration arrests reached 49,571 in July. This figure represents a 15% jump from June's previous high of 43,021 apprehensions, averaging roughly 1,599 arrests per day.</p>
<p>The spike was fueled by significant factors, including billions in recent congressional funding for immigration enforcement and an aggressive hiring surge for ICE officers. Furthermore, the numbers incorporate the initial wave of arrests stemming from the revocation of Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for Haitian immigrants in July.</p>
