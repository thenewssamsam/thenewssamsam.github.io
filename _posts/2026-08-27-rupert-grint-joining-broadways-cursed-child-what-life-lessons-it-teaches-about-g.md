  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: Stepping Back into Hogwarts</h2>
    <p>BBC News reports that actor Rupert Grint will join the cast of <em>Harry Potter and the Cursed Child</em> on Broadway starting next February for a four-month run. Grint, who famously starred in all eight films from 2001 to 2011, will portray an adult Ron Weasley, navigating parenthood 19 years after the original series concluded.</p>

    <p>Grint is the only member of the iconic trio—comprising Harry, Ron, and Hermione—to return to their original cinematic roles on stage, though co-star Tom Felton has been playing Draco Malfoy in the same production. In a recent statement, Grint reflected on the poetic symmetry of the moment: "There is something so full-circle and so special about stepping back into Ron's shoes now when we are both fathers; it is somehow both very familiar and completely new."</p>
    <p>Previously, Grint candidly told the BBC that he accepted he would likely never step out of Ron Weasley's shadow, noting, "I'm fine with that. I think it's great." That radical acceptance forms the core of a fascinating psychological and personal growth story.</p>
