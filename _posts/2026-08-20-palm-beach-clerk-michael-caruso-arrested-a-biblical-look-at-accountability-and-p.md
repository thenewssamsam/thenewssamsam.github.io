<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in Palm Beach County</h2>
<p>According to the arrest affidavit released by state authorities, 67-year-old Michael Caruso—a former Republican legislator who served as Palm Beach County's Clerk of Court and Comptroller—was taken into custody and faces five felony counts. These include kidnapping, lewd or lascivious molestation, lewd or lascivious exhibition, luring or enticing a child, and child abuse causing mental injury. If convicted, Caruso faces a possible life sentence.</p>

<p>The investigation began after a family member observed troubling behavior and the young victim disclosed details of alleged abuse occurring over a span of time, including incidents in Orange County, Palm Beach County, and during a family cruise aboard Royal Caribbean's <em>Icon of the Seas</em> in August 2025. Investigators outlined how Caruso allegedly isolated the child under the pretext of getting ice cream or going fishing.</p>
