<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened at Trump National Golf Club</h2>
<p>On August 2, federal agents and local law enforcement detained an armed man at Trump National Golf Club Los Angeles in Rancho Palos Verdes, California. The suspect, identified as 38-year-old Jeanine John Taele, was found walking the grounds while apparently monitoring security planning ahead of a scheduled presidential visit. According to prosecutors and court documents revealed during a Los Angeles County court hearing, Taele possessed an alarming collection of materials.</p>

<p>Authorities reported finding a 16-round magazine loaded with hollow-point ammunition in his pants pocket. A subsequent search of his vehicle uncovered a loaded pistol with a round in the chamber and additional ammunition. When investigators searched Taele's Downey home, they reportedly recovered an illegally modified AR-platform rifle, a .45-caliber pistol, body armor, high-capacity magazines, bulk ammunition, radio signal devices, and over 200 pages of handwritten notes spread across seven notebooks.</p>
