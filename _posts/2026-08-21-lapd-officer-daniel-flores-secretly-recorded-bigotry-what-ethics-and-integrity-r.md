  <section class="ncg-intro" style="margin-bottom: 24px;">
    <p style="font-size: 1.125rem; font-weight: 500; color: #2d3748; margin-bottom: 16px;">When a law enforcement officer secretly records colleagues swapping racist, sexist, and homophobic slurs, the ensuing fallout touches the deepest nerves of public trust. The legal battle surrounding LAPD officer Daniel Flores has taken a dramatic turn, forcing a public reckoning over accountability, institutional loyalty, and the heavy price paid by those who shine a light into dark places.</p>
    
    <p>In a high-profile announcement, Los Angeles District Attorney Nathan Hochman declared that felony charges against Daniel Flores would be reduced to misdemeanors. The decision marks a pivotal moment in an intricate legal drama that has gripped Southern California, highlighting the precarious line between upholding the law and exposing internal corruption within major institutions.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section" style="margin-bottom: 32px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; color: #2b6cb0; margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; border-left: 4px solid #3182ce; padding-left: 12px;">What Happened in the Daniel Flores Case?</h2>
    
    <p>Daniel Flores, a 43-year-old Los Angeles Police Department officer assigned to the Recruitment and Employment Division, found himself facing 16 felony counts of eavesdropping and recording confidential communications. According to prosecutors, Flores made 122 secret recordings of colleagues in 2024 without their permission. Those recordings allegedly captured fellow officers making explicit racist, sexist, and homophobic comments.</p>
    
    <p>Flores maintained that his actions were part of a lawful investigation into a hostile work environment fostered by a supervisor who took over the division in late 2021. In June, Flores filed a civil lawsuit against the city, detailing deeply offensive remarks directed at women, Asian, Hispanic, and Black individuals, alongside derogatory comments regarding sexual orientation.</p>
