  <section id="what-happened" style="margin-bottom: 32px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; color: #111827; margin-bottom: 16px; border-bottom: 2px solid #e5e7eb; padding-bottom: 8px;">What Happened in the Primaries?</h2>
    <p>
      According to recent reports from NBC News, President Donald Trump's near-sterling endorsement record during the 2026 election cycle has taken a noticeable hit as the primary season nears its final stretch. While Trump-backed candidates still boast a formidable overall record—winning far more primaries than they have lost, sitting at a robust 67-6—the month of August has introduced rare vulnerabilities. 
    </p>
    <p>
      With additional high-stakes primary tests scheduled for the coming week, political analysts are closely watching whether this late-month stumble represents a temporary blip or a shifting tide in voter sentiment. In the high-stakes arena of American politics, endorsements are often treated as political gold, symbolizing kingmaking authority and unmatched sway over the electorate. Yet, as this recent stretch demonstrates, even the most powerful political backing cannot entirely insulate a candidate from the unpredictable currents of local voter preferences.
    </p>
    <p>
      This unfolding story brings into sharp focus the eternal dance between power, influence, and the fickle nature of public approval. When political heavyweights throw their weight behind candidates, expectations soar, making any stumble feel much larger than life. But beneath the campaign ads and vote tallies lies a deeper human reality about how we pursue authority and where we place our trust.
    </p>
