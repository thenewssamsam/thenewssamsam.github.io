<p>According to recent reporting from CNN, Natalie Harp has cemented her status as one of the most trusted and constant figures in Donald Trump's orbit. From riding in a vehicle trunk outside Trump Tower in October 2023 to secure her spot on a courthouse trip, to boarding secret flights and feeding the president printouts of laudatory articles and social media posts (earning her the nickname "human printer"), Harp's total dedication has made her indispensable to the president.</p>

<p>While her defenders praise her relentless work ethic and absolute loyalty, her rapid ascent has reportedly ruffled feathers among senior West Wing staff who sometimes find themselves boxed out of private meetings. This dynamic burst into public view recently when Senator Jon Ossoff referenced her during a speech attacking the administration, sparking a fierce online and political backlash. Yet, history suggests that tension between formal government officials and informal palace favorites is as old as civilization itself.</p>
