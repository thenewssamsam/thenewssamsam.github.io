    <h1>Trump Economic Policy and the 2026 Midterms: What a Financial Lens Reveals About Senate Control</h1>
    <p class="ncg-subtitle">As voters prepare for midterm elections, economic friction points—from shifting tariffs to stubborn inflation—are altering household budgets and threatening the Republican hold on the Senate.</p>
  </header>

  <section class="ncg-section" id="ncg-introduction">
    <p>When voters head to the polls in upcoming midterm elections, economic reality often dictates political fate. According to recent analysis by economist Peter Morici, the current economic landscape under Trump 2.0 is proving bumpier than many anticipated, with growth hovering around 2% annually despite tailwinds from artificial intelligence and oil booms. For ordinary households watching grocery prices climb and auto costs soar, these macro figures translate directly into kitchen-table stress—and that frustration is casting a long shadow over Republican control of the United States Senate.</p>
    
    <p>To understand why political fortunes are shifting, we have to look past the political rhetoric and examine the underlying financial mechanics at play. Tariffs, shifting labor markets, and immigration policy adjustments are creating unexpected friction across key sectors, ultimately reshaping voter sentiment.</p>
  </section>
