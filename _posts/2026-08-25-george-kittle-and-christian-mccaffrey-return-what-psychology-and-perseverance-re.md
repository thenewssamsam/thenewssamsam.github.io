    <h2>What Happened: A Major Boost for the 49ers</h2>
    <p>On Sunday, August 23, 2026, the 49ers announced that George Kittle was officially activated from the Active/Physically Unable to Perform (PUP) list. Suffering a torn Achilles tendon during a playoff game on January 11, Kittle faced a grueling rehabilitation process <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07W4Q4D3R?tag=thenewssam-20" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Resistance Bands Exercise Set - $19.99</a>. Returning to individual work in pads less than eight months later puts him on an accelerated, yet carefully managed, timeline for Week 1.</p>
    <p>Offensive lineman Colton McKivitz spoke to reporters after practice, praising Kittle's work ethic. "It shows what kind of professional he is," McKivitz noted. "For him to have that injury in the playoffs in January, to come back that soon, and be out there in pads today, it's incredible."</p>
    <p>Simultaneously, Christian McCaffrey returned to team drills for the first time since August 8. McCaffrey explained that his time away was entirely intentional as part of a workload management plan designed to keep the star running back fresh for the grueling regular season. Meanwhile, young contributors like rookie running back Jordan James also returned from a rib injury, further bolstering a deep 49ers backfield under running backs coach Bobby Turner.</p>
