<section id="introduction">
<p>The recent announcement by Pennsylvania Attorney General Dave Sunday regarding an alleged cocaine-trafficking operation at Penn State University has sent shockwaves through State College. With 14 individuals charged—most of them students or former students—the incident highlights a dark intersection between campus social life and criminal activity. For the local community, this isn't just a headline; it’s a moment of reckoning for the safety and culture of university-affiliated organizations.</p>

<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened?</h2>
<p>Authorities allege that between 2023 and 2024, a highly organized drug network operated out of two Greek houses: Delta Upsilon and Sigma Chi. Investigators claim that senior fraternity members used these houses not just as social hubs, but as distribution centers for cocaine, even involving pledges in the cutting and packaging process as a form of "indoctrination."</p>
