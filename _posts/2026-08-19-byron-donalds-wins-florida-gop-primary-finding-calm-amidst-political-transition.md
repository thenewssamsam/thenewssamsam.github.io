    <h2>The Results of the Florida GOP Primary</h2>
    <p>Rep. Byron Donalds, a third-term congressman, emerged victorious in the Florida Republican gubernatorial primary this Tuesday. His victory was bolstered by an early endorsement from President Donald Trump, which solidified his position as a front-runner despite a field that included notable figures like Lt. Gov. Jay Collins. Donalds, who has campaigned on an "America First" agenda focusing on infrastructure, deregulation, and affordability, will now face former Republican-turned-Democrat David Jolly in the general election.</p>
