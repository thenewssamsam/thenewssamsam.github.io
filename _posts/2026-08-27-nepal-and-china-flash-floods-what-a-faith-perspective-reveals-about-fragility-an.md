<p class="ncg-lead">Massive flash floods triggered by a glacial avalanche along the border of Nepal and China have left at least 160 people dead and hundreds missing, turning mountain valleys into rivers of mud in a matter of minutes. As international rescue efforts scramble to find survivors among stranded tourists and locals, disasters of this magnitude confront us with a sobering reality: the profound fragility of human life and infrastructure against the raw, unbridled forces of nature.</p>

<div class="ncg-quick-take">
<h3>Quick Take: What You Need to Know</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>The Event:</strong> A suspected ice and rock avalanche from a warming Himalayan glacier triggered sudden, massive flash floods in Nepal and Tibet (China).</li>
  <li><strong>The Toll:</strong> At least 160 people confirmed dead, with hundreds of locals, foreign tourists, and pilgrims reported missing.</li>
  <li><strong>The Cause:</strong> Climate researchers point to accelerating glacial melt and unstable terrain in the warming Hindu Kush Himalaya region.</li>
  <li><strong>The Lens:</strong> Looking at sudden catastrophe through Scripture reminds us of our earthly vulnerability while calling us toward steadfast compassion and stewardship.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Himalayan Borderlands</h2>

<p>The disaster struck without warning on Wednesday morning. According to reports from the Associated Press, an avalanche of ice and rock swept down through steep Himalayan valleys, causing the Trishuli and Lhende Khola rivers to swell drastically in a matter of minutes. Water levels at one monitoring point surged by as much as nine meters (27 feet) in just half an hour.</p>

<p>The wall of muddy water tore through villages, destroying nearly 40 kilometers of roads, 19 bridges, and several hydropower projects. In Nepal's hard-hit Nuwakot and Rasuwa districts, buildings were buried up to their second floors, vehicles were swallowed by silt, and local markets like Dhunge bazaar were obliterated before residents had time to flee for higher ground.</p>
