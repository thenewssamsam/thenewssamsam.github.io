  <section id="introduction">
    <p>The passing of actor Hayden Panettiere at age 36 has prompted a wave of reflection across the country. Known for her roles as a millennial icon in <em>Heroes</em> and <em>Nashville</em>, Panettiere’s life was a testament to the complexities of fame, the weight of public expectation, and the private battles many face behind the spotlight. Her story serves as a poignant reminder of the importance of mental health awareness and the necessity of extending grace to those navigating life’s most difficult chapters.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>Understanding the News</h2>
    <p>Authorities in South Carolina reported that emergency responders were called to the scene of a suspected cardiac arrest on Sunday. While an autopsy confirmed there were no signs of trauma, the exact cause of death remains under investigation. For those who grew up watching her career—which began when she was just 11 months old—the news is a sobering reminder that even the most recognizable faces navigate profound personal hardships.</p>
  </section>
