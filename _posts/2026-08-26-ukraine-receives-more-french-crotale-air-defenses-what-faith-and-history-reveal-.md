  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened: The Crotale Reinforcement</h2>
    <p>The announcement that France and its allies have transferred additional Crotale air defense units marks another chapter in the ongoing technological adaptation of Ukraine's armed forces. Developed originally in the late 1960s and continually upgraded into the Crotale NG (Next Generation) of today, the system provides a specialized response to low-flying threats.</p>
    <p>When the first Crotale units arrived in late 2022, they immediately filled a critical tactical gap. Firing massive, expensive interceptors at low-cost loitering munitions like Iranian-designed Shahed drones was economically unsustainable and practically inefficient. The Crotale, with its rapid reaction time, VT-1 high-speed missiles, and integrated radar and electro-optical sensors, offered a precise solution. With Russia scaling up drone attacks from a handful per day in 2022 to thousands daily in 2026, keeping these mobile systems supplied with ammunition is essential for protecting both infrastructure and civilian lives.</p>
