    <h3>The Toll of Sleep Deprivation on Normalcy Bias</h3>
    <p>Human beings rely heavily on the concept of home as a sanctuary—a place to reset. Persistent nighttime warmth disrupts the biological necessity of restorative sleep. Psychologically, consecutive nights of high indoor temperatures foster a subtle cognitive fatigue. Decision-making slows down, irritability spikes, and what psychologists call <q>normalcy bias</q> can lead individuals to underestimate cumulative physiological stress until it manifests as acute exhaustion or heat illness.</p>
