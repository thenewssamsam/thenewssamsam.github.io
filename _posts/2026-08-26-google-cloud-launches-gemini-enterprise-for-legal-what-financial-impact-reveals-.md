  <section id="what-happened" style="margin-bottom: 40px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; color: #1a202c; border-bottom: 1px solid #e2e8f0; padding-bottom: 8px; margin-bottom: 20px;">What Happened: The Launch of Gemini Enterprise for Legal</h2>
    <p>On August 25, 2026, Google Cloud officially announced the release of <strong>Google Cloud Gemini Enterprise for Legal</strong>, an enterprise-grade, purpose-built agentic AI platform tailored specifically for the complex operational requirements of the legal industry. Debuting in preview with launch customers including prominent legal powerhouses such as Cleary, Freshfields, Weil, and Williams & Connolly, the tool aims to bridge the gap between general-purpose artificial intelligence and the strict security, confidentiality, and verification standards demanded by modern legal practice.</p>
    <p>According to Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian, the platform combines specialized skills for legal professionals, robust data connectors to core industry platforms like iManage, RelativityOne, and Thomson Reuters, and a secure infrastructure where client data stays within an organization's private perimeter. Rather than forcing law firms to stitch together fragmented point solutions—a practice that historically incurred a heavy integration tax and created fragile workflows—Gemini Enterprise for Legal provides an integrated control plane designed to execute complete legal tasks autonomously.</p>
  </section>
