  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The 200,000 Visa Revocation Plan</h2>
    <p>According to recent reports, the Trump administration is laying the groundwork to revoke B-1 (business) and B-2 (tourism) visas issued between 2016 and 2026 for individuals who subsequently sought or are currently seeking asylum in the United States. For millions of travelers, these temporary visas serve as standard entry documents for family visits, tourism, or professional meetings. However, federal officials argue that utilizing a temporary visitor visa as a pathway to request asylum bypasses traditional immigration channels.</p>
    
    <p>While the scope of the proposal reaches nationwide—impacting an estimated 200,000 people—immigration attorneys and local advocates are closely monitoring how federal agencies will roll out the administrative process. Because the scale of such revocations is unprecedented, legal professionals stress that the operational reality will likely unfold gradually rather than overnight.</p>
