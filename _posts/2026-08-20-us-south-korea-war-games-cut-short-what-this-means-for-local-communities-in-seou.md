  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened on the Ground?</h2>
  <p>For months, local preparations for the annual Ulchi Freedom Shield exercise had been moving forward in communities near military installations across South Korea. Roughly 18,000 South Korean soldiers and 28,500 stationed American military personnel prepared for the massive combined field training event. But on Wednesday, the South Korean defense ministry announced an abrupt adjustment: the exercise period and scale would be scaled back, concluding on August 21 instead of the planned August 27.</p>
  <p>According to reports from Seoul, neither South Korean nor US military officials stationed in the region were aware in advance about the directive from Washington. President Trump openly connected the decision to geopolitical disagreements, noting in statements on Truth Social and in the Oval Office that South Korea had declined to assist in operations regarding Iran, despite the heavy US military presence guarding the peninsula.</p>
