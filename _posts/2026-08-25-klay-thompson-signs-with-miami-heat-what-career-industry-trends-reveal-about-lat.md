<h2>What Happened: Klay Thompson Clears Waivers and Joins Miami</h2>
<p>The dust has finally settled on one of the more turbulent chapters of the NBA offseason. After clearing waivers, future Hall of Famer Klay Thompson has officially inked a two-year, $11.5 million contract with the Miami Heat, anchored by a player option for the second year and a 15 percent trade kicker. For a player who entered the league as a foundational pillar of Golden State's dynastic "Splash Brothers" backcourt, the move marks a dramatic new chapter.</p>

<p>Thompson’s exit from the Dallas Mavericks followed a buyout agreement on Friday, terminating a brief and chaotic tenure that began with high championship aspirations alongside Luka Dončić. Instead of contending for titles in Texas, Thompson found himself navigating front office upheaval, a major roster reset, and a transition to a bench role—averaging a career-low 11.7 points per game while shooting 38.3 percent from beyond the arc. Now, heading into his age-36 season, Thompson lands in Miami to bolster a revamped roster featuring Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo.</p>
