<h2>What Happened in San Jose</h2>
<p>According to the San Jose Police Department, the sequence of events began hours before the final gunfire. Police initially responded to a call around 12:30 p.m. involving an armed-robbery suspect spotted in a vehicle. Later in the evening, around 6 p.m., officers located the suspect's SUV and attempted a traffic stop.</p>
<p>The situation escalated immediately. Police state that the suspect brandished a firearm at officers before fleeing. The vehicle was subsequently abandoned near Lick Avenue and Goodyear Avenue. According to SJPD spokesperson Stacie Shih, officers tracked the suspect into a nearby residential home occupied by a family, including at least one juvenile.</p>

When individuals face extreme threat environments—whether fleeing from law enforcement or entering a stranger's home with a firearm—the human brain's survival mechanisms take absolute precedence.</p>
