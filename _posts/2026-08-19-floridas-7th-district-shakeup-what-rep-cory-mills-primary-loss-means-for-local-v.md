    <h2>What Happened in the 7th District?</h2>
    <p>In a significant political shift for Florida, Republican Representative Cory Mills has been defeated in his primary bid. NBC News projects that former television journalist Ryan Elijah has secured the Republican nomination for Florida’s 7th District. Despite receiving an endorsement from President Donald Trump, Mills could not overcome the momentum generated by Elijah’s campaign and the shadow of multiple ongoing investigations.</p>
