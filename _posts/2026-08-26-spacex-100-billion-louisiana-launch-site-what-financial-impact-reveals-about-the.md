  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened: The $100 Billion Bet in Vermilion Parish</h2>
    <p>SpaceX has officially pulled back the curtain on its most ambitious infrastructure undertaking to date: a planned $100 billion spaceport located near the southern coast of Louisiana’s Vermilion Parish. Billed as "Starbase, Louisiana," the facility aims to host the company's gargantuan Starship rockets for upwards of 30 flights per day. Construction is slated to begin by the end of next year, with the first operational launches targeted as early as 2029.</p>

    <p>This development marks a tectonic shift for both SpaceX and the broader U.S. aerospace landscape. Louisiana, historically known for traditional energy extraction and maritime commerce, is poised to become home to the busiest launch hub on the planet. Governor Jeff Landry praised the initiative, contrasting it with historical extractive industries by framing the project as an investment in permanent prosperity. However, with a projected price tag of $100 billion, financial analysts and regional economists are closely examining how such an immense capital expenditure will transform local markets, labor demands, and corporate balance sheets.</p>
