    <p>A coalition of 21 former international cricket captains, including prominent figures from Australia, England, and India, has addressed a direct letter to Pakistan's current leader, Shehbaz Sharif. The signatories—ranging from Greg Chappell to Michael Atherton and Kapil Dev—are pressing authorities to ensure that Imran Khan receives adequate medical attention while serving time in custody since 2023.</p>
    
    <p>Khan, who famously led Pakistan to victory in the 1992 Cricket World Cup before transitioning into a polarizing political career, has been detained amidst more than 100 legal cases ranging from corruption to leaking state secrets. His supporters and legal team argue these charges are politically motivated. Recently, concerns spiked dramatically over reports regarding Khan's health, specifically a reported loss of vision in his right eye. Despite Supreme Court directions for assessment by private physicians and a specialized medical board, reports indicate he was evaluated briefly by a state-appointed hospital team and quickly returned to prison, prompting the contempt petition and the extraordinary international sports appeal.</p>
