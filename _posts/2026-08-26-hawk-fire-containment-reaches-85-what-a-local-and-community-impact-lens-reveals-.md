    <h2>What Happened: The Hawk Fire Progress</h2>
    <p>Fire crews battling the destructive <strong>Hawk Fire</strong> in the Reno, Nevada area have made significant headway, pushing containment to 85% as of Tuesday afternoon, August 25, 2026. Sparked on August 22 near the base of Peavine Peak, the blaze has scorched 15,067 acres of mountainous terrain, driven by continuous fuels and critically dry conditions. Operations Section Chief Matt Armantrout confirmed in a video update that forward progress on the wildfire has been officially stopped, allowing officials to downgrade several red "Evacuate Now" orders to yellow "Get Set" advisories in the White Lake area along Village Parkway.</p>

    <p>While the forward push of the flames has been halted, the human and structural toll is heavy. Washoe County officials reported that 47 homes have been destroyed and eight others damaged, alongside one business lost. U.S. 395 has reopened to both northbound and southbound traffic, though local authorities urge residents returning to affected areas to remain vigilant. Meanwhile, investigators have confirmed the fire was human-caused, though no suspect has been identified yet as the probe continues.</p>
