  <section id="introduction">
    <p>As Florida voters head to the polls this Tuesday to narrow the field for their next governor, the political atmosphere is charged with anticipation. With 17 candidates vying for major-party nominations to succeed term-limited Governor Ron DeSantis, the state is at a significant crossroads. While the headlines focus on polling leads and campaign financing, this moment serves as a quiet reminder for people of faith to consider the weight of civic stewardship and the biblical principles that should guide our participation in government.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The State of the Race</h2>
    <p>The Republican field is currently led by U.S. Rep. Byron Donalds, who has secured the endorsement of Donald Trump and maintains a significant lead in recent polling. Meanwhile, the Democratic primary is headlined by former U.S. Rep. David Jolly, who has shifted his political affiliation and is campaigning on a platform focused on the cost of living, housing, and education. With Florida’s political landscape shifting heavily toward Republican registration over the last decade, this primary is more than a contest—it is a test of the state's future direction.</p>

    <h2>A Biblical Perspective on Stewardship</h2>
    <p>When we participate in elections, we are exercising a form of stewardship. In the book of Proverbs, we are reminded: <em>"When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice: but when the wicked beareth rule, the people mourn"</em> (Proverbs 29:2, KJV). This verse does not suggest that any human candidate is the savior of our state, but it does highlight the importance of selecting leaders who reflect virtues like integrity, justice, and a commitment to the common good.</p>

  <section id="how-to-respond">
    <h2>How Should Christians Respond?</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Pray for Discernment:</strong> Ask for clarity to see past the campaign slogans and understand the true character and plans of those seeking office.</li>
      <li><strong>Engage with Humility:</strong> Recognize that fellow believers may arrive at different conclusions. Focus on the issues and values rather than attacking the personhood of others.</li>
      <li><strong>Focus on Policy Impact:</strong> Consider how candidates' proposed policies—whether regarding housing, health, or education—will impact the most vulnerable members of your community.</li>
      <li><strong>Stay Informed:</strong> Use reliable sources to understand the candidates' records, not just their rhetoric.</li>
    </ul>

Help us to remain faithful citizens, seeking the peace and prosperity of our communities, knowing that our ultimate hope rests in You alone. May Your will be done in the governance of this state, and may we live in a way that honors You in all our public and private actions. In Jesus' name, Amen.</p>
