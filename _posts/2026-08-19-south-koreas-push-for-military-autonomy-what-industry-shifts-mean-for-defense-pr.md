<section id="news-hook">
    <p>In a move that signals a significant pivot for South Korea’s national security strategy, President Lee Jae Myung has accelerated the push to reclaim wartime operational control (Opcon) from the United States. Following Donald Trump’s directive to scale back the ongoing Ulchi Freedom Shield joint military exercises, Seoul is doubling down on developing its own independent defense capabilities, including a nuclear-powered submarine program.</p>
</section>

<section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened?</h2>
    <p>President Lee’s announcement comes on the heels of President Trump’s decision to curtail joint military drills, citing a "very good relationship" with North Korean leader Kim Jong-un. For decades, the US has held wartime operational authority over South Korean forces—a legacy of the 1953 armistice. Now, Seoul is moving to certify its own readiness to assume this command, aiming to transform its military from a dependent partner into an autonomous force.</p>
</section>
