  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in Forest Grove</h2>
  <p>The sequence of harrowing events began early on a Saturday morning. Firefighters responding to a vehicle fire on Northwest Otis Lane—a quiet, dead-end street outside Forest Grove, Oregon—quickly realized they had stumbled upon something far more sinister than a routine car blaze. Observing immediate suspicious circumstances, first responders alerted law enforcement.</p>

  <p>What followed was a massive, multi-agency mobilization involving the Washington County Sheriff’s Office, the Washington County Major Crimes Team, forensic units from the Oregon State Police, and specialized tactical teams. By Sunday, authorities confirmed the grim reality: five people were found dead inside the property alongside multiple domesticated animals. Washington County Sheriff Caprice Massey, reflecting on more than two decades in law enforcement, told reporters she had never witnessed a scene quite as complex.</p>

  <p>As investigators worked meticulously to identify the victims and notify next of kin, the search for answers took an abrupt turn. Authorities identified 36-year-old Benjamin Parker as the suspect. By Sunday evening, deputies located Parker dead inside his car in Enumclaw, Washington, in what investigators believe was death by suicide. While officials assured the public there was no ongoing active threat, the profound shockwave of the tragedy remained firmly anchored in the Pacific Northwest community.</p>
