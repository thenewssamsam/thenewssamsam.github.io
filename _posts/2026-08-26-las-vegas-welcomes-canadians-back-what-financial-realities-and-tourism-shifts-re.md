  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The Canadian Sales Mission</h2>
    <p>Canada has historically been the number one international market for Las Vegas. However, recent economic pressures took a toll. Data from 2025 revealed an 18 percent year-on-year drop in Canuck arrivals, totaling 1.18 million visitors. While this decline was actually slightly better than the broader national average drop of 22 percent across various U.S. destinations, tourism leaders recognized that proactive measures were required.</p>
    
    <p>Led by Fletch Brunelle, VP of marketing at the LVCVA, and President & CEO Steve Hill, a contingent of 12 Las Vegas suppliers traveled to Vancouver, Calgary, and Edmonton. Their goal was straightforward: reconnect with 100 key travel advisors, provide updates on multi-billion-dollar infrastructure projects like the upcoming MLB stadium, and highlight affordable entry points such as the complimentary Neon City Festival and lower-cost Formula 1 ticket options.</p>

    <div class="ncg-quote-box">
      <p>"We love you, we want you, we need you." — The resounding mantra from the Las Vegas tourism contingent during their Western Canadian sales mission.</p>
