<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened: The Patria and General Cherry Agreement</h2>
<p>On August 23, 2026, Finnish defense major Patria and the Ukrainian drone manufacturer Center of Unmanned Technologies LLC—known widely as General Cherry—officially inked a Letter of Intent (LoI). According to press releases from Patria, the partnership is designed to bring General Cherry’s battle-tested small drone and interceptor expertise directly into Finnish manufacturing facilities.</p>

<p>Mikko Leino, Executive Vice President of Patria’s Defence and Weapon Systems business area, emphasized that unmanned systems sit at the core of future defense requirements. By combining General Cherry’s front-line engineering with Patria’s industrial scale and deep-rooted defense sector experience, the collaboration aims to build scalable, agile counter-UAS solutions tailored specifically for Western European defense needs.</p>

Interceptor drones offer a cost-effective equilibrium, lowering the per-engagement cost for military budgets.</p>
