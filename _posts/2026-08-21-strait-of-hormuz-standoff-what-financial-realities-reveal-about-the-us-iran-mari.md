  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened in the Strait?</h2>
    <p>The narrow corridor connecting the Persian Gulf to the open ocean has once again become one of the most volatile geopolitical flashpoints on Earth. Recent maritime traffic data and rising tensions between the United States and Iran have thrust the Strait of Hormuz back into global headlines. US President Donald Trump recently insisted that American forces remain in control of traffic through the waterway, even hinting at potential efforts to formally take charge of the territory. Meanwhile, Iran maintains that the strait is closed to any vessel failing to secure Tehran's approval before transit.</p>
    
    <p>This war of words is backed by real kinetic force. The US maintains a strict naval blockade targeting all Iran-linked commercial traffic, while both Washington and Tehran have actively targeted vessels perceived to be violating their respective maritime rules. Amid conflicting claims of control, shipping data from maritime intelligence firm Kpler offers an empirical look at how commercial carriers are actually voting with their rudders.</p>
  </section>

    <h2>The Financial Lens: Who Pays the Price?</h2>
    <p>From an economic and financial perspective, the Strait of Hormuz is not merely a geographic chokepoint; it is the vital artery through which a massive share of the world's petroleum and liquefied natural gas (LNG) flows. When military blockades collide with sovereign territorial claims, the ultimate cost is borne by supply chains, insurance underwriters, and ultimately, everyday consumers at the fuel pump.</p>

    <h2>Navigating the Dark Corridors</h2>
    <p>The high volume of vessels turning off their Automatic Identification System (AIS) transponders reveals a sophisticated adaptation by the commercial shipping sector. By going dark, captains reduce their digital visibility to warring military factions. However, this lack of transparency introduces secondary market inefficiencies, including safety risks and complex logistical choreography.</p>
