  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened: The Collapse of UAE-Iran Trade</h2>
    <p>The announcement from Abu Dhabi on Wednesday marks a dramatic turning point in an already volatile regional conflict. Following incoming ballistic missile fire that triggered nationwide shelter warnings across the UAE, the Emirati Foreign Ministry announced an immediate halt to all commercial and financial exchanges with Tehran "until further notice."</p>
    
    <p>For months, the Strait of Hormuz has served as the central flashpoint of a broader war involving the United States, Israel, and Iran. While limited maritime trade had briefly resumed in late June as hostilities temporarily eased, renewed attacks on commercial tankers—including ADNOC vessels operated by Abu Dhabi—shattered any fragile sense of normalcy. With roughly a fifth of the world's traded oil and natural gas normally moving through this narrow artery, the shutdown of trade channels between the UAE and Iran threatens to plunge an already struggling Iranian economy into even deeper isolation.</p>
