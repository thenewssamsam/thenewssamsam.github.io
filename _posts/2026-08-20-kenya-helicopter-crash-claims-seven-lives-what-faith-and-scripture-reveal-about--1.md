    <h2 style="font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; color: #1f2937; border-bottom: 2px solid #e5e7eb; padding-bottom: 8px; margin-top: 0;">
      What Happened: The Tragedy in Northern Kenya
    </h2>
    <p>
      On a quiet Wednesday morning that began like any other tourist excursion, a routine charter flight turned into an unspeakable tragedy. A safari helicopter operating in the remote and rugged landscapes of northern Kenya crashed at approximately 9:13 AM. According to local police and the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority, the aircraft was flying from the Loisaba Conservancy toward the Ewaso Nyiro area in Samburu county when it went down in the foothills of Mount Ololokwe.
    </p>
    <p>
      Local authorities and search-and-rescue operators—including dispatch units from Tropic Air Kenya—rushed to the remote scene, but confirmation soon arrived that none of the seven people onboard had survived. The victims included six passengers and the pilot. Among those identified by local officials and international reports were José Suárez, a 55-year-old president and general manager of several Telemundo stations in Florida, and Michele Sensi-Contugi, the director of Ecuador's national intelligence center, who was traveling alongside his wife, Stephany Hollihan Vasconez. In total, five of the victims were United States citizens.
    </p>
    <p>
      For families across Florida, Ecuador, and beyond, an ordinary vacation or business journey transformed instantly into an unimaginable void. Aviation accidents in tourist-dependent regions like Kenya highlight the complex logistics of remote travel, but for those left behind, statistics matter very little compared to the heavy, immediate reality of personal grief.
    </p>

    <p>
      Why do sudden accidents happen to people in the prime of life? It is a question that echoes through every generation. Scripture does not offer glib, overly simplistic answers to human suffering, but it does assure us that God is near to the brokenhearted. The tragedy in Samburu county reminds us that creation itself lives under the weight of a fallen world, where mechanical failures, sudden weather shifts, and human frailty intersect with devastating results.
    </p>

    <h2 style="font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; color: #1f2937; border-bottom: 2px solid #e5e7eb; padding-bottom: 8px; margin-top: 0;">
      How Should Christians Respond to Unexpected Sorrow?
    </h2>
    <p>
      When breaking news brings reports of sudden loss across the globe, our response should move past passive consumption of headlines into active spiritual reflection and compassion. Here are concrete ways to process such events:
    </p>
    <ul style="padding-left: 20px; color: #4b5563;">
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">
        <strong>Intercede for the Grieving Families:</strong> The immediate circle of family, friends, and colleagues mourning the five Americans and Ecuadorian officials are facing an agonizing shock. Intentional prayer for their comfort and strength is a tangible way to bear one another's burdens.
      </li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">
        <strong>Evaluate Our Own Priorities:</strong> Sudden tragedy acts as a spiritual mirror, asking us whether our daily investments align with what truly matters. Are we holding our earthly plans with an open hand, prioritizing love, forgiveness, and faithful stewardship?
      </li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">
        <strong>Practice Gratitude for Daily Grace:</strong> Every waking day is an unearned gift. A sobering news cycle should cultivate a deeper appreciation for the people in our lives and a renewed commitment to living out our faith authentically right where we are placed.
      </li>
    </ul>
