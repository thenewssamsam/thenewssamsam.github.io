Throughout history, ancient kingdoms have clashed over territory, influence, and survival, often leaving innocent populations to bear the heaviest burdens.</p>
    
    <div class="ncg-verse-box">

    <p class="ncg-p">It is easy for news consumers to reduce conflicts to statistics—number of explosions, diplomatic statements issued, square miles affected. Yet, behind every smoke plume in Southern Lebanon are families wondering if they should pack their bags, children whose schooling is interrupted, and farmers whose livelihoods hang in the balance.</p>
    <p class="ncg-p">This brings to mind the sobering reality of human vulnerability. No matter how advanced military technology becomes, the civilian toll remains the most tragic constant. Observing these events from a distance can easily harden our hearts into desensitized spectators, or conversely, fill us with helpless anxiety. Scripture invites us toward neither apathy nor despair, but toward active empathy.</p>
