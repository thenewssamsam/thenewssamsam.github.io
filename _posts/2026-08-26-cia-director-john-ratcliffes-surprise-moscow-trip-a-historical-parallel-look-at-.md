<section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
<h2>What Happened in Moscow?</h2>
<p>Flight-tracking data first flagged a U.S. military transport aircraft traveling from Joint Base Andrews through Latvia and touching down at Moscow's Vnukovo Airport on August 25. U.S. officials later confirmed to RFE/RL that CIA Director John Ratcliffe was on board, making his first publicly known trip to Russia since taking office under President Donald Trump.</p>

<p>Neither the CIA nor the White House immediately commented on the substance of the meetings or whom Ratcliffe met behind closed doors. However, the timing of the journey speaks volumes. Washington's diplomatic push to end Russia's war in Ukraine has effectively stalled, and tensions over Moscow's deepening military and commercial alliance with Iran have reached a boiling point. In the intricate dance of international relations, such visits are rarely social calls; they are high-stakes communications delivered away from the glare of public media cameras.</p>
