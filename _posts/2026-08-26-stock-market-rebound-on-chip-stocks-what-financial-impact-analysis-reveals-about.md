The Nasdaq Composite rose 0.7%, while the S&P 500 and Dow Jones Industrial Average each added 0.3%, marking the Dow's third consecutive winning session. Yet, beneath the surface of green numbers and tech sector enthusiasm, diverging economic indicators—from sliding oil prices to soaring cryptocurrency valuations—paint a nuanced picture of the current financial landscape.</p>
    <p>Understanding these market mechanics requires looking past the daily ticker tape.
