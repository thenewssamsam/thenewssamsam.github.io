    <p>As Florida heads to the polls for its first major primary elections following a significant mid-decade redistricting effort, the state finds itself at a political crossroads. Beyond the shifting district lines and campaign strategies, these elections invite voters to reflect on the deeper responsibilities of leadership, character, and the stewardship of our communities.</p>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Shifting Landscape</h2>
    <p>The new congressional maps, passed by the Florida legislature, have reshaped the electoral environment. Analysts suggest these changes could allow Republicans to flip up to four seats in the upcoming midterms. This shift has created significant tension, particularly for Democratic incumbents like Reps. Darren Soto, Jared Moskowitz, Debbie Wasserman Schultz, and Kathy Castor, who now face new challenges in redrawn districts.</p>
