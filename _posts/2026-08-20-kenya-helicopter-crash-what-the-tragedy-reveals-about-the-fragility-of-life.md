    <section id="the-news">
        <h2>A Sudden Tragedy in Samburu</h2>
        <p>A scenic flight intended to showcase the breathtaking beauty of Kenya's Samburu county ended in profound tragedy this Wednesday. A Eurocopter EC130 B4, operated by Lady Lori Helicopters, crashed near Mount Ololokwe, claiming the lives of seven people on board.</p>
        <p>Among the victims was Michele Sensi-Contugi, the director general of Ecuador’s Strategic Intelligence Center, who was traveling with his wife, Stephany Hollihan. The remaining five victims have been identified as American citizens, including respected NBCUniversal journalist José Alberto Suárez and Miami restaurateur Roger Duarte. As investigations by the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority (KCAA) continue, the world is left to process the sudden loss of these individuals in a region known for its remote, wild splendor.</p>
