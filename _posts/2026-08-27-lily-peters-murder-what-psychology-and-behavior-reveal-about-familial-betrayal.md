    <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in Chippewa Falls</h2>
    <p>The details of the case, as outlined in court documents and recent legal proceedings, remain deeply unsettling. On the evening of April 24, 2022, 10-year-old Lily Peters disappeared while walking home from her aunt's residence. Her cousin, Carson Peters-Berger—then just 14 years old—walked with her, ultimately luring her off the trail. Investigators later uncovered a chilling sequence of events. Peters-Berger confessed to punching, striking, and strangling the young fourth-grader before fleeing the scene, returning later to conceal her remains.</p>
    
    <p>While initial charges included sexual assault, those specific counts were later dismissed and read-in, allowing the judge to consider them during sentencing. On Wednesday, August 26, 2026, now 18 years old, Peters-Berger pleaded guilty to first-degree intentional homicide and received a sentence of life in prison with the possibility of extended supervision after 25 years. For the community and Lily's family—who remembered her through a poignant poem expressing a wish that "people were kind to each other"—the legal resolution marks a solemn milestone in an ongoing grief.</p>
