<section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: Remembering Tim Curry and Portland's Connection</h2>
    <p>Tim Curry’s career was nothing short of legendary. From his terrifying turn as Pennywise the clown in the 1990 <em>It</em> miniseries to his sharp comedic timing as Wadsworth in <em>Clue</em>, Curry possessed a rare chameleon-like ability to command the screen. Yet, it was his 1975 portrayal of the sweet transvestite from transsexual galaxy, Dr. Frank-N-Furter, that cemented his immortal status in pop culture history.</p>
    
    <p>Few places understood this better than Southeast Portland's Clinton Street Theater. Operating as a cultural anchor, the venue has played <em>The Rocky Horror Picture Show</em> every single Saturday night since 1978. Even through the darkest days of the global pandemic, screenings continued—sometimes playing to nearly empty rooms just to keep the sacred tradition alive. Upon hearing of Curry's passing, the theater organizers released a heartfelt statement noting that Curry was "bigger than the big screen" and praising his earnest dedication to the character that continues to unite misfits and moviegoers alike.</p>
