    <h1 class="ncg-title">Former Hostage Evyatar David Wedding Celebration: What Faith Reveals About Resilience Against Hamas</h1>
    <p class="ncg-intro">When former Gaza hostage Evyatar David danced at fellow survivor Eliya Cohen's wedding, it was more than a personal milestone—it stood as a striking public declaration of life overcoming darkness. In moments of profound trauma, choosing to celebrate joy becomes an act of quiet defiance.</p>

    <h2 class="ncg-h2" id="what-happened">A Gathering of Triumph Amidst Lingering Scars</h2>
    <p class="ncg-p">The recent wedding of Eliya Cohen brought together survivors, families, and communities whose lives were forever altered by the horrors of captivity under Hamas. Among the attendees was 25-year-old Evyatar David, stepping into the joyous celebration just months after returning home to Kfar Saba.</p>
    <p class="ncg-p">In his first interviews with international media following his harrowing ordeal, David shared fragments of his experience. Yet, rather than focusing solely on the shadows of captivity, the overarching narrative centered on the dance floor—where shared survival transformed into a communal victory over terror. When you have stared into the abyss of hopelessness, walking into a wedding hall filled with music, laughter, and new beginnings is a staggering testament to the human spirit.</p>

From a biblical perspective, gathering to celebrate marriage and family in the wake of immense suffering echoes the ancient prophetic promises of restoration.</p>
