  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The $119 Million Warning</h2>
    <p>Local government budgets rarely make front-page news unless an impending shortfall threatens everyday conveniences or public safety. That changed when Seminole County officials publicly detailed the sobering projections surrounding Amendment 3.</p>
    <p>According to reports from county leadership, if Amendment 3 is fully implemented, the local government estimates a recurring revenue reduction of approximately $119 million. While county manager Darren Gray noted that Seminole County's total annual budget sits at roughly $1.3 billion—making a $119 million cut look modest on a percentage basis—the reality of municipal finance makes those numbers far more rigid than they appear on paper.</p>
