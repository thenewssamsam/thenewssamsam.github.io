    <p>Under extreme physiological arousal, the human brain experiences narrowed focus. The suspect's bizarre decision to stop for gas or call a news station points toward a severe distortion of reality. When adrenaline floods the system, individuals often succumb to normalcy bias, acting as though standard rules simply pause while they improvise their immediate survival.</p>
