<section id="what-happened">
  <h2>What Happened: A Record-Breaking Deployment</h2>
  <p>The aircraft carrier USS Abraham Lincoln left its home port of San Diego back in November, eventually getting redirected to the Middle East to support operations in the U.S.-Israeli war with Iran. On Saturday, the vessel finally departed the region after setting a modern-day record for consecutive days at sea. Thai officials confirmed on Tuesday that the carrier strike group will dock in eastern Thailand next week for a brief, routine port visit focused entirely on personnel rest, recreation, and logistical support, with no military exercises planned.</p>
  
  <p>While U.S. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth noted that some public reports concerning shipboard conditions completely misrepresented the reality on board, family members of sailors shared profound concerns regarding declining morale and the immense psychological strain of passing the 200-day mark without stepping on solid ground.</p>
</section>
