  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened at Jets Practice?</h2>
  <p>Monday marked one of the most demanding practice sessions of Aaron Glenn's tenure as head coach. Facing a multifaceted workout designed to test situational football, the Jets offense found themselves on their own 30-yard line with the clock winding down in a simulated two-minute end-of-half drill. Geno Smith took command of the first-team unit, cleanly distributing the ball to weapons like star wide receiver Garrett Wilson and tight end Mason Taylor.</p>

  <p>Adversity struck when offensive lineman Olu Fashanu was flagged for a false start, pushing the unit back to the 18-yard line. Instead of faltering, Smith immediately responded by threading a tight-window pass up the middle to rookie wide receiver Omar Cooper Jr. for a touchdown. Head coach Aaron Glenn praised the offense's composure, noting that Smith "did a hell of a job of getting his guys in the end zone." Meanwhile, the defense flashed formidable playmaking of their own, with returning edge rusher Joseph Ossai, safety Andre Cisco, linebacker Jaden Keller, and edge Nate Lynn all recording disruptive plays in the backfield.</p>
