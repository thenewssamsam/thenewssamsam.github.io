<section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
<h2>What Happened: The Charges Against Joshua Kohut</h2>
<p>When leadership breaks down at an institutional level, the shockwaves are immediate. In late March 2026, state authorities in New Jersey arrested 51-year-old Joshua Kohut, the Dean of Research at the School of Environmental and Biological Sciences (SEBS) at Rutgers University. Kohut, a prominent figure residing in Cranbury, New Jersey, now faces a fourth-degree charge of invasion of privacy following a swift and alarming law enforcement investigation.</p>

<p>According to the New Jersey Attorney General’s office, investigators seized multiple electronic devices belonging to Kohut after receiving disturbing reports concerning his conduct while working on campus. A forensic examination of one of those devices revealed illicit "upskirt" videos—recordings capturing another individual's undergarment-clad intimate parts without their knowledge or consent.</p>

<div class="ncg-quote-box" style="border-left: 4px solid #7c3aed; background: #f5f3ff; padding: 1.5rem; margin: 1.5rem 0; border-radius: 0.5rem;">

<p style="margin: 0.5rem 0 0 0; font-size: 0.9rem; font-weight: bold; color: #6d28d9;">— Jennifer Davenport, New Jersey Attorney General</p>
</div>

<p>Rutgers University administrators reacted swiftly, labeling the allegations "extremely disturbing." The institution placed Kohut on immediate administrative leave and issued a strict campus ban while the criminal proceedings move forward. If convicted of the fourth-degree charge, Kohut faces up to 18 months in state prison and financial penalties reaching $10,000. Meanwhile, state investigators and the Division of Criminal Justice have opened lines of communication, urging anyone with additional information or potential victims to step forward.</p>
