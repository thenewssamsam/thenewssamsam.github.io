    <h3>Normalcy Bias vs. Hyper-Vigilance</h3>
    <p>During active seasons like 2026, psychologists often observe two contrasting reactions: normalcy bias and hyper-vigilance. Normalcy bias convinces people that because a major storm has never directly hit their specific neighborhood before, it never will. On the flip side, hyper-vigilance traps individuals in a loop of constant anxiety, tracking every minor shift in a forecast cone as an impending catastrophe.</p>
