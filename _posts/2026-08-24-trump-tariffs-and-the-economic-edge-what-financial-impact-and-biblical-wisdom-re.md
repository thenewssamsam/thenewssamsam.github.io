    <p>According to recent reports, former President Donald Trump has faced sharp criticism from economists, trade experts, and political opponents over proposals for sweeping, broad-based tariffs on imported goods. Detractors argue that such aggressive fiscal measures represent a massive policy miscalculation that could trigger retaliatory trade actions, accelerate inflation, and strain household budgets just as consumers are navigating ongoing financial uncertainties.</p>

<section id="financial-impact">
    <h2>The Financial Realities and Market Pressures</h2>
    <p>From a purely financial standpoint, tariffs function essentially as a tax on imports paid directly by domestic importers, costs that are typically passed down to the retail consumer. When broad tariffs are implemented without targeted exemptions, supply chains can experience severe friction, forcing businesses to absorb higher input costs or raise prices.</p>
