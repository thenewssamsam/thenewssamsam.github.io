<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: Meta's Massive Legal Turn</h2>
<p>On Wednesday, Meta (META) stunned market analysts by agreeing to pay up to $16.68 billion to resolve a sweeping lawsuit brought by a coalition of 29 state attorneys general. The core of the legal battle centered on explosive allegations that Facebook and Instagram intentionally engineered features to hook children and teens, while harvesting data from users under the age of 13 without adequate safeguards.</p>

<p>While Meta officially denied any wrongdoing in court filings, the company opted to settle rather than drag out a grueling trial. As part of the agreement, Meta committed to rolling out sweeping structural changes across Instagram and Facebook. These changes include hard caps on daily time limits, mandatory "Productive Pauses," strict nighttime feed blocks from midnight to 6:00 a.m., and the removal of push notifications during school hours.</p>
