  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened</h2>
    <p>Chicago will not make history by hosting back-to-back Democratic National Conventions. Officials announced on Thursday that the city's bid to host the 2028 DNC has been unsuccessful, narrowing the final host consideration list down to three other major American cities: Philadelphia, Boston, and Denver.</p>
    <p>Having successfully welcomed the nation for the 2024 DNC, local leaders submitted an enthusiastic bid to retain the spotlight for the subsequent presidential cycle. However, representatives for the Chicago 2028 Bid Committee confirmed that the torch will be passed elsewhere, expressing continued support for whichever destination ultimately secures the nomination.</p>

    <h2>The Loss Aversion Trap in City Bids</h2>
    <p>Behavioral economists often point to loss aversion—the psychological phenomenon where the pain of losing something feels significantly stronger than the pleasure of gaining it—to explain why cities fight so hard to repeat past successes.</p>
