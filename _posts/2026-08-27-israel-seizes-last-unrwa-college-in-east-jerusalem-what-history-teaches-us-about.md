<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened at the Qalandia Training Centre?</h2>
<p>The raid on Tuesday targeted the Qalandia Training Centre, established in 1952, which provided essential vocational education—such as carpentry, car mechanics, and plumbing—to roughly 340 young Palestinians each year. Situated between Qalandia and Kfar Aqab behind Israel's separation barrier in East Jerusalem, the compound served as an educational lifeline for refugee youth across the West Bank.</p>

<p>Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir personally joined the operation, broadcasting video footage from the director's office and declaring that the agency had no place under his watch. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's office confirmed that the evacuation was carried out under official government directives stemming from legislation passed last year. This legislation banned UNRWA from operating within Israeli-claimed territory and severed all official state contact with the agency.</p>
