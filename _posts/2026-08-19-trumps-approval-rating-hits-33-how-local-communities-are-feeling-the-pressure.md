  <section id="introduction">
    <p>President Donald Trump’s approval rating has dipped to a historic low of 33 percent, according to recent Reuters/Ipsos polling. As the war in Iran enters an uncertain chapter, the ripple effects are moving from the halls of Washington directly into the driveways and local economies of towns across America.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="the-facts">
    <h2>What Happened?</h2>
    <p>The latest polling indicates that 64 percent of Americans now disapprove of the President’s job performance. This drop coincides with deep public anxiety regarding the duration of the conflict with Iran, which many citizens initially expected to be a short-term military engagement. With fuel costs rising nearly 33 percent over the last year, the economic reality of the war is becoming a primary driver of public sentiment.</p>
  </section>
