<h2>What Happened in the Ossoff-Harp Media Cycle?</h2>
<p>The controversy began over the weekend during a campaign event for Georgia Democratic Senator Jon Ossoff. While rallying supporters, Ossoff took a sharp swing at President Donald Trump, suggesting that Trump would rather "build a ballroom and travel with Natalie" than tend to his presidential duties. The comment, widely interpreted by critics and supporters alike as relying on suggestive innuendo regarding Trump and his 35-year-old personal aide, Natalie Harp, immediately ignited a firestorm.</p>

<p>Conservatives swiftly condemned the remark as sexist and cowardly, noting that when pressed on MS NOW, Ossoff insisted he simply meant Harp served as a "security blanket" rather than standing by the heavier implications of his phrasing. Meanwhile, progressive media outlets seized on the moment. Major publications and cable networks dedicated substantial airtime and digital inches to profiling Harp, dissecting her proximity to the president, and even airing an interview with her estranged brother.</p>

<section id="practical-response">
<h2>How Should Christians Respond?</h2>
<p>Navigating a news cycle dominated by political gossip and viral jabs requires intentionality and spiritual discernment. Here are concrete ways to respond:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Practice the Discipline of Refusal:</strong> When sensational headlines or suggestive rumors cross your social media feed or news alerts, choose not to engage, click, or share. Starve the outrage machine of your attention.</li>
<li><strong>Guard Your Speech:</strong> Evaluate how you talk about public figures and political opponents in private conversations. Remember that political disagreement does not exempt us from the call to speak with kindness and truth.</li>
<li><strong>Look Past the Caricature:</strong> Remember that the people featured in news reports are human beings with families, faith, and inherent dignity. Pray for leaders and staffers on all sides of the political aisle rather than viewing them merely as combatants in a cultural war.</li>
<li><strong>Seek Constructive Truth:</strong> Support journalism and commentary that prioritizes substantive policy discussion, factual reporting, and charitable dialogue over cheap viral moments.</li>
</ul>
