  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The Solano County Stand off</h2>
    <p>Hours after the Solano County Board of Supervisors voted to avoid taking a supportive position on state proposals designed to assist the California Forever developers, Governor Newsom addressed reporters in Vallejo with a characteristically cryptic assessment: "There's yes, no and maybe because we have to see."</p>
    <p>The core of the dispute involves state-level maneuvers prepared by the Newsom administration, local lawmakers, and the tech-billionaire-backed project. These proposals would fast-track environmental lawsuits and allow future manufacturers or shipyards in Southern Solano County to utilize older 2008 environmental impact reports. The legislative clock is ticking rapidly toward an August 31 deadline, and while local representative Assemblywoman Lori Wilson respects the county's cautious stance, the governor remains convinced that his framework is "inevitable."</p>
