<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: Disney Fights Back</h2>
<p>On Tuesday, Disney filed a lawsuit in the DC District Court alleging that the Trump administration and FCC Chairman Brendan Carr are waging a "retaliatory campaign" against ABC. The catalyst for this escalation involves regulatory scrutiny over ABC station licenses, investigations into diversity policies, and an equal-time inquiry regarding the daytime talk show "The View."</p>

<p>According to the lawsuit, the pressure began mounting after conservative backlash over late-night comic Jimmy Kimmel and intensified as the FCC demanded early license renewals for eight Disney-owned ABC stations—a procedure virtually never invoked outside of its standard eight-year cycle. Disney argues that these actions violate First Amendment protections, forcing the company to choose between capitulating to government demands or fighting a costly regulatory war.</p>
