<h2>What Happened at the Great New York State Fair?</h2>
<p>The Great New York State Fair is officially underway in Geddes, New York, welcoming crowds eager to experience the sights, sounds, and culinary staples of late summer. Governor Kathy Hochul recently opened the 2026 festivities, highlighting the enduring tradition of the massive regional gathering. For many attendees, the initial barrier to entry appears quite reasonable: general fair admission is set at $8.32, with children aged 12 and under and seniors 65 and older entering completely free of charge.</p>

Behavioral economists refer to this as lower upfront friction, which successfully drives high volume through the gates.</p>
