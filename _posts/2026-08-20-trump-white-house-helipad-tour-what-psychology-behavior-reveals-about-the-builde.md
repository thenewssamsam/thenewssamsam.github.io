    <h2>What Happened: A Construction Site Press Conference</h2>
    <p>President Donald Trump traded the traditional briefing room for the South Lawn on Wednesday, turning a routine presidential update into an impromptu construction walkthrough. Surrounded by the thrum of heavy machinery, the president showed off a newly laid white granite helipad designed to withstand 35,000 pounds of pressure per square inch. Gushing over the durability of natural stone compared to asphalt, he even leaned over to autograph a piece of the granite that will eventually form part of the presidential seal.</p>

<section id="the-psychology-of-building">
    <h2>The Psychology of the Builder-in-Chief</h2>
    <p>From a behavioral standpoint, watching a leader pivot intensely toward physical construction projects during high-stress political seasons offers a fascinating window into human coping mechanisms. When the abstract variables of geopolitics, legislative battles, and economic forecasts feel overwhelming or intractable, the human brain naturally craves domains where tangible control is still possible.</p>
