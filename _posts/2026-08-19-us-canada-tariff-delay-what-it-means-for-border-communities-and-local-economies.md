<section id="the-local-reality">
<h2>Why Border Towns Are on Edge</h2>
<p>For those living in border regions—from the automotive hubs in the Midwest to the timber and manufacturing towns of the Pacific Northwest—the threat of a 50% tariff is not just a policy headline. It is a potential shock to the local cost of living and employment stability. When trade flows are interrupted, the "just-in-time" supply chains that sustain local factories often grind to a halt.</p>
