<h2>What Happened: The Mecca Pact Expands</h2>
<p>According to reports originating from the Iranian Parliament news agency and covered by outlets like Al Mayadeen, Tehran has been formally approached to enter the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement. This development follows the initial signing of the pact on August 7, when Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, and Turkey formalized an arrangement stipulating that an armed attack on any single member nation would be treated as an attack on all three. US President Donald Trump publicly welcomed the initiative on Truth Social, calling it a bold step toward regional self-reliance.</p>
