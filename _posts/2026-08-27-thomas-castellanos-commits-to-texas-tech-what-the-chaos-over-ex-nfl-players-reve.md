      <h2>What Happened: The Castellanos Transfer and Roster Chaos</h2>
      <p>The landscape of college football shifted once again as Thomas Castellanos officially committed to Texas Tech. Castellanos, who competed as a wide receiver during the Miami Dolphins' rookie minicamp after going undrafted, is back in the collegiate ecosystem thanks to rapid-fire courtroom interventions. Following a temporary injunction granted in a Kentucky eligibility lawsuit—protecting players from retroactive Class of 2022 restrictions—Castellanos became eligible to make the jump.</p>

      <p>Last season, Castellanos put up impressive numbers for the Seminoles after transferring from Boston College, completing 58.3% of his passes for 2,760 yards, 15 touchdowns, and nine interceptions, while adding 557 rushing yards and nine scores. He even guided Florida State to an unforgettable Week 1 upset over Alabama. At Texas Tech, he is expected to serve as a high-value backup to starter Will Hammond as Joey McGuire's squad eyes a dominant run in the Big 12.</p>
