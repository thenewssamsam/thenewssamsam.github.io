  <section id="introduction">
    <p>The recent announcement by Pennsylvania Attorney General Dave Sunday regarding a massive cocaine distribution ring at Penn State University has sent shockwaves through the academic and professional community. With 14 individuals charged—including current students and members of two off-campus fraternities—the case highlights a disturbing convergence of criminal enterprise and organizational "indoctrination." Beyond the legal implications, this situation serves as a stark case study in how toxic organizational cultures can normalize illegal behavior under the guise of group belonging.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Anatomy of the Alleged Operation</h2>
    <p>According to the Attorney General's office, the operation functioned as a highly profitable, coordinated drug-trafficking crew. The alleged ringleaders, Agostino Abbatiello and Thomas Robinson, reportedly managed a supply chain that brought large quantities of cocaine from New York and Philadelphia to the Penn State campus. </p>
