    <h1>Palestinian American Returns to West Bank Home Under Siege: A Faith and Scripture Perspective</h1>

<p>When Loui Ridi boarded a flight from Ohio back to the West Bank, he wasn't traveling for a vacation or a family reunion. He was answering a desperate call to protect his own home, which had been surrounded and besieged by Israeli settlers for over a week. For days, Ridi had watched his family's property through live home security feeds from thousands of miles away, witnessing a nightmare unfold in real time.</p>

<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in Qusra?</h2>
<p>The standoff centers on the village of Qusra in the occupied West Bank, where groups of Israeli settlers have surrounded several Palestinian homes, effectively trapping residents inside and preventing them from leaving. Ridi's brother and teenage son remained inside the house to defend the property, while Ridi rushed back from the United States after watching the siege unfold on his cameras.</p>
<p>This localized standoff is part of a broader, alarming surge in West Bank violence. According to Palestinian health officials, Israeli soldiers and settlers have killed 87 Palestinians so far this year, while Palestinian attacks have killed three Israelis. Human rights observers note that settlements and settler movements have experienced heightened tensions amid a political climate that has emboldened right-wing groups to ramp up raids, harassment, and property destruction across Palestinian towns and villages.</p>

<h2 id="how-should-christians-respond">How Should Christians Respond?</h2>
<p>For believers watching conflicts flare up across the globe, responding requires more than passive observation or taking partisan sides. It calls for active discernment and compassion:</p>
<ul>
    <li><strong>Pray for Peace and Protection:</strong> Lift up families like the Ridis who are enduring fear and displacement in their own homes, praying specifically for physical safety and emotional endurance.</li>
    <li><strong>Stand for Truth and Accountability:</strong> Support credible reporting and humanitarian efforts that bring transparency to injustices, regardless of who is committing them.</li>
    <li><strong>Cultivate a Heart for the Oppressed:</strong> Reflect on our own spheres of influence to ensure we are never complicit in the marginalization or mistreatment of our neighbors.</li>
</ul>
