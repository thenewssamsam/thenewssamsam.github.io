    <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Vitruvias Recall?</h2>
    <p>Drug manufacturer Vitruvias Therapeutics Inc. issued a voluntary nationwide recall for Lot 504950 of 30 mg Thyroid Tablets. According to announcements posted by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), these tablets were distributed to wholesalers nationwide between January 31, 2025, and September 30, 2025.</p>
    <p>The core issue involves potency variations. The recalled pills were found to be "superpotent," meaning they contain a higher concentration of active ingredients than intended. For patients managing hypothyroidism—a condition where the thyroid gland does not produce enough hormones—taking an excessively strong dose can accidentally push the body into the opposite physiological extreme: hyperthyroidism, or an overactive thyroid.</p>
