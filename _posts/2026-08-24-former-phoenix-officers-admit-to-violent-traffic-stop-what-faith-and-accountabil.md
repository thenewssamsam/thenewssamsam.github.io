  <h2 style="color: #2c3e50; border-bottom: 1px solid #eaeaea; padding-bottom: 8px; margin-top: 30px;">What Happened: The Allegations Against Former Phoenix Officers</h2>
  <p>When the very people entrusted with upholding the law cross the line into egregious criminal conduct, the shockwaves reverberate far beyond a single city block. Recently released court documents in Phoenix, Arizona, have laid bare a deeply disturbing incident involving two former police officers. Antonio Felix, 31, and Luis Vasquez Jr., 31, were fired and subsequently arrested following what Phoenix Police Chief Matt Giordano bluntly classified as severe criminal behavior.</p>
  
  <p>The harrowing sequence of events began just after 11:30 p.m. on August 5. According to detailed probable cause documents and prior investigative reports, Vasquez and Felix were driving an unmarked Phoenix police Tahoe when they began following a Dodge Charger in south Phoenix. Witnesses and investigators noted that the officers utilized intermittent overhead light and siren activations that starkly contrasted with standard law enforcement traffic stop procedures.</p>
  
  <div style="background-color: #fcf8e3; border-left: 4px solid #f0ad4e; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0; border-radius: 4px;">
    <strong>Quick Take:</strong> The allegations span multiple felony charges—including aggravated assault, kidnapping, tampering with physical evidence, hindering prosecution, and threatening and intimidating—demonstrating a total breakdown of professional and ethical boundaries.
  </div>

  <p>When the Charger eventually stopped near 15th Avenue and Broadway Road, the unmarked police vehicle allegedly struck the rear bumper of the civilian car. What followed bore little resemblance to a routine police interaction. Court paperwork indicates that Vasquez approached the driver's side while Felix took the passenger side, immediately threatening to break windows if the occupants did not exit.</p>
