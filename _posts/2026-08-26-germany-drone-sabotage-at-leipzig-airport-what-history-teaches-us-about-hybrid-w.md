As intelligence agencies probe deeper into who is orchestrating these brazen attacks, the incident forces us to look past the immediate headlines and examine how great powers have waged shadow wars across generations.</p>

    <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened at Leipzig Airport?</h2>
    
    <p>The sequence of events revealed by German authorities paints a picture of a sophisticated, multi-pronged sabotage operation. On August 4, a drone carrying explosives was found near a Ukrainian Antonov cargo plane filled with kerosene. Investigators deduced that the device—though it ultimately failed to detonate—was intended to strike the fuel-laden wings of the aircraft.</p>
