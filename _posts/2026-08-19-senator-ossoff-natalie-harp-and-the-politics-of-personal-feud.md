  <p class="ncg-intro">When a sitting U.S. senator calls out a lesser-known presidential assistant by name at a campaign rally, it signals a deliberate shift from policy debate to high-stakes psychological warfare. Georgia Senator Jon Ossoff's recent remarks targeting Natalie Harp have ignited an intense personal feud with Donald Trump, instantly reshaping a critical battleground Senate race.</p>

  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in Atlanta</h2>
  <p>During a campaign rally in Atlanta, Democratic Senator Jon Ossoff took direct aim at President Donald Trump and his inner circle. Ossoff, who faces a tough reelection battle in November against Trump-backed Republican Representative Mike Collins, questioned the president's focus and energy.</p>

  <p>In his speech, Ossoff invoked Natalie Harp, a close executive assistant to the president who frequently travels with him. Ossoff criticized the administration's global posture and foreign trips, specifically referencing travel with Harp on what he characterized as a gifted aircraft.</p>

  <p>The remarks drew immediate cheers from the crowd and triggered swift, fiery responses from the White House. Communications Director Steven Cheung and spokesman Davis Ingle launched sharp verbal counterattacks on social media, while President Trump compared the senator to Pee-wee Herman during an Oval Office interview with CNN.</p>
