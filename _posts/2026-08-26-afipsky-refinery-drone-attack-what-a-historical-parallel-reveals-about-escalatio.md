  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened at the Afipsky Refinery?</h2>
  
  <p>On Tuesday, local authorities confirmed a devastating fire broke out at the Afipsky oil refinery following a wave of overnight drone attacks. Governor Veniamin Kondratyev reported that at least two people were killed and two others injured by falling drone debris at the nearby Afipsky railway station. Simultaneously, two maintenance and construction workers were wounded at the Russian-controlled Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant during a separate strike near its hydraulic structures.</p>

  <p>Ukraine's General Staff confirmed its forces targeted facilities at Afipsky alongside a gas processing plant in the Astrakhan region. These operations form part of an escalating campaign to disrupt Moscow's war effort by targeting fuel supplies and economic revenue. In response, Russian air defenses claimed to have intercepted 148 drones overnight, while military strikes hit cargo vessels and port infrastructure near Odesa.</p>

  <p>The conflict's expanding footprint prompted Russian President Vladimir Putin to sign a decree allowing the state to take temporary control of critical infrastructure deemed inadequately protected. Meanwhile, in Kyiv, leaders from the "Coalition of the Willing" pledged continued support, with discussions centering on expanding Ukraine's long-range strike capabilities.</p>
