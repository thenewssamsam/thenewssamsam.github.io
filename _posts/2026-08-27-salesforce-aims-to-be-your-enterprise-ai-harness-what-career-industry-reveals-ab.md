    <h1>Salesforce Aims to Be Your Enterprise AI Harness: What Career & Industry Reveals About the Future of Work</h1>
    <p class="ncg-lead-paragraph">Salesforce is rolling out AIforce to act as a master governance harness for enterprise data and AI agents, aiming to protect corporate security through zero data retention while reshaping how business software operates. For professionals navigating an increasingly automated landscape, this shift signals a profound transformation in enterprise technology careers.</p>
  </header>

  <section class="ncg-section" id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened at Salesforce</h2>
    <p>During Salesforce's second-quarter earnings call, CEO Marc Benioff offered a glimpse into the company's upcoming Dreamforce conference strategy, putting a massive spotlight on a proprietary framework called <strong>AIforce</strong>. Developed and refined over the past three years, AIforce is designed to serve as an enterprise harness that safely governs corporate data and workflows, funneling them into AI agents without requiring messy custom integrations.</p>

    <p>In tandem with this announcement, Salesforce and artificial intelligence lab Anthropic revealed <em>Claudeforce</em>. This specialized integration allows Salesforce to supply the necessary context and enterprise data while Claude provides the reasoning and answers—all built around a critical enterprise requirement: zero data retention. By ensuring that external AI models do not retain corporate data, Salesforce is attempting to solve the number-one security barrier holding companies back from full-scale AI adoption.</p>
