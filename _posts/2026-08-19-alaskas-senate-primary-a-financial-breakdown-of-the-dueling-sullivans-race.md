  <section id="the-economic-stakes">
    <h2>The Financial Platforms at Play</h2>
    <p>For the average Alaskan, this race is fundamentally about the cost of living. Incumbent Senator Dan S. Sullivan has built his platform on the expansion of domestic energy production. From a financial perspective, his support for oil and gas extraction is framed as a direct mechanism to lower energy costs and stimulate state revenue. Conversely, former U.S. Representative Mary Peltola has centered her campaign on "fish, family, and freedom," criticizing a system she views as tilted toward special interests. Her critique touches on a nerve for many voters: the feeling that political influence has become a commodity that keeps the cost of housing and groceries unnecessarily high.</p>
