  <section id="introduction">
    <p>Recent reports regarding the scaling back of joint U.S.-South Korea military exercises have sparked significant debate about the future of regional security. As President Lee Jae Myung calls for a more independent defense posture, misinformation regarding the nature of these drills has begun to circulate. Here, we separate the myths from the facts to clarify what is actually shifting on the Korean Peninsula.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Current Situation</h2>
    <p>Following directives from President Trump to "substantially reduce" joint military drills, U.S. Forces Korea commander Gen. Xavier Brunson visited the South Korean Defense Ministry. Concurrently, President Lee has emphasized the necessity of South Korea developing its own robust defense capabilities, including a nuclear-powered submarine program, while maintaining the alliance with the United States.</p>
  </section>
