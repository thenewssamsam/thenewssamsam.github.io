<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened: The Expanding Drone Campaign</h2>
<p>The geography of the Russia-Ukraine war shifted further inward as long-range Ukrainian drones struck a major warehouse belonging to Wildberries—Russia’s largest online retailer—burning the facility in the Tambov region southeast of Moscow to the ground. This strike follows a series of escalating aerial operations targeting logistical hubs far from the front lines, alongside a separate overnight drone attack that set ablaze a massive Lukoil oil refinery in Nizhny Novgorod.</p>

<p>According to reports from the Associated Press, these operations represent a deliberate expansion of Ukraine's drone strategy. Kyiv contends that e-commerce giants like Wildberries distribute equipment and technical components utilized by the Russian military, an assertion the company denies. Meanwhile, the relentless nightly barrage—with Russian air defenses claiming to intercept hundreds of drones across multiple regions—has prompted President Vladimir Putin to issue a decree empowering the state to temporarily seize control of critical infrastructure if private owners fail to secure or rebuild damaged facilities.</p>
