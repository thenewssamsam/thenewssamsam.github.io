  <h2 id="financial-literacy">What This Means for General Financial Literacy</h2>
  <p>This situation illustrates a vital lesson in financial literacy: <strong>valuation is always a forward-looking expectation, not a historical reward.</strong></p>
  <p>A company can report phenomenal trailing earnings and still experience a falling stock price if investors believe future earnings will contract. Conversely, a stock can soar while a company loses money if markets anticipate future dominance. When evaluating high-growth cyclical stocks, investors must separate the operational health of the business today from the cyclical risk of the industry tomorrow.</p>
