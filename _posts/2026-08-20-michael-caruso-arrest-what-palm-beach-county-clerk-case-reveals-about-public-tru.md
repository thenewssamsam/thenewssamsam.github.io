<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened in Palm Beach County</h2>
<p>The arrest of Palm Beach County Clerk of Court and Comptroller Michael Caruso on five felony charges has sent shockwaves through local government and the legal community. According to the Florida Department of Law Enforcement and Attorney General James Uthmeier, the 67-year-old former Republican legislator faces grave allegations involving a child under the age of 12, including kidnapping, lewd or lascivious molestation, and child abuse causing mental injury.</p>

<p>Following the announcement, Florida Governor Ron DeSantis moved swiftly to suspend Caruso from office, barring him from performing official duties, receiving pay, or accessing public facilities connected to his elected role. Law enforcement agencies have secured the clerk's office to ensure records remain untouched as the investigation continues. While Caruso was denied bond pending his initial court appearance, legal analysts point out that an arrest is only the beginning of a rigorous judicial process.</p>
