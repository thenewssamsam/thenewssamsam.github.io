  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Current Situation</h2>
    <p>Following a Truth Social post, President Trump directed Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to curtail the Ulchi Freedom Shield exercises, citing a desire to avoid signaling hostility toward North Korea. Trump noted his "very good" relationship with Kim Jong Un, while simultaneously pressuring Seoul to provide more support for U.S. operations in Iran. With approximately 28,500 U.S. personnel stationed in the region, the scaling back of these drills represents a significant pivot in how the U.S. manages its military footprint and strategic alliances.</p>
