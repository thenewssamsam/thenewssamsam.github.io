    <h2>What Happened: The 72-Hour Tariff Pause</h2>
    <p>Late Tuesday, just minutes before sweeping import taxes were set to take effect, President Trump announced on social media that he was halting the proposed 50 percent tariffs against Canada for a three-day period. According to the administration, the pause was enacted because the U.S. and Canada reached a tentative deal, subject to the finalization of documents.</p>
