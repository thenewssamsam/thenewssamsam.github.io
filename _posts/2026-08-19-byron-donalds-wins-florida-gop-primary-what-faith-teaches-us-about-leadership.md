  <section id="news-hook">
    <p>Representative Byron Donalds has secured a decisive victory in Florida’s crowded Republican gubernatorial primary, positioning himself as the candidate to succeed term-limited Governor Ron DeSantis. Backed by a preemptive endorsement from President Donald Trump, Donalds now moves forward to face Democratic challenger David Jolly in what promises to be a defining election for the state's future.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Path to the Nomination</h2>
    <p>The primary race was a high-stakes contest featuring several prominent Florida figures. Despite the crowded field, Donalds—a three-term congressman—leveraged his strong alignment with the "Trump agenda" to separate himself from the pack. His primary opponent, Lt. Gov. Jay Collins, ran on a platform of continuing the DeSantis legacy, but voters ultimately favored Donalds’ vision for the state.</p>
