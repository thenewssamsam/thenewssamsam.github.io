  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The 177% Surge Explained</h2>
    <p>The healthcare sector witnessed one of its most dramatic trading sessions of 2026 when Moderna (MRNA) and its partner Merck (MRK) announced that their personalized mRNA cancer vaccine, intismeran, successfully hit its primary goal in a Phase 3 late-stage clinical trial. The treatment demonstrated a meaningful reduction in melanoma recurrence among patients who had already undergone surgical tumor removal.</p>

    <p>The trial evaluated intismeran administered alongside Merck's blockbuster immunotherapy drug, Keytruda, compared against Keytruda alone. Patients receiving the combination therapy lived meaningfully longer without their cancer returning or spreading. Dr. Ezekiel Emanuel highlighted the magnitude of the results on Yahoo Finance, calling it "a home run, maybe even a grand slam."</p>

    <p>However, the volatile nature of biotech stocks quickly showed its teeth. After the explosive 177% single-day gain, Moderna shares pulled back roughly 20% in early trading as short-term traders locked in profits and institutional investors digested the long-term commercial implications.</p>
