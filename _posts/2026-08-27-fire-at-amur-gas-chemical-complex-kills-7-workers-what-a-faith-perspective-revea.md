<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened at the Amur Facility?</h2>
<p>According to reports from <em>The Moscow Times</em>, the catastrophic incident occurred at a support section of a pyrolysis unit within the AGCC site. The complex, a sprawling $10 billion partnership between Russian petrochemical giant Sibur and China's state-owned Sinopec, was undergoing rigorous final testing ahead of an anticipated commercial launch in early 2027.</p>
<p>Initial reports confirmed that nineteen workers were hospitalized with moderate to severe injuries, while more than a hundred others received medical treatment for minor cuts and bruises. Tragically, recovery operations later confirmed the deaths of six Chinese workers and one Russian national, with several others remaining missing as emergency crews sift through the aftermath. Regional consumer protection authorities have reported no toxic chemical spikes in nearby Svobodny, but the emotional and physical toll on the families of the victims remains immeasurable.</p>
