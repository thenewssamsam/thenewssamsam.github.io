<section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
<h2>What Happened?</h2>
<p>Orange County commissioners are officially moving forward with negotiations on a proposed $19.4 million pedestrian promenade designed to reshape one of Orlando's busiest tourist corridors. Centered around the heavily traveled intersection of International Drive and Sand Lake Road, the initiative aims to tackle long-standing safety hurdles for pedestrians navigating the bustling district.</p>

<p>According to recent reports, the project is intended to complement an upcoming pedestrian bridge at the exact same location. While theme park visitors and local commuters frequently squeeze through the area in heavy vehicle traffic, residents and everyday walkers have long pointed out that simply getting across the street feels like an exhausting endurance test. Tuesday's commission vote marks a decisive step toward turning conceptual renderings into actual infrastructure.</p>

<h2>From Grand Vision to Concrete Reality</h2>
<p>Big infrastructure projects in major tourism centers rarely happen overnight, and this proposal carries a rich history. Project representatives highlighted that the current agreement between Gateway and Orange County breathes life back into the long-dormant "I-Drive 2040 vision," which had largely lost momentum since 2017.</p>

<p>Transforming stagnant blueprints into concrete asphalt and public green spaces requires patience. Current municipal timelines suggest that promenade construction could kick off in 2028 and stretch through 2029, with the corresponding pedestrian bridge project following afterward and continuing through 2031.</p>
