    <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section" style="margin-bottom: 35px;">
        <h2 style="font-size: 1.6rem; font-weight: 700; color: #2d3748; margin-bottom: 15px; border-bottom: 2px solid #edf2f7; padding-bottom: 8px;">What Happened: The Tragedy in Islamabad</h2>
        <p>In the quiet early hours of Tuesday, August 26, 2026, routine gave way to horror at a hospital in Islamabad, Pakistan. According to reports from The Associated Press and local medical personnel, a sudden and catastrophic fire erupted around 6:45 a.m. within the nursery of the gynecology ward. The immediate catalyst was identified as an air-conditioning unit compressor explosion—a mechanical failure that transformed a place meant for healing and new beginnings into a site of unimaginable grief.</p>
        
        <p>The human toll was immediate and devastating: fourteen newborns perished in the flames and thick smoke before rescue teams could fully intervene. Government and hospital officials quickly responded with expressions of profound sorrow. Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi took to social media to voice his anguish, while Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif labeled the incident "an irreparable loss" and promptly ordered a formal investigation into the safety protocols and maintenance history of the facility. President Asif Ali Zardari echoed these sentiments, offering heartfelt condolences to the grieving families and emphasizing the sheer heartbreak of a disaster that targeted the most vulnerable members of society.</p>
    </section>
