        <h2 style="font-size: 1.6em; color: #222; margin-top: 40px; border-bottom: 1px solid #eee; padding-bottom: 8px;">What Happened: Rupert Grint Steps Back Into the Wizarding World</h2>
        <p>
            For a full decade, from 2001 to 2011, millions of moviegoers watched Rupert Grint grow up on the silver screen as Ron Weasley, the loyal, brave, and frequently exasperated best friend of Harry Potter. Now, according to reports from ABC News, Grint is preparing to officially reprise that career-defining role on Broadway. He is slated to join the cast of <em>Harry Potter and the Cursed Child</em> at the Lyric Theatre for a limited run from February 2 to May 30, 2027.
        </p>
        <p>
            The stage production picks up the narrative nineteen years after the conclusion of <em>Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows</em>. In this timeline, an adult Harry is married to Ginny Weasley, while Ron is married to Hermione Granger. Both couples are raising children, and Ron can be found running his signature joke shop, Weasley's Wizard Wheezes.
