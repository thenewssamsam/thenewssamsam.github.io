<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the UN Security Council?</h2>

<p>The high-stakes warning follows Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's public rejection of Washington's newly proposed 15-point peace plan. The US-backed initiative aims to finally secure an enduring end to the devastating war in Gaza, which erupted following Hamas's October 7, 2023, attacks that killed roughly 1,200 people in southern Israel and saw 240 taken captive. Since then, the conflict has claimed the lives of at least 73,422 Palestinians, according to local health officials, with an additional 1,288 killed since a previous US-brokered ceasefire attempt began in October.</p>

<p>Under the new Board of Peace roadmap—which Hamas agreed to on July 30—the group would hand over its weapons to a new Palestinian governing body, with the decommissioning supervised by an International Stabilization Force led by US Major General Jasper Jeffers. Civilian and humanitarian affairs would fall to an emerging National Committee for the Administration of Gaza, comprised of independent Palestinian technocrats.</p>
