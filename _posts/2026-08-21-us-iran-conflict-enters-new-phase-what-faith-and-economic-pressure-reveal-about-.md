<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: A Shift to Economic Warfare</h2>
<p>The geopolitical standoff between the United States and Iran has crossed a threshold. Vice President JD Vance recently announced that the ongoing confrontation has entered a distinct "new phase," leaning heavily on economic isolation and financial leverage rather than direct military engagement alone. This declaration follows sweeping threats from President Donald Trump of unprecedented economic warfare, which Iranian officials have publicly dismissed as domestic political diversion.</p>

<p>Simultaneously, the physical theater of operations is shifting. The U.S. Navy's USS Abraham Lincoln is returning home after a grueling nine-month deployment marked by crew strain and supply challenges, while the USS George Washington has arrived in the region to enforce an expanding naval blockade in the Strait of Hormuz. According to U.S. Central Command, commercial shipping restrictions continue to mount, trapping vital trade routes in a high-stakes standoff while new Treasury sanctions target cash-smuggling networks linked to Hezbollah.</p>
