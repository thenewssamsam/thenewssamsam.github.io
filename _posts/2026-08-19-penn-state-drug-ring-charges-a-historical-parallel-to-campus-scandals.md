<section id="introduction">
    <p>Fourteen individuals, including current and former Penn State students, are facing charges following an alleged cocaine-trafficking operation tied to two local fraternity organizations. The scope of the operation—which allegedly used fraternity houses for the packaging and distribution of narcotics—has sent shockwaves through the university community. While modern law enforcement uses digital tracking and payment apps to dismantle these rings, the underlying tension between campus culture and illicit activity is a story as old as the American university system itself.</p>
</section>

<section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Allegations at Penn State</h2>
    <p>Pennsylvania Attorney General Dave Sunday announced that the operation, active throughout 2023 and 2024, was allegedly spearheaded by senior members of the Delta Upsilon and Sigma Chi fraternities. Investigators claim that pledges were sometimes required to package cocaine as part of their "indoctrination" into the groups. The charges include felony counts of corrupt organizations and conspiracy, with one defendant even accused of using a family member to hide proceeds.</p>
