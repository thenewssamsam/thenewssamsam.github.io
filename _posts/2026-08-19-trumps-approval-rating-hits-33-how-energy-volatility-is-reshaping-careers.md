    <h2>The Current Political and Economic Climate</h2>
    <p>According to the latest Reuters/Ipsos poll, 64 percent of Americans now disapprove of the President’s handling of his job. The drop aligns with the ongoing war in Iran, which has caused gasoline prices to climb nearly 30 percent higher than they were a year ago. Despite initial promises that the conflict would be brief, 80 percent of Americans now believe the war will continue for an extended period.</p>

    <h3>2. The Shift Toward Efficiency Roles</h3>
    <p>Economic downturns—or fears of them—often lead to a "flight to quality" regarding human capital. Businesses prioritize roles that directly impact the bottom line. Professionals who can demonstrate how their work reduces waste, optimizes supply chains, or improves energy efficiency become highly valuable when corporate budgets are strained.</p>
