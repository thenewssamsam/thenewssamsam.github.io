  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: The Arrest and Suspension of Michael Caruso</h2>
  <p>Palm Beach County Clerk of Court and Comptroller Michael Caruso, 67, was arrested by the Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE) following an extensive investigation by the Office of Statewide Prosecution. Attorney General James Uthmeier announced the five felony charges, which include kidnapping, lewd or lascivious molestation, lewd or lascivious exhibition, luring or enticing a child, and child abuse causing mental injury. If convicted, Caruso faces a possible life sentence.</p>

  <p>The investigation began after a family member observed concerning behavior in late 2025, leading to disclosures by the young victim about alleged incidents occurring across Orange County, Palm Beach County, and during a family cruise aboard Royal Caribbean's Icon of the Seas in August 2025. Following the arrest, Florida Governor Ron DeSantis moved swiftly, issuing an executive order suspending Caruso from office and prohibiting him from performing official duties, receiving pay, or accessing records.</p>
