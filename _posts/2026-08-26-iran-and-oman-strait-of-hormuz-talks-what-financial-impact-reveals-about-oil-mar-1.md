  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Strait of Hormuz?</h2>
  <p>Diplomatic talks between Iranian and Omani officials in Tehran have centered on establishing a temporary joint shipping route through the strategic Strait of Hormuz, alongside a planned mission to clear the crucial waterway of mines. Oman's Foreign Minister, Sayyid Badr bin Hamad Albusaidi, expressed hope that practical arrangements to restore safe navigation would soon be announced.</p>
  
  <p>However, these diplomatic efforts unfold amid profound geopolitical friction. Oman's mention of active mine-clearing operations openly contradicts recent statements from U.S. President Donald Trump, who noted that the U.S. Navy informed him all mines had been cleared from international waters in Hormuz. Meanwhile, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced that the U.S. will sanction any entity engaging in business with the Islamic Republic, and Washington has strongly pushed back against Muscat's bilateral negotiations.</p>
