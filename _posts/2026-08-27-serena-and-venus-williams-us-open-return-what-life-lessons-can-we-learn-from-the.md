<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: The Return to Flushing Meadows</h2>
<p>The United States Tennis Association announced that tennis legends Serena and Venus Williams have been awarded a wild card into the 2026 US Open women's doubles tournament. For the American icons, who famously claimed the New York doubles titles together in 1999 and 2009, this marks their first Grand Slam appearance as a team since the 2022 US Open.</p>

<p>The road back hasn't been without hurdles. Following Serena's return to the WTA Tour during the grass-court season, a knee injury at Wimbledon temporarily halted their plans. After reuniting briefly at the Western & Southern Open in Cincinnati, the sisters are now gearing up for the electric atmosphere of the US Open alongside other notable wild card pairs like Robin Montgomery, Ashlyn Krueger, and collegiate standouts.</p>
