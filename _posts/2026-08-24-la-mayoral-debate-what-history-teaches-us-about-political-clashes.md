<h2>What Happened in the Debate?</h2>
<p>The 2026 Los Angeles mayoral race officially shifted into high gear as incumbent Mayor Karen Bass and challenger Nithya Raman squared off in their first heated debate of the general election season. Hosted by the Sherman Oaks Homeowners Association, the encounter brought deep policy divisions and sharp personal clashes to the forefront for local voters.</p>

<p>The debate immediately zeroed in on Los Angeles' most pressing challenges: homelessness, housing development, police staffing, and support for the struggling entertainment industry. With recent polling showing Bass vulnerable and unsheltered homelessness ticking upward after previous drops, Raman pressed the incumbent hard on city governance and accountability. Bass fired back by questioning Raman's record, legislative alliances, and past political rhetoric, setting the stage for a hard-fought campaign leading up to November.</p>
