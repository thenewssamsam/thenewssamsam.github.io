<section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened in the Markets?</h2>
    <p>As trading desks across the Asia-Pacific region blinked open on Monday, August 24, 2026, the global financial landscape was met with a compounding wave of geopolitical and macroeconomic stress. Most notably, gold surged to a three-month high, breaking above $4,640 an ounce before experiencing volatile profit-taking that pushed it briefly under $4,600. This dramatic price action did not happen in a vacuum.</p>
    
    <p>Investors woke up to a trifecta of compounding anxieties: the Trump administration preparing its toughest-ever sanctions package against Iran, the total collapse of US-Canada trade negotiations following the implementation of heavy 50% tariffs, and growing concerns over China's economic growth slipping below official targets. Equity markets in Asia responded predictably, with South Korea's KOSPI index tumbling up to 3% intraday and Japan's Nikkei 225 opening lower. In moments like these, when institutional certainty frays, capital shifts rapidly toward traditional safe havens.</p>
</section>
