<section class="ncg-intro">
<p>The United States is preparing to launch what Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent describes as "the single greatest financial offensive ever" against Iran, setting the stage for a dramatic escalation in an economic war that has now entered its sixth month. As Washington moves to tighten secondary sanctions and isolate foreign financial arteries connected to Tehran, Iranian officials have countered with threats of their own—vowing to target regional neighbors and enforce strict transit rules, fines, or vessel seizures in the critical Strait of Hormuz.</p>
<p>For ordinary global citizens, business owners, and everyday consumers, this high-stakes standoff is far more than a distant geopolitical headline. When superpowers clash over the world's most vital energy chokepoint, the ripple effects hit household budgets, fuel prices, and supply chains instantly. To understand what happens next, we need to examine the financial mechanics driving this escalation, the broader economic vulnerabilities on both sides, and the timeless moral wisdom found in Scripture regarding conflict and human pride.</p>
</section>

<section id="financial-impact">
<h2>The Financial Mechanics of the Strait of Hormuz Chokepoint</h2>
<p>From a financial and economic perspective, the Strait of Hormuz is arguably the most sensitive geographic bottleneck on planet Earth. Before the current war began, roughly one-fifth of the world's seaborne oil passed through this narrow waterway daily. Any sustained disruption, threat of seizure, or mine hazard instantly reverberates across global commodities markets.</p>
