    <section id="what-happened" style="margin-bottom: 40px;">
        <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; color: #2d3748; margin-bottom: 15px; border-bottom: 2px solid #3182ce; padding-bottom: 5px;">What Happened?</h2>
        <p>On Wednesday, Boston-based U.S. District Judge Indira Talwani lifted a nationwide injunction that had previously blocked President Trump's executive order regarding mail-in ballots. This judicial pivot followed a crucial 6-3 Supreme Court decision on Monday that favored the administration in a parallel case brought by California and 22 other states.</p>
        <p>The executive order in question directs the U.S. Postal Service to track mail-in ballots and potentially reject those submitted by individuals who do not appear on a federal list of eligible voters. While the Supreme Court concluded that lower courts had acted too hastily in issuing broad injunctions, the high court did not officially rule on whether the underlying postal service regulation is ultimately lawful.</p>
