<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Florida Primary?</h2>
<p>Rep. Cory Mills, a Republican military veteran who held a comfortable lead in early polling, saw his political standing collapse following a series of ethics investigations and alleged federal probes. Despite frequently leaning on Donald Trump's coveted endorsement on the campaign trail to weather the storm, Mills was unseated by Ryan Elijah, a former Orlando-area news anchor and NFL sideline reporter who campaigned heavily on community roots in Seminole County.</p>

<p>The defeat follows months of mounting pressure. Bipartisan friction inside the House saw lawmakers like Rep. Nancy Mace push for censures and investigations, turning Mills into a central flashpoint for congressional conduct. For parents watching the news cycle unfold with their children in the room, the race offers much more than a political scoreboard. It presents an opening to talk about accountability, character, and the very real consequences of our actions.</p>
