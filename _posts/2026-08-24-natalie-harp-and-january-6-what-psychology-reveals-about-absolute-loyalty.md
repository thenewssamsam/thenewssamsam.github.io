<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: The Resurfaced Social Media Record</h2>

<p>According to recent reports by CNN and the Daily Beast, Natalie Harp operated a social media account on what is now known as X where she posted fervently in support of Donald Trump during the weeks surrounding the 2020 election and the January 6, 2021, Capitol riot. On the day of the Capitol attack itself, records show she posted approximately 150 times, featuring slogans such as "FIGHT FOR TRUMP!", "Today, DC is TRUMP COUNTRY!", and "We will never concede!"</p>

<p>Second, identity fusion plays a significant role. In social psychology, identity fusion occurs when the boundary between the personal self and a social group or leader becomes porous. For individuals deeply immersed in a political movement, defending the leader feels indistinguishable from defending oneself. This can foster remarkable resilience in the face of widespread criticism, but it can also narrow perspective, filtering out dissenting information that challenges the core narrative.</p>
