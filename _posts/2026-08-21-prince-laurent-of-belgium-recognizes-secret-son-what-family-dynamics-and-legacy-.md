  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened in the Belgian Royal Family?</h2>
    <p>According to recent reports from outlets like <em>Town & Country</em> and <em>Soir Mag</em>, Prince Laurent formally registered his paternity regarding Clément Vandenkerckhove earlier this year, roughly a year after first acknowledging him publicly. Clément, born in 2000 to model, actress, and singer Iris Vandenkerckhove, is now legally a Prince of Belgium and carries the style of Royal Highness, courtesy of a 2015 royal decree.</p>
    <p>Despite the grand title, Clément will not receive public funding—allowances in the Belgian monarchy are strictly reserved for the reigning monarch and the direct heir to the throne—nor will he undertake official public duties. He remains eligible for his father's private inheritance, joining Prince Laurent's three other children: Princess Louise and twin sons Prince Nicolas and Prince Aymeric. More importantly, Clément has expressed immense joy over their repaired bond, emphasizing the simple moments they share over pasta in Brussels rather than the weight of the crown.</p>
