<section id="introduction">
    <p>Following the announcement of serious criminal charges against 14 individuals—mostly current or former Penn State students—linked to alleged narcotics operations and hazing, the university community is grappling with the fallout. While legal proceedings move forward, the incident highlights a deeper need to examine the campus environments that shape student health and long-term well-being.</p>
</section>

<section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened?</h2>
    <p>Pennsylvania Attorney General Dave Sunday recently announced criminal charges involving an alleged narcotics operation spanning 2023 and 2024. The investigation centers on activities linked to two off-campus Greek organizations: Delta Upsilon and Sigma Chi. In response, Penn State has placed Delta Upsilon on interim suspension, while noting that Sigma Chi currently operates outside of university oversight. The university is conducting parallel investigations through its Office of Student Accountability and Conflict Response.</p>
</section>
