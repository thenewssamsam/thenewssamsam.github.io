    <h2>What Happened in Florida's 7th District?</h2>
    <p>In Florida's 2026 primary election, incumbent Republican Representative Cory Mills was unseated by former local television news anchor Ryan Elijah. According to race calls by The Associated Press, Elijah secured a victory by about 12 percentage points in a race that stretched from the northern suburbs of Orlando to the coast south of Daytona Beach.</p>
    <p>The defeat follows a turbulent period for Mills, who has faced an ongoing House Ethics Committee investigation over allegations of sexual misconduct and campaign finance violations. Although Mills has consistently denied any wrongdoing and previously won the district twice, accumulating political baggage shifted the dynamic of the race. Local voters like Tiffany Davis expressed concerns that the mounting controversies made the seat vulnerable in a general election against Democratic nominee Bale Dalton, prompting a shift in support toward Elijah.</p>
