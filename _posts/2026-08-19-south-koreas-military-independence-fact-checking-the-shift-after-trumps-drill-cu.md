<time datetime="2026-08-18">Published: August 18, 2026</time> | 7 min read

<section id="introduction">
<p>In a move that has sparked intense debate over the future of the Korean Peninsula, U.S. President Donald Trump has ordered a substantial reduction in current joint military exercises with South Korea. President Lee Jae Myung is responding by accelerating the push for South Korea to regain wartime operational control (Opcon) and expand its own military capabilities. As rumors and claims circulate, it is essential to separate political rhetoric from the reality of military readiness.</p>
</section>

<section id="myth-vs-fact">
<h2>Myth vs. Fact: Sorting Through the Claims</h2>
<div class="ncg-fact-check" style="border-left: 4px solid #6a0dad; background: #f9f4ff; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0;">
<h3>Myth: The reduction of joint drills means the US-South Korea alliance is ending.</h3>
<p><strong>Fact:</strong> While the scale of the <em>Ulchi Freedom Shield</em> exercises has been reduced, the alliance remains officially intact. The U.S. continues to maintain a significant military presence, including at Camp Humphreys. Adjusting the scope of drills is a tactical decision, not a formal dissolution of the mutual defense treaty.</p>
</div>

<div class="ncg-fact-check" style="border-left: 4px solid #6a0dad; background: #f9f4ff; padding: 15px; margin: 20px 0;">
<h3>Myth: North Korea is currently "unthreatening" because of personal diplomacy.</h3>
<p><strong>Fact:</strong> Despite claims of a "very good relationship" between Trump and Kim Jong-un, military officials—including Col. Ryan Donald—note that North Korea’s integration of battlefield lessons from the Ukraine war has objectively increased their tactical capabilities. The regime’s deepening military pact with Russia remains a documented security concern for regional stability.</p>

<section id="the-opcon-question">
<h2>What is Wartime Opcon?</h2>
<p>Wartime Operational Control (Opcon) is the authority to command forces during an active conflict. Since the 1953 armistice, this authority has rested with the U.S.-led Combined Forces Command. Reclaiming this is a central pillar of South Korea’s desire for full national sovereignty. It is not merely a bureaucratic change; it is a symbol of a mature, independent defense posture.</p>
