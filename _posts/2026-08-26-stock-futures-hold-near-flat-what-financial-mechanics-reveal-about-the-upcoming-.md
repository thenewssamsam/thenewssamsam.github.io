  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened in the Markets</h2>
    <p>As trading desks prepared for a heavy data calendar, S&amp;P 500 futures remained little changed, while Nasdaq-100 futures ticked up by nearly 0.1%. This pause follows a strong winning session where the S&amp;P 500 climbed 0.3%, the Nasdaq Composite advanced 0.7%, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average added 160.24 points to mark its third consecutive positive day.</p>
    <p>Globally, Asia-Pacific markets traded mixed on Wednesday. Japan's Nikkei 225 dipped 0.25%, South Korea's Kospi fell 0.3%, while Australia's S&amp;P/ASX 200 edged 0.2% higher. Hong Kong's Hang Seng Index rose 0.96%, and the CSI 300 added 0.37%. Meanwhile, international benchmark Brent crude futures for October delivery declined 2.52% to $86.35 a barrel, and U.S. West Texas Intermediate futures dropped 2.17% to $80.56 per barrel following less severe than anticipated U.S. sanctions on Iran.</p>
  </section>
