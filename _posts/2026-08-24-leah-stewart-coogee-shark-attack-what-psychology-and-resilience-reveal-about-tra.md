<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened at Coogee Beach</h2>
<p>On the morning of June 13, a routine swim at Sydney's Coogee Beach turned into a fight for survival. Thirty-four-year-old mother Leah Stewart came face-to-face with a great white shark in an attack that left her in a medically induced coma for two weeks, requiring multiple surgeries and ultimately resulting in the amputation of her arm.</p>
<p>Months after the horrific event, Stewart has broken her silence in an emotional interview with the Nine Network’s <em>60 Minutes</em>. Recounting the harrowing ordeal, she described the shark as a "monster" whose face she will never forget. Yet, amidst the terror and physical trauma, her first and most enduring memory is not the jaws of the predator, but the image of her 18-month-old daughter, August.</p>

For Stewart, that instinct was entirely anchored to her child.</p>
