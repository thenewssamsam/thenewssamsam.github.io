    <h2>What Happened: The Allegations Against Rep. Jimmy Gomez</h2>
    <p>The House Ethics Committee announced an official investigation into 51-year-old Democratic Representative Jimmy Gomez of California, examining whether he engaged in sexual misconduct and inappropriate sexual contact with a House staffer. While the congressman has acknowledged personal mistakes outside his marriage, he maintains that those relationships were consensual and that he violated no ethics rules.</p>

From Eric Swalwell and Tony Gonzales to Sheila Cherfilus-McCormick and Chuck Edwards, a wave of scandals has exposed systemic vulnerabilities in how congressional offices handle personal conduct, power imbalances, and accountability.</p>
