    <h2 id="what-happened" style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; color: #2d3748; border-left: 4px solid #3182ce; padding-left: 12px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
      What Happened in Mays Landing
    </h2>
    <p>
      On a quiet Saturday morning that should have brought routine weekend stillness to Mays Landing, New Jersey, sudden tragedy struck at the intersection of Route 50 and Third Street. According to reports from the Atlantic County Prosecutor's Office, a stolen vehicle careened into a head-on collision with another car around 7:30 a.m., resulting in the immediate deaths of two adults traveling in the victimized vehicle. 
    </p>
    <p>
      The aftermath of the crash quickly escalated beyond a standard traffic accident. Authorities confirmed that the occupants of the stolen vehicle—consisting of one adult and three juveniles—survived the crash and now face criminal charges. Adding a layer of complex scrutiny, the New Jersey Attorney General's Office announced that its Office of Public Integrity and Accountability has launched an active investigation. Under state guidelines, this specialized division steps in whenever a fatality occurs during or in close connection with law enforcement encounters, though officials have not yet detailed the exact nature of police involvement leading up to the impact.
    </p>
