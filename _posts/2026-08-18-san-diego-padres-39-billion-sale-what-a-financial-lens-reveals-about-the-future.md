    <h2>Economic Ripple Effects for San Diego and Petco Park</h2>
    <p>Crucially, the transaction includes a 30% ownership stake in Petco Park, meaning the deal requires procedural approval from the City of San Diego before closing by the end of the week. This real estate component highlights why sports franchise transactions carry immense local economic weight.</p>
    <p>Petco Park is widely regarded as one of the crown jewels of Major League Baseball, anchoring the East Village neighborhood and driving substantial economic activity for local hospitality, dining, and retail businesses. When new owners commit to "investing ambitiously and thoughtfully," local stakeholders watch to see how capital expenditure flows into the stadium experience and the surrounding urban ecosystem. Unlike volatile public markets, sports infrastructure investments are deeply localized, tying the financial fortunes of the ownership group directly to the economic vitality of the city itself.</p>
