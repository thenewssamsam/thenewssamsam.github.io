<h3>The Escalation of Commitment</h3>
<p>In behavioral psychology, the "escalation of commitment" describes a pattern where individuals justify increased investment in a decision, based on the cumulative prior investment, despite new evidence suggesting the cost outweighs the benefit. When legal fees climb into the millions, both sides become deeply anchored to their positions. Walking away feels like an admission of defeat, driving parties to double down even when a settlement or moderation might serve their long-term well-being better.</p>
