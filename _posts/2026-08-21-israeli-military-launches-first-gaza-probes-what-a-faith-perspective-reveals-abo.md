    <h2>What Happened in the Gaza Investigations?</h2>
    <p>In a rare policy shift after months of escalating international pressure, Israel's military announced its first formal criminal investigations into specific incidents of troop conduct in Gaza. The probes center on two deeply harrowing events that captured global attention: the February 2024 killing of 5-year-old Hind Rajab, who was trapped in a vehicle surrounded by her slain family members while pleading for help on the phone, and the March 2025 shooting death of 15 Palestinian paramedics and civil defense workers whose bodies were subsequently bulldozed into a mass grave.</p>

    <h2>Aid Workers and the Limits of Internal Inquiry</h2>
    <p>The decision by the military to forgo criminal investigations into the deaths of World Central Kitchen and Doctors Without Borders personnel—despite reprimands and the removal of certain officers—highlights the profound friction between institutional self-preservation and external demands for justice. Aid organizations like World Central Kitchen have publicly rejected the military's explanations, asserting that such internal reviews muddy the narrative and shield responsible parties from true consequence.</p>
