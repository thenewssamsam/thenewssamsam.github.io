<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened: The 2028 DNC Shortlist</h2>
<p>The Democratic National Committee announced that Philadelphia is one of three finalist cities contending to host the 2028 presidential nominating convention. Joining Philadelphia on the narrowed shortlist are Denver and Boston, while Chicago and Atlanta have been cut from the running. If the party selects Philadelphia, the convention will unfold from August 7 to 10, 2028, utilizing the Xfinity Mobile Arena in the South Philadelphia sports complex.</p>
<p>This development follows a spring evaluation tour led by DNC Chair Ken Martin and other top officials to assess logistical readiness, transit infrastructure, and security capabilities. Mayor Cherelle Parker and local boosters have leaned heavily into Philadelphia's recent streak of hosting high-profile global gatherings—ranging from FIFA World Cup matches to MLB All-Star events and America’s 250th-anniversary celebrations—to argue that the city is uniquely prepared to manage an event of this scale.</p>
