<h2>What Happened in Niger State?</h2>
<p>The incident unfolded in broad daylight last week in the Dekera district of Niger State, north-central Nigeria. Heavily armed attackers targeted local mosques, rounding up approximately 600 worshippers—men, women, and children—and marching them into dense forested areas. According to local district officials, at least 30 people lost their lives during the initial violence and were buried on Sunday.</p>

<h2>Echoes of History: The Vulnerability of Borderlands</h2>
<p>The mass abduction in Niger State highlights a persistent historical pattern: the extreme vulnerability of remote border regions. Northwest and north-central Nigeria, sharing porous borders with Niger, Chad, and Benin, have long served as transit zones for illicit arms and armed groups. Historically, when central governments struggle to project effective administrative and security reach into outlying provinces, opportunistic armed factions and extremist networks fill the vacuum.</p>
