  <p>While artificial intelligence investments and booming oil exports provide some upward lift, broader economic realities tell a more complicated story for average households. For anyone trying to balance a family budget or plan long-term investments, understanding the mechanics behind these trends is essential.</p>

    <p>Economic growth during the first Trump and Biden presidencies hovered around an annual average of 2.5%. However, data cited by Morici shows that Trump 2.0 has delivered closer to 2% growth. Despite the massive tailwinds provided by artificial intelligence data centers and petroleum exports, the broader economy is largely treading water.</p>
