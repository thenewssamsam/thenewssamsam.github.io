<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened?</h2>
<p>Seminole County officials announced that construction is slated to begin within the next month or two on the Midway Trail—a mile-long pedestrian path designed specifically to protect children walking to Midway Elementary and Millennium Middle schools in the historic Midway community near Sanford.</p>
<p>Currently, children traveling from the Washington Oaks neighborhood must navigate a busy stretch along State Road 46, crossing near an 85-acre stormwater pond. Local residents, such as Terry Britton, have voiced ongoing concerns about young students distracted by play while walking alongside heavy highway traffic. In addition to student safety, the project aims to address long-standing physical separations between neighborhoods by introducing community amenities like shaded plazas, green spaces, exercise stations, and a fishing pier.</p>
