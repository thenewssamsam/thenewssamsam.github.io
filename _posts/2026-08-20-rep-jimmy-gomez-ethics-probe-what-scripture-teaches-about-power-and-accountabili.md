    <h2>What Happened in the Jimmy Gomez Investigation?</h2>
    <p>The House Ethics Committee has officially opened an investigation into Democratic Representative Jimmy Gomez of California following serious allegations of sexual harassment and assault. According to statements released by attorneys Lisa J. Banks and Sarah E. Nesbitt, one of their clients informed the ethics panel that Gomez subjected her to non-consensual sexual contact.</p>

Congressman Gomez has acknowledged making what he termed personal mistakes outside his marriage, but he maintains that those actions were consensual and did not violate the law or congressional guidelines.</p>
