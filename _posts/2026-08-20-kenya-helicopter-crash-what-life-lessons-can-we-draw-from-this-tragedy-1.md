  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Tragic Reality</h2>
    <p>On Wednesday morning, a routine flight turned into a national tragedy in Kenya. A Eurocopter EC130 B4 helicopter, carrying seven passengers, crashed near Mount Ololokwe in Samburu County. The incident resulted in the loss of all souls on board, including five American citizens and the director general of Ecuador’s intelligence agency, Michele Sensi-Contugi, along with his wife, fashion designer Stephany Sensi.</p>
    <p>The aircraft was being operated by Lady Lori Helicopters and was traveling between the Loisaba wildlife conservancy and the Ewaso Ngiro river. As search and recovery teams navigate the difficult, rugged terrain, the world is left to process the suddenness of such a loss. While authorities investigate the technical cause, the human element remains at the forefront of our thoughts.</p>
