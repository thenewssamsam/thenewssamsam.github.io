    <h2>What Happened</h2>
    <p>Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz has officially ordered the Israel Defense Forces (IDF) to ramp up the pace of dismantling Hezbollah's extensive terror infrastructure across southern Lebanon. According to recent reports from the Jerusalem Post, ongoing engineering operations are not only neutralizing hidden militant assets, bunkers, and weapons caches but are also actively denying Hezbollah the physical terrain required to stage guerrilla raids against northern Israeli communities.</p>
    
    <p>For months, the border region has remained a volatile flashpoint. By systematically clearing out entrenched underground networks and fortified positions, military planners aim to fundamentally alter the security landscape. But beyond the tactical maps and military briefings lies a deeper human reality—one that touches on the ancient human quest for safety, the heavy cost of prolonged conflict, and the moral dimensions of national defense.</p>
