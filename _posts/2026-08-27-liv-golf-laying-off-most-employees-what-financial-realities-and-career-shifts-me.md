<section id="what-happened">
  <h2>What Happened at LIV Golf?</h2>
  <p>In a stunning corporate about-face that underscores the volatile economics of modern sports ventures, LIV Golf told its workforce of more than 300 employees on Wednesday that the vast majority will be laid off next week. The cuts come as the controversial, Saudi-backed golf league attempts to reinvent itself under a new financial backer, pivoting toward a scaled-down operating model CEO Scott O'Neil has dubbed "LIV 2.0."</p>
  
  <p>According to reports from Forbes, the league lost its primary funding from Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund in April, after burning through more than $1 billion since its founding in 2022 against $5 billion in total investment. While executives have spent months securing a term sheet with a new lead investor—widely reported to be Ted Goldthorpe of BC Partners—the transition requires severe structural trimming. Bankruptcy remains a distinct possibility on the table as a mechanism to restructure lingering debt and renegotiate massive, multi-million dollar player contracts with stars like Jon Rahm and Bryson DeChambeau.</p>
