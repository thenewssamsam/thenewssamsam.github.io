  <section id="introduction">
    <p>A routine flight turned into a profound tragedy this Wednesday when a helicopter crashed near Mount Ololokwe in central Kenya, claiming the lives of all seven people on board. Among the victims were five Americans and the director general of Ecuador’s intelligence agency, Michele Sensi-Contugi. As investigators work to determine the cause, the incident serves as a sobering reminder of the unpredictability of human life.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened in Samburu County?</h2>
    <p>The Eurocopter EC130 B4 was en route from the Loisaba wildlife conservancy to the Ewaso Ngiro river when it went down in the rugged terrain of Samburu County. The Kenya Civil Aviation Authority confirmed that all seven occupants—five Americans, the intelligence chief, and his wife, fashion designer Stephany Sensi—perished at the scene.</p>
    <p>Recovery efforts have been significantly hindered by the difficult mountain landscape and an active fire at the crash site. The US Embassy is currently coordinating with Kenyan authorities to assist the families of the victims.</p>
