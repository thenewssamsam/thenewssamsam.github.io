<h2>What Happened: The August Missile Barrage</h2>
<p>On Thursday afternoon, South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff (JCS) detected a barrage of short-range ballistic missiles fired eastward from Pyongyang. Japan's Defense Ministry confirmed that at least one projectile was launched, landing safely outside Japan's exclusive economic zone. This incident marks the third ballistic missile test by North Korea in August alone, following a previous launch on August 12.</p>
<p>The timing of these launches is deeply intertwined with regional geopolitics. They occurred squarely during the ongoing U.S.-South Korean Ulchi Freedom Shield military drills. However, these exercises are slated to end early following an order from President Donald Trump to "substantially reduce" the annual drills. President Trump cited his reported "very good" relationship with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un when announcing the cutback, signaling plans for a future meeting later this year.</p>
