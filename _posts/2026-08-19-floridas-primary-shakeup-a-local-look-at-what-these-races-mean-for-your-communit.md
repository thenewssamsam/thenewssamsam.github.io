<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Is Happening at the Polls?</h2>
<p>Florida’s primary election is a pivotal moment for state and congressional representation. In the race to succeed Governor Ron DeSantis, Republican voters are deciding between several candidates, including Rep. Byron Donalds. Meanwhile, the state is operating under a newly redrawn congressional map that has shuffled district boundaries, forcing many voters to adjust to new representatives and different policy priorities.</p>
</section>

<section id="local-impact">
<h2>Why This Matters to Your Community</h2>
<p>When district lines change, so does the connection between a representative and their constituents. In many parts of Florida, residents are finding themselves in new districts where the local concerns—whether it’s coastal infrastructure, education funding, or regional economic development—may be prioritized differently than they were previously.</p>
