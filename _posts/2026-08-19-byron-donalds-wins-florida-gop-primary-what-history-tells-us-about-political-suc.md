    <p>Rep. Byron Donalds has secured a decisive victory in Florida’s crowded Republican gubernatorial primary, signaling a new chapter for the Sunshine State as it prepares to transition away from the two-term tenure of Governor Ron DeSantis. Donalds, buoyed by an early endorsement from President Donald Trump, now pivots to a general election campaign against Democrat David Jolly. While the immediate focus is on the upcoming vote, the dynamics of this succession offer a fascinating case study in how political legacies are passed—or redefined—in American history.</p>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Path to the Nomination</h2>
    <p>The primary result was a definitive win for Donalds, who faced stiff competition from figures like Lt. Gov. Jay Collins. Despite Collins running on a platform of "protecting the DeSantis legacy," voters opted for the candidate most closely aligned with the Trump-era agenda. Donalds has centered his campaign on economic deregulation, immigration enforcement, and infrastructure projects, framing his candidacy as the natural evolution of the populist movement that has reshaped Florida’s political landscape over the last decade.</p>
  </section>
