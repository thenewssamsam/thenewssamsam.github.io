<p>During a high-level meeting in Kyiv, Ukrainian Prime Minister Serhii Koretskyi and Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre outlined an extensive framework for bilateral cooperation. The discussions zeroed in on three critical pillars: immediate winter preparations, reinforcement of Ukraine’s air defense grid, and the long-term stabilization of national finances.</p>

<p>The centerpiece of the visit was the formal signing of the Defense Technology Cooperation Agreement—widely referred to as the Drone Deal. This pact is designed to accelerate joint defense manufacturing, pool technological expertise, and streamline the production of unmanned systems. Rather than operating purely on a donor-recipient model, the agreement creates a collaborative industrial pipeline between Kyiv and Oslo.</p>
