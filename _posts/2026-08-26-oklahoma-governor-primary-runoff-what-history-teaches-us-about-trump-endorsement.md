<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened in the Oklahoma Primary Runoff?</h2>
<p>The Republican primary runoff to replace term-limited Governor Kevin Stitt pits Mike Mazzei, a 61-year-old investment firm founder and former state senator, against Gentner Drummond, the state's 62-year-old attorney general and a former Air Force pilot. In the crowded first round of voting on June 16, Drummond finished first among nine candidates, but by a razor-thin margin of roughly 1,100 votes out of more than 403,000 cast—with both front-runners capturing about 26% of the vote.</p>

<p>The campaign has escalated into an expensive and deeply personal battle. President Donald Trump threw his weight behind Mazzei in May, repeatedly reaffirming his support on social media and launching sharp attacks against Drummond, labeling him a "FAKE Republican" due to past comments regarding Trump's 2021 impeachment inquiry. Meanwhile, Drummond has pointed to Mazzei's campaign ties to longtime Trump adviser Roger Stone, while both candidates have navigated voter frustrations with the outgoing Governor Kevin Stitt, whom Trump also criticized as a "RINO."</p>
