    <h3>Age-Appropriate Conversations</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>For Younger Children (Ages 5-9):</strong> Focus on the concept of "choices." Explain that elections are how people in a community decide who will help make rules. Keep it simple: "Today, people in Florida are voting to decide who they want to represent them."</li>
      <li><strong>For Tweens and Teens (Ages 10+):</strong> Encourage critical thinking. Ask them, "What do you think that candidate's main goal is?" or "How do you think this change will affect our local community?" This turns a stressful news cycle into a lesson on civic engagement.</li>
    </ul>
    
    <p>It is also important to model emotional regulation. If you find yourself frustrated by election results, take a breath before discussing them. Children look to us to see how to handle disappointment or victory with grace.</p>
