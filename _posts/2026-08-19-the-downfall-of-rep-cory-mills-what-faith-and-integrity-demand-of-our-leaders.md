    <section id="the-news">
        <h2>The Defeat of an Incumbent</h2>
        <p>In a significant political development, Republican Representative Cory Mills has lost his primary bid for Florida’s 7th District. Despite securing the endorsement of former President Donald Trump, Mills fell to challenger Ryan Elijah in a contest that saw nearly 80% of the vote tallied with Elijah leading 46% to 34%. This result marks a notable shift in Florida politics and highlights the vulnerabilities of incumbents facing serious ethical questions.</p>
        
        <p>The primary was defined not just by policy debates, but by the shadow of multiple investigations—including a House Ethics Committee probe into alleged sexual misconduct and campaign finance violations, alongside a Department of Justice investigation into his financial dealings. While Mills has consistently denied any wrongdoing, the weight of these allegations clearly resonated with the electorate, leading fellow Republicans to break ranks and support his challenger.</p>
