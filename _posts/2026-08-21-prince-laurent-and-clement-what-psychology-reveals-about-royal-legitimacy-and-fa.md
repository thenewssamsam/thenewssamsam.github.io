<h2>What Actually Happened</h2>
<p>According to recent reports from <em>Soir Mag</em> and <em>Town & Country</em>, Prince Laurent of Belgium—the younger brother of King Philippe—has officially recognized Clément Vandenkerckhove as his son. Speculation had swirled since Clément’s birth in 2000, stemming from his mother’s, actress and model Iris Vandenkerckhove, public ties to the Prince in the late 1990s.</p>

<p>Thanks to a 2015 royal decree stating that all children and grandchildren of King Albert II may use the title of Prince or Princess of Belgium along with the style of Royal Highness, Clément is now officially a prince. However, there are distinct boundaries: he receives no public funding, as financial allowances are strictly limited to the monarch and the heir to the throne, and he will undertake no official public duties.</p>
