    <h2>A Moment of Calm in the Trade Storm</h2>
    <p>President Donald Trump recently announced a last-minute pause on the 50% tariffs scheduled for  billion worth of Canadian imports. This three-day delay arrives just hours before the sanctions were set to hit everything from hockey sticks to essential medical tongue depressors. While negotiators in Washington and Ottawa scramble to finalize a deal, the announcement provides a much-needed breath of fresh air for households and businesses currently living in a state of high-stakes limbo.</p>

      <p><strong>Quick Take:</strong> The "wait-and-see" approach in trade relations is often more mentally taxing than the policy itself. While the tariffs are paused, the underlying uncertainty reminds us how much our daily peace of mind is tied to global economic stability.</p>

  <section id="wellness-lens">
    <h2>The Psychology of "Economic Limbo"</h2>
    <p>From a wellness perspective, the primary challenge here isn't just the potential for higher prices—it is the chronic uncertainty. When we read headlines about trade wars, our brains often shift into a state of "threat detection." This is a natural evolutionary response; we are wired to worry about resources and the future. When news changes by the hour, our nervous systems struggle to find a "baseline" of safety.</p>
