    <h2>What Happened: The New Maximum Pressure Campaign</h2>
    <p>President Donald Trump has announced what he terms the "most crushing economic operation ever taken against any country," setting off a high-stakes standoff aimed at completely isolating Tehran. This renewed "maximum pressure" campaign threatens sweeping penalties for any foreign nation or business attempting to throw Iran an economic lifeline.</p>
    
    <p>Yet, rather than capitulating, Tehran is digging in its heels. According to regional security analysts, the Iranian government is aggressively deepening its strategic, financial, and military ties with Beijing and Moscow. Central Bank of Iran Governor Abdolnaser Hemmati recently announced that Tehran will soon join the BRICS-backed New Development Bank, utilizing the economic bloc as a shield against Western financial dominance.</p>
