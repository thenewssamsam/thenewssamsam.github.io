<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened</h2>
<p>Hollywood and global audiences are mourning the loss of legendary actor Tim Curry, who passed away in Los Angeles at the age of 80, according to his longtime manager and friend Marcia Hurwitz. While an immediate cause of death was not disclosed, Curry had previously faced significant health challenges after suffering a major stroke in 2012.</p>

<p>Immediately following the announcement, tributes poured in from across the entertainment industry. Co-stars and admirers—including Carol Burnett, Susan Sarandon, Rob Schneider, Luke Evans, Michael McKean, Lesley Ann Warren, and Jennifer Tilly—shared deeply personal memories of a performer whose career spanned iconic roles in <em>The Rocky Horror Picture Show</em>, <em>Clue</em>, <em>Annie</em>, <em>Home Alone 2: Lost in New York</em>, and Stephen King’s <em>It</em>. Far beyond a simple retrospective, these tributes reveal a master craftsman whose fearless commitment to his art left an indelible mark on both his colleagues and millions of fans.</p>
</section>
