<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened?</h2>
<p>According to reports by Kurt Badenhausen on Yahoo Sports, LIV Golf has laid off the majority of its staff as leadership charts a so-called "2.0 version" of the tour. The sweeping workforce reduction represents a profound structural shift for the organization, which burst onto the global sports scene backed by immense financial backing from Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF).</p>

<p>While details surrounding the exact headcount reductions are emerging, the move signals that the high-spending, rapid-expansion startup phase has given way to a leaner, more cost-conscious operational model. For an enterprise that spent billions acquiring marquee talent, building out complex tournament logistics, and challenging the entrenched PGA Tour, this downsizing forces a hard look at the long-term economics of modern professional golf.</p>
</section>
