<p>Psychologically, human beings are wired for social cooperation. We rely on heuristic shortcuts—mental rules of thumb—to evaluate people quickly so we can function in daily life. When hiring or enlisting a caregiver, parents naturally want to believe the best. This desire often triggers <em>normalcy bias</em>, a mental state where people underestimate the possibility of disaster because nothing bad has happened yet.</p>
