    <h2 id="legal-rights-perspective">A Legal Rights Perspective: Understanding Campus Investigations and Conspiracy Charges</h2>
    <p>When multiple individuals are implicated in a campus distribution network, prosecutors frequently introduce conspiracy charges alongside direct drug offenses. From a legal standpoint, a criminal conspiracy charge does not require that a defendant personally executed every transaction. Under criminal law, a conspiracy exists when two or more people agree to commit a crime, and at least one person takes an overt act in furtherance of that agreement.</p>
