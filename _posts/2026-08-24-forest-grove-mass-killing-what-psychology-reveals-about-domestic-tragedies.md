Instead, they uncovered a horrific crime scene that left a community reeling. Five people and multiple domestic animals were found dead in what police later classified as a targeted mass killing near Forest Grove.</p>

    <p>The tragedy highlights a grim reality of modern violence: the most devastating acts often occur behind closed doors, rooted in personal relationships rather than random acts of terror. As investigators piece together the timeline, the incident forces us to confront uncomfortable questions about isolation, domestic dynamics, and the psychological breaking points that precede such devastation.</p>

    <h3>The Danger of Isolation and Normalcy Bias</h3>
    <p>Psychologists frequently point to extreme social isolation as a primary amplifier in domestic violence cases. When individuals detach from community support structures, their internal narratives go unchecked by reality. Grievances magnify, minor conflicts transform into existential threats, and the perpetrator's worldview becomes entirely insular.</p>
