  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened in Arundel</h2>
    <p>During an August event in Arundel, Maine, Republican gubernatorial nominee Bobby Charles told supporters that if elected, he would appeal to White House border czar Tom Homan to deploy ICE agents and U.S. Marshals to voting booths in the weeks leading up to the midterm elections. Audio of the remarks, initially reported by the <em>Bangor Daily News</em>, quickly drew intense scrutiny from state officials, legal experts, and political opponents.</p>

    <p>Charles later clarified his statements, emphasizing that his intention was to ensure robust communication between local election managers and federal authorities to prevent any potential illegality, violence, or intimidation at voting sites. His campaign reiterated that individuals legally entitled to vote should do so free from interference, while those not authorized should not participate.</p>
