<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened</h2>
<p>On Tuesday morning, August 19, 2026, Palm Beach County Clerk of the Circuit Court and Comptroller Michael Caruso was arrested following an ongoing investigation led by the Florida Attorney General's office, the Florida Department of Law Enforcement, and local authorities. According to charging documents released alongside the announcement, Caruso faces severe criminal charges, including kidnapping, lewd or lascivious molestation, lewd or lascivious exhibition, luring or enticing a child, and child abuse causing mental injury.</p>

<p>The swift legal action triggered an immediate political and administrative response. Hours after the arrest, Florida Governor Ron DeSantis suspended Caruso from office. Meanwhile, the Palm Beach County Clerk's office released a public statement acknowledging the notification of "serious criminal charges" while assuring residents and its 650 employees that daily court operations, public record services, and community commitments would continue uninterrupted.</p>
