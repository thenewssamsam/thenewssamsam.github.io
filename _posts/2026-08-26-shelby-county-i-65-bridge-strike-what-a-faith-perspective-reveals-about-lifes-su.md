<h2>What Happened on I-65</h2>
<p>Life can change in the span of a single heartbeat. On Tuesday, August 25, 2026, motorists traveling along Interstate 65 in Shelby County, Alabama, experienced a stark reminder of that fragility when an 18-wheeler hauling construction equipment struck the underside of the County Road 87 overpass at Exit 234.</p>

<p>According to reports from the Alabama Department of Transportation (ALDOT) and local officials, the severe impact sent concrete debris raining down onto moving vehicles below. One person sustained critical injuries after falling debris smashed through a car's windshield. Calera Fire Chief Sean Kendrick and Alabaster Police responded swiftly, shutting down the interstate and diverting traffic for hours while emergency crews cleared heavy debris and structural inspectors evaluated the 66-year-old bridge's integrity.</p>
