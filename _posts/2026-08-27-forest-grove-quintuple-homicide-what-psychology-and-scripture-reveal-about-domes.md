<section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
<h2>What Happened in Forest Grove</h2>
<p>The quiet community of Forest Grove, Oregon, was shaken to its core following a horrific act of violence. According to the Washington County Sheriff’s Office, 36-year-old Benjamin Charles Parker perpetrated a quintuple homicide, taking the lives of five people after a domestic dispute.</p>
<p>The victims included Parker’s girlfriend, Stella Jade Kimsey, along with Kimsey’s grandmother, mother, best friend, and the best friend’s partner. Officials stated that Parker and Kimsey had engaged in a fierce argument either Thursday night or Friday morning, which tragically escalated into mass murder. District Attorney Kevin Barton noted that domestic violence accounts for a staggering portion of local homicides—five out of eight in Washington County for the current year, mirroring similar statistics from the previous year.</p>
<p>Local advocates from the Domestic Violence Resource Center and the sheriff's office have emphasized that domestic abuse is frequently hidden behind closed doors and heavily underreported. Understanding how such violence manifests is vital for preventing future tragedies.</p>
