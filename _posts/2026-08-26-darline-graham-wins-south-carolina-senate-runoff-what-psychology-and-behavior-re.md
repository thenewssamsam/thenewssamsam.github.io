<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened in the South Carolina GOP Senate Runoff</h2>
<p>The political landscape in South Carolina shifted dramatically when long-serving Senator Lindsey Graham passed away unexpectedly in July. Governor Henry McMaster appointed Darline Graham—the late senator's sister, who previously led the state's Commission for the Blind and had no prior elected experience—to fill the temporary vacancy. This set up a high-stakes, two-week snap primary runoff against Representative Ralph Norman, a nine-year veteran of Congress and a member of the Freedom Caucus.</p>

One of the most potent mental shortcuts our brains use is the <em>familiarity heuristic</em>. When faced with a complex choice in a compressed two-week window, voters naturally gravitate toward what feels known and safe.</p>
