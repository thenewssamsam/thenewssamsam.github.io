<section id="what-happened">
<p>When an independent local band steps into the spotlight with a sophomore release, it represents far more than just a collection of new songs. It marks the culmination of countless hours in cramped rehearsal spaces, unpaid gigs, and quiet moments of self-doubt. Orlando-based rock quartet Walking Blue has officially captured that exact lightning in a bottle with their brand-new sophomore album, <em>Something Cosmic</em>, celebrating its arrival with a high-energy hometown release show at the legendary Will's Pub.</p>

<p>Composed of drummer James Thome, guitarists Jimmy Kildea and Lucca Colucciello, and bassist Sean Laing, Walking Blue has been steadily building underground momentum in Central Florida. With <em>Something Cosmic</em>, the band’s signature blend of emo and pop-punk has sharpened into something vibrant and urgent. Tracks like "Phases of the Moon," "Feel Alive," and the Stardust Video & Coffee nod "Stardust Spin Kick" showcase a band refusing to stay stagnant, proving that dedication to craft eventually pays off.</p>
</section>
