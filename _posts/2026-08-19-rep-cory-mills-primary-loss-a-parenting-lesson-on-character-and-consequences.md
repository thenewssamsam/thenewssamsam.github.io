  <section id="news-hook">
    <p>In a significant political upset, Republican Representative Cory Mills has lost his Florida primary election to challenger Ryan Elijah. While the political world focuses on the shifting dynamics of the 7th District, the story offers a stark, real-world opportunity for parents to discuss the vital importance of character, integrity, and accountability with their families.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The News: A Primary Defeat</h2>
    <p>NBC News projects that Cory Mills, despite holding the endorsement of Donald Trump, was defeated by Ryan Elijah after a campaign dogged by multiple investigations. These include a House Ethics Committee probe regarding allegations of sexual misconduct and campaign finance violations, as well as a separate Justice Department inquiry into his finances. Mills has consistently denied any wrongdoing.</p>
    <p>The loss marks Mills as the 11th House member to lose a primary this year, a notably high number. Prominent Florida Republicans, including Representatives Anna Paulina Luna and Mike Haridopolos, broke with party tradition to endorse Elijah, citing a need for "integrity, energy, and an unwavering commitment to the people."</p>

  <section id="how-to-respond">
    <h2>How Should Families Respond?</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>For Younger Children:</strong> Keep it simple. Use the concept of "doing the right thing because it's right, not because you're afraid of getting caught."</li>
      <li><strong>For Teens:</strong> Discuss the difference between a "mistake" and a "pattern." Ask them: "If someone looked at your actions over the last month, what would they say you value most?"</li>
      <li><strong>Model It:</strong> The most powerful lesson is our own behavior. If we are honest about our own mistakes, our children are far more likely to value honesty in themselves.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
