    <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: New Air Defense Supplies Arrive in Kyiv</h2>
    <p>During a meeting with Estonian President Alar Karis in Kyiv on August 25, President Zelenskyy announced that new military agreements reached during the Coalition of the Willing summit have begun bearing fruit. Alongside the Patriot interceptor missiles, Ukraine secured Crotale air defense systems and crucial AIM missiles designed to intercept incoming projectiles.</p>
    
    <p>The shipments arrive at a critical juncture. European partners, while pledging billions in aid—including targeted regional support from Belgium, Denmark, and Estonia, alongside Swedish funding for drone production—are facing strained inventories of PAC-2 and PAC-3 interceptors. As winter approaches, the race to secure adequate airspace protection highlights both international solidarity and the grim realities of prolonged conflict.</p>
