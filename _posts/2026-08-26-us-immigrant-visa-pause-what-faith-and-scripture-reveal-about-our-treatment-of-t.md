  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened: The 2026 US Immigrant Visa Freeze</h2>
    <p>In early August, the U.S. State Department quietly initiated a sweeping pause on all immigrant visa applications across embassies and consulates worldwide. According to confirmed reports, scheduled visa interviews were abruptly canceled, with applicants receiving notices that new dates would eventually be assigned following mandatory "in-depth training" for consular officers.</p>
    
    <p>The stated goal of this administrative overhaul is to ensure that every prospective immigrant is comprehensively and consistently evaluated under stricter "public charge" criteria. Officials want to guarantee that those entering the system will not rely on U.S. public benefits. This pause is part of a broader, aggressive executive strategy that also includes plans to revoke up to 200,000 legal asylum seeker visas originally granted for tourism or business, alongside heightened social media vetting and increased processing fees.</p>

Yet, despite ongoing legal battles, the immediate reality for thousands of lawful applicants is severe disruption.</p>
