  <section id="financial-impact">
    <h2>Financial Impact: What This Means for Portfolio Strategy</h2>
    <p>For everyday investors, the lesson from Berkshire's multibillion-dollar portfolio adjustment is about matching time horizons with capital intensity. Major technological transitions require heavy upfront asset accumulation. Alphabet is absorbing billions in near-term capex to build the infrastructure that will power enterprise AI agents and cloud computing for the next decade.</p>
    <p>If you maintain a 12-month horizon and believe that Google Cloud's massive backlog will convert to recognized revenue as guided by management, current price levels represent an attractive entry point. Conversely, if your strategy requires immediate free cash flow normalization before taking on risk, waiting for capex intensity to plateau may be more suitable.</p>
