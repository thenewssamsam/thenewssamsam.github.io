    <h2>What Happened in the Florida Primary?</h2>
    <p>In one of the most surprising outcomes of the 2026 primary cycle, Democratic Socialist of America member and state representative Angie Nixon defeated Alexander Vindman for the Democratic nomination for the US Senate in Florida. Nixon secured 56.1% of the vote compared to Vindman’s 43.9%, according to the Associated Press. She now moves on to face Republican Ashley Moody in November to serve the remaining two years of Marco Rubio's term.</p>
    
    <p>The race caught national attention largely due to the massive financial disparity between the two campaigns.
