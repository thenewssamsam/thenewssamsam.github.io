<h2>What Happened: A Massive Investment in Paris</h2>
<p>When French President Emmanuel Macron stepped forward to announce a staggering 6 billion euro (approximately US$7 billion) theme park project just outside Paris, it caught global entertainment and financial markets by surprise. Planned for Cergy-Pontoise—about 30 kilometers northwest of the capital—the development is slated to build three distinct theme parks on a scale not witnessed in the region since the arrival of Disneyland Paris decades ago.</p>

<p>According to reports from <em>The Japan Times</em> and the <em>BBC</em>, one of the parks is officially "expected to be themed on the <em>Dragon Ball</em> series," tapping into the enduring global phenomenon created by the late Akira Toriyama. The project was formally advanced during a high-profile two-state visit by Saudi Arabia's Crown Prince Mohammad bin Salman, during which leaders signed a memorandum of understanding.</p>
