  <h2 id="psychology-leadership">The Psychology of Institutional Transitions</h2>
  
  <p>Whenever a major public institution undergoes a leadership shift, it triggers a predictable psychological response among the public: a mixture of cautious optimism and underlying anxiety. In behavioral science, this phenomenon is closely tied to <strong>normalcy bias</strong>—our natural tendency to underestimate the likelihood of disruption, coupled with a deep desire for institutional stability.</p>
