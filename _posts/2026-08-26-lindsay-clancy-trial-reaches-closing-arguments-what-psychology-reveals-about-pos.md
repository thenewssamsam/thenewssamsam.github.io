    <h2>A Psychological Perspective on Severe Postpartum Illness</h2>
    <p>To the outside observer, acts committed during severe psychiatric crises defy rational comprehension. Society naturally seeks logical motives, malicious intent, or straightforward accountability. However, clinical psychology and forensic psychiatry deal in realms where normal cognitive frameworks have completely fractured.</p>

    <p>Postpartum psychosis is a rare, severe psychiatric emergency occurring in approximately one to two out of every 1,000 deliveries. Unlike the more common postpartum depression, psychosis involves a complete break with reality. It frequently features terrifying hallucinations, intense paranoia, disorganized thinking, and profound delusions. When an individual experiences command hallucinations or severe psychotic depression, their internal world is entirely divorced from objective truth.</p>
