    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">

    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      A striking historical parallel to the current deadlock can be found in the winter of 1916–1917, prior to major shifts in global alliances. When initial secret peace feelers failed and neither coalition secured a decisive battlefield breakthrough, both sides abandoned restraint. The calculation shifted from winning a swift tactical victory to breaking the opponent's domestic and infrastructural will through total economic and logistical pressure. When diplomatic channels dry up, leaders often resort to maximum force simply because they perceive any compromise as political suicide.
    </p>
