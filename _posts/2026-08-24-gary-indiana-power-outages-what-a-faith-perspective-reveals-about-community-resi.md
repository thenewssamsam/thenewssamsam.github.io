  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened in Gary, Indiana?</h2>
    <p>For 77-year-old Terry Dubois, the storm sounded like nothing she had ever encountered in her life. On August 11, a sudden wall of severe weather tore across the US Midwest, bringing destructive tornadoes and winds peaking at 99mph to Gary, Indiana. While the most ferocious part of the tempest lasted barely ten minutes, its consequences have dragged on for nearly two weeks.</p>
    <p>As of recent reports from the Northern Indiana Public Service Company (Nipsco), power has been restored to more than 345,000 customers. Yet nearly 30,000 households—many in the former industrial heartland of Gary—remained in the dark and without air conditioning as a brutal heat settled over the region. With local infrastructure buckling, residents have faced shuttered petrol stations, empty grocery shelves, and mounting frustration that culminated in a class-action lawsuit against the utility provider.</p>

In Gary, neighbors have shared harrowing accounts: wheelchair users crawling down unlit stairwells, elderly residents dependent on oxygen tanks sweating through humid nights, and patients like Jack Tipold forced to abandon dental surgery mid-procedure without access to proper pain relief.</p>
