  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened at the Kennedy Center?</h2>
    <p>In a striking development in an already bitter legal and political tug-of-war, legal representatives for the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts filed court papers on Monday warning that the historic venue may need to be entirely demolished. According to the filing, if the center cannot undergo extensive renovations—which have been championed by the Trump administration and fiercely contested by political opponents—the structure "will deteriorate further into an unsafe, decrepit structure that will be required to be taken down."</p>
    
    <p>The filing outlines that if demolition becomes unavoidable, difficult decisions would follow regarding what might replace the riverside landmark. Among the suggestions mentioned by some observers over the years is a large outdoor amphitheater overlooking the Potomac River. This latest legal salvo arrives on the heels of repeated votes by the center's board—which includes numerous Trump allies—to shutter the facility for years-long renovations and to inscribe President Donald J. Trump's name prominently onto the building's facade.</p>
