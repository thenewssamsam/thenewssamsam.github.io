<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened at the Spratt Issues Conference?</h2>
<p>The Spratt Issues Conference has served for more than a decade as an annual rallying point for South Carolina Democrats. This year's event in Florence drew significant attention, anchored by Pete Buttigieg’s keynote address focusing on grassroots engagement and electoral strategy ahead of the November 3 general election. Early voting for the state's election cycle is scheduled to run from October 19 through October 31.</p>

<p>During his remarks, Buttigieg emphasized the necessity of forging meaningful connections with voters in rural communities. Pointing to challenges such as rural hospital closures, wage stagnation, and local economic hurdles, he argued that Democrats must champion policies that place tangible resources back into the pockets of working families. Local leaders echoed these sentiments; Mattie Thomas of the Florence County Democratic Party underscored the ongoing drive to involve young voters—noting that roughly 7.6% of registered voters in Florence County are under 25—while attendees like Michelle Demery from Clarendon County stressed the long-term stakes for upcoming generations.</p>
