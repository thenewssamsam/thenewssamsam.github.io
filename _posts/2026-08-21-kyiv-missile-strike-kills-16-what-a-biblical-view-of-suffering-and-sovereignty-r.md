<h2 id="what-happened" style="color:#2c3e50;margin-top:32px;">What Happened: A Night of Terror in Kyiv</h2>
<p>The aerial assault that shook the Ukrainian capital and its surrounding region was sweeping and coordinated. According to local authorities and international reports, Russian forces unleashed scores of missiles and drones in a multi-hour nighttime blitz. Ukraine’s air force reported that hits were recorded at 28 locations across the country, striking industrial sites, logistics centers, and residential neighborhoods.</p>

<p>Moscow’s strategy has increasingly relied on exploiting Ukraine’s chronic shortage of advanced air defense interceptors, such as U.S.-made Patriot systems. With international stockpiles strained by concurrent global conflicts and years of sustained warfare, intercepting every incoming ballistic and cruise missile has become an overwhelming challenge. While Ukrainian intelligence estimates that Russia maintains a steady monthly production of over 200 missiles alongside deep stockpiles, Ukraine has countered with its own long-range drone strikes targeting oil facilities deep inside Russian territory. Yet, the heavy toll continues to be paid primarily by civilians caught in the crossfire.</p>
