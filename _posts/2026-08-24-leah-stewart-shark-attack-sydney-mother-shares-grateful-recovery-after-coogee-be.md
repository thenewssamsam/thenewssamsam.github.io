<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened at Coogee Beach</h2>
<p>Leah Stewart's harrowing encounter occurred during what should have been a peaceful morning swim at Coogee Beach in Sydney. Amid a concerning spate of shark attacks across Australia this year—including multiple incidents in a single 48-hour window in January and fatal encounters in Queensland and Western Australia—Stewart was suddenly targeted by a shark she described as a "monster."</p>

<p>Sustaining multiple severe bites to her arms and legs, Stewart was rushed to the hospital in critical condition. She spent over a week in an induced coma, undergoing numerous complex surgeries, including the amputation of one of her arms. Yet, in her recent public update after months of agonizing rehabilitation, Stewart focused not on the terror of the attack, but on the overwhelming wave of global support and her own remarkable physical and emotional progress.</p>
