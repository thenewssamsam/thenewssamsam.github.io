      What Happened in East Oakland?
    </h2>
    <p>
      On a Monday evening that began like any other, peace was abruptly shattered across several blocks in East Oakland, California. According to reports from the Oakland Police Department, a rapid succession of violent encounters unfolded just before 7:30 p.m., turning public parks and transit hubs into active crime scenes. 
    </p>
    <p>
      The sequence of violence began at Cesar Chavez Park on 38th Avenue, where the first victim was brutally stabbed and ultimately pronounced dead at the scene. As first responders and witnesses scrambled to comprehend the unfolding crisis, a second victim was discovered nearby at 38th Avenue and San Leandro Street. While emergency crews tended to that individual, another urgent call directed police to the Fruitvale BART station, where a third stabbing victim was located. The terror did not stop there; two additional victims were subsequently found near 39th Avenue and International Boulevard.
    </p>
