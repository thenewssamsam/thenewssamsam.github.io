    <h2>What Happened in the Hewlett Tragedy?</h2>
    <p>The routine rhythms of a Long Island family's life shattered in an instant on a Friday that was supposed to mark the beginning of joy.     <p>According to sources close to the ongoing police investigation, authorities believe Rahmani suffered an unexpected medical episode just moments before losing control of her vehicle. In a harrowing final act of awareness, she managed to dial 911 from inside the sinking vehicle. First responders rushed to the scene, but despite heroic rescue efforts, she tragically passed away on the way to the hospital. Investigators have confirmed that foul play is not suspected.</p>
