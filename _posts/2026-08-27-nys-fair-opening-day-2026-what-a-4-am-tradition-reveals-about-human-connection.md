<h2>What Happened at the 2026 NYS Fair</h2>
<p>The race to be first through the gates of the New York State Fair ended long before most people's alarm clocks rang. For Ron Delosh, 75, and Daisy St. Laurent, 73, the journey began at 4:05 a.m. at a Watertown McDonald's. Armed with coffee and sausage burritos, the couple made the pre-dawn drive south, pulling into the Pink Lot at 5:10 a.m. to unload their red electric scooters and secure their spot at Gate 1.</p>

<p>According to reports from Syracuse.com, Ron and Daisy had not claimed the coveted first-in-line position since 2023. With nearly four hours to wait before the gates swung open at 9 a.m., they had the area largely to themselves, settling comfortably into a rhythm they have maintained across decades of companionship.</p>

<p>This early-morning dedication is more than a quirky photo opportunity; it is a testament to a 33-year partnership and a deep affection for an annual regional milestone. While the fairgrounds have transformed over the decades—becoming cleaner and easier to navigate, as Ron notes—the fundamental appeal of experiencing the event together remains entirely unchanged.</p>
