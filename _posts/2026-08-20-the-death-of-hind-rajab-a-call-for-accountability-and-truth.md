    <section id="what-happened">
        <h2>The Admission of Responsibility</h2>
        <p>For over two years, the case of 5-year-old Hind Rajab has stood as a haunting symbol of the humanitarian toll in Gaza. After initial denials and claims that its forces were not present, the Israel Defense Forces (IDF) have now acknowledged that they opened fire on the vehicle carrying the young girl and her family in January 2024. This admission marks a significant shift in the military's posture, as they have now initiated a criminal investigation into the incident.</p>
        
        <p>The tragedy began when Hind and her family were traveling in northern Gaza. Caught in the crossfire, the young girl survived the initial barrage and spent hours on the phone with Palestinian paramedics, pleading for rescue. The subsequent death of both the family and the rescue team sent to aid them became a focal point for international outcry. Twelve days later, Hind’s body was recovered from a car riddled with bullet holes.</p>

Palestinian Ambassador to the UN Riyad Mansour echoed this sentiment, arguing that a truly credible investigation must lead to real consequences, not just administrative review.</p>
