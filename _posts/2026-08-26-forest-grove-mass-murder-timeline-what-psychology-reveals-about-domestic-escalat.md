<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: The Forest Grove Timeline</h2>
<p>Investigators with the Washington County Sheriff’s Office have reconstructed the harrowing sequence of events that unfolded near Forestgrove, Oregon, culminating in the murders of five family members and friends. The suspect, 36-year-old Benjamin Charles Parker, ultimately died of a self-inflicted gunshot wound hundreds of miles away in Enumclaw, Washington, after fleeing the scene.</p>

<p>The tragedy claimed the lives of five individuals: 85-year-old Mary Elizabeth Kimsey, 60-year-old Rowan Karla Kimsey, 26-year-old Stella Jade Kimsey (Parker’s girlfriend), 26-year-old Linsey Mae Haraldsen, and 27-year-old Alvin Raymond Chu. According to lead investigator Detective Mark Pavoni, the incident began with a major argument between Stella Kimsey and Parker late Thursday night or early Friday morning, which resulted in Stella demanding that Parker leave the property.</p>
