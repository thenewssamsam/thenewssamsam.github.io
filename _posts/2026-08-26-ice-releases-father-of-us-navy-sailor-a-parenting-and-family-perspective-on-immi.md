<h2>What Happened: The Arrest and Release of Luis Manuel Aviles Roa</h2>
<p>Luis Manuel Aviles Roa, a native of Nicaragua and the father of an active-duty U.S. Navy sailor, was released by Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) on Tuesday. His release came just days after his son, Joshua Aviles, shared a heartbreaking public plea that resonated nationwide. Joshua, serving his ninth month aboard the aircraft carrier USS Abraham Lincoln—a vessel that has recently set records for uninterrupted time at sea amid reported crew fatigue—learned of his father's detention while on active duty.</p>

<p>According to the Department of Homeland Security, Aviles Roa had entered the country illegally and was initially arrested on Saturday as part of broader federal immigration enforcement efforts. Joshua expressed the agonizing psychological toll of the situation in a widely shared social media post: <em>"I don't know how I can mentally continue working 12+ hour days knowing that my dad is somewhere, possibly being treated like a criminal."</em> While Katty Delgado, Joshua's half-sister, confirmed Tuesday afternoon that their father was free, the specific conditions of his release remain under review as media outlets await further comment from DHS.</p>
