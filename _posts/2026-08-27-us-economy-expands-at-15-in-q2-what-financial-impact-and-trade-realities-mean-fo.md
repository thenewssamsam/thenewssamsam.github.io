  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Second Quarter?</h2>
  <p>According to the Commerce Department's second estimate, Gross Domestic Product (GDP) — the nation's total output of goods and services — decelerated to a 1.5% annualized rate in the second quarter. This matched the government's initial estimate released earlier.</p>
  <p>However, headline numbers rarely tell the whole story. Delving deeper into the report reveals that consumer spending, which accounts for roughly 70% of all U.S. economic activity, actually accelerated to a healthy 3.4% annual clip, bouncing back sharply from the meager 0.5% pace recorded between January and March.</p>
