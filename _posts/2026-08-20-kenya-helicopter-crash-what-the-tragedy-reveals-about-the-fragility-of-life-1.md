    <h2>The Tragedy in Samburu</h2>
    <p>A routine scenic flight turned into a national tragedy this week in Kenya’s Samburu county. A Eurocopter EC130 B4, operated by Lady Lori Helicopters, crashed near Mount Ololokwe, resulting in the deaths of all seven people on board. Among the victims were Michele Sensi-Contugi, the director general of Ecuador’s Strategic Intelligence Center, and his wife, Stephany Hollihan. The remaining five passengers were American citizens, including respected NBCUniversal journalist José Alberto Suárez and Miami restaurateur Roger Duarte.</p>
