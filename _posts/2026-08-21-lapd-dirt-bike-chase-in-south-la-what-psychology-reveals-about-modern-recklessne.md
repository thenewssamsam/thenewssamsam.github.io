    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; color: #2d3748; border-bottom: 2px solid #edf2f7; padding-bottom: 8px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
      What Happened: The South LA Dirt Bike Pursuit
    </h2>
    <p>
      It began around 5:45 p.m. on a Wednesday evening near Dodger Stadium. According to reports from the Los Angeles Police Department and aerial coverage from NewsChopper4, a young man in his early 20s led officers on a chaotic, hourlong pursuit across surface streets aboard a dirt bike that had previously been reported stolen. 
    </p>
    <p>
      With roughly six LAPD cruiser units trailing behind and a police helicopter hovering overhead, the rider wove through dense traffic. News footage captured dangerous moments: traveling on the wrong side of the road, narrowly clipping a car's side mirror, and cutting recklessly through public spaces like Earvin "Magic" Johnson Park and Athens Park—one of which was actively populated by young football players practicing.
    </p>
    <p>
      The erratic journey featured bizarre moments, including a brief stop at a Huntington Park gas station on Alameda Street where the rider attempted to refuel before speeding off with part of the pump hose still attached. The high-stakes game of evasion finally concluded on 111th Street when the machine ran out of gas. Abandoning the stolen bike, the suspect sprinted through multiple backyards before attempting a last-ditch concealment inside a blue recycling bin. Officers apprehended him near 112th Street and Broadway around 6:45 p.m. He now faces felony evading and possession of a stolen vehicle charges, though miraculously, no injuries were reported.
    </p>
