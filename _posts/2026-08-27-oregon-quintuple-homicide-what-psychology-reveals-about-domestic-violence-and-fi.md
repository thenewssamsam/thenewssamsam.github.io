<h2>What Happened in Forest Grove</h2>
<p>According to the Washington County Sheriff Office, the systematic killings took place on a rural property near Forest Grove on a Saturday morning. Investigators identified the victims as 26-year-old Stella Kimsey, the suspect girlfriend; her 85-year-old grandmother, Mary Kimsey; her 60-year-old mother, Rowan Kimsey; Stella best friend, 26-year-old Linsey Haraldsen; and Linsey partner, 27-year-old Alvin Chu. All five victims were shot multiple times.</p>

<h2>A Psychological Perspective on Escalation and Control</h2>
<p>From a behavioral and psychological standpoint, crimes of this nature are rarely impulsive snaps; rather, they are often the catastrophic culmination of extreme narcissistic injury, loss of control, and a distorted sense of grievance. When an individual whose internal stability depends entirely on dominance and possession is confronted with a definitive rejection—such as being told to leave the home—the psychological reaction can trigger what clinicians describe as an extinction burst or severe narcissistic collapse.</p>
