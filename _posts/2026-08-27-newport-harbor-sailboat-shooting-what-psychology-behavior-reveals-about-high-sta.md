<p>Psychologists frequently point to the concept of <em>threat rigidity</em>, a behavioral phenomenon where individuals facing an overwhelming stressor narrow their cognitive focus entirely on immediate survival, discarding rational cost-benefit analysis. Instead of evaluating the long-term consequences of surrender versus resistance, the mind fixates on a catastrophic illusion of control.</p>
