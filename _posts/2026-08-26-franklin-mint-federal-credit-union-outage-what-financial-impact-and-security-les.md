  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened at Franklin Mint Federal Credit Union?</h2>
    <p>For members of the Pennsylvania-based institution, the trouble began over the weekend and persisted through Tuesday morning. Users trying to log into the Franklin Mint Federal Credit Union mobile app encountered persistent error messages, while others attempting to access web services discovered that certain digital domains were unexpectedly pointing to GoDaddy domain-for-sale listings. Records suggest those web links may have fallen under the control of an individual overseas.</p>
