  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Facts: What We Know</h2>
    <p>On the morning of the announcement, 14 individuals were charged in connection with a criminal narcotics operation spanning 2023 and 2024. The investigation specifically identified off-campus activities linked to two Greek organizations: Sigma Chi and Delta Upsilon. In response, Penn State immediately placed Delta Upsilon on interim suspension. The university has confirmed it is cooperating fully with law enforcement while launching its own internal investigation through the Office of Student Accountability and Conflict Response.</p>
