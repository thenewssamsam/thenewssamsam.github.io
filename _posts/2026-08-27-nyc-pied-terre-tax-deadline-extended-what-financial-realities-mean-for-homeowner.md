<h2>What Happened: The Tax Rollout and Extensions</h2>
<p>The administrative rollout of New York City's controversial luxury second-home tax has hit another speed bump. City Hall announced that the deadline for property owners to submit proof of primary residency has been extended for a second time, moving from late August to September 18, and now landing on <strong>October 6</strong>. The extension comes as new court filings reveal that over 1,200 property owners initially flagged for the tax are actually full-time city residents who should never have been notified.</p>

Mayor Zohran Mamdani defended the administration's actions, explaining that early access to 2025 state income tax filings—which typically become available in February—was restricted during the initial rollout, forcing the city to rely on a broad net of roughly 900,000 potentially impacted properties.</p>
