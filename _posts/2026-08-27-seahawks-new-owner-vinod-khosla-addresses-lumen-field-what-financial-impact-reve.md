    <h2>What Happened</h2>
    <p>The defending Super Bowl champion Seattle Seahawks officially have a new owner. Following a unanimous vote by NFL owners at a special meeting in Atlanta, a group led by billionaire tech investor Vinod Khosla purchased the franchise from the trust of the late Paul Allen for a staggering $9.612 billion. Among the many pressing questions facing the incoming ownership group, the future of Lumen Field—the team's home since 2002—took center stage after reports hinted at eventual replacement discussions. While Khosla stopped short of making absolute guarantees, his initial remarks pointed toward preservation of the iconic stadium and its passionate fanbase.</p>

Rumors raised earlier in the year by ESPN regarding the eventual need to replace or majorly overhaul the venue highlight a recurring financial reality: aging sports facilities require massive capital expenditures to remain competitive in luxury seating, digital connectivity, and event hosting capacity.</p>
