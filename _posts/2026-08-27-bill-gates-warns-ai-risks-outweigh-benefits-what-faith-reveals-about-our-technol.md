    <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: Bill Gates Calls for Emergency Brakes on AI</h2>
    
    <p>In a detailed essay titled <em>“The turbulent AI era is here. The choices we make now are critical,”</em> Bill Gates argued that the transition to artificial intelligence will be one of the most turbulent periods in human history. Speaking later with CNN's Anderson Cooper, Gates expressed shock that rigorous criteria and safety reviews for powerful new models are largely missing from corporate and government agendas.</p>

    <p>Gates highlighted several major dangers:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Widespread Unemployment:</strong> The potential for AI and advanced robotics to displace human labor faster than the workforce can adapt.</li>
      <li><strong>Security Threats:</strong> Escalating risks of sophisticated cyberattacks and bioterrorism enabled by autonomous models.</li>
      <li><strong>Social and Cognitive Harm:</strong> Negative impacts on children's development and the erosion of public trust in shared truth.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>To slow down the displacement of human workers, Gates proposed novel policy ideas, such as taxing AI or robots similarly to payroll taxes for human employees, and intentionally preserving certain roles exclusively for humans. Yet, he acknowledged the grim reality: geopolitical competition makes a coordinated global slowdown unlikely.</p>
