    <h1 style="font-size: 32px; font-weight: 800; color: #1a202c; line-height: 1.25; margin: 0 0 15px 0;">
      Cory Mills Defeat in Florida: What Faith and Stewardship Reveal About Power
    </h1>
    <p style="font-size: 18px; color: #4a5568; margin: 0; font-weight: 400;">
      When embattled Florida Republican Rep. Cory Mills lost his primary seat to challenger Ryan Elijah, it highlighted a timeless truth about earthly authority: character, public trust, and stewardship ultimately outweigh political brand recognition.
    </p>
  </header>

  <section id="what-happened" style="margin-bottom: 40px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; color: #2d3748; margin-bottom: 15px; border-left: 4px solid #3182ce; padding-left: 12px;">
      What Happened in Florida's 7th Congressional District?
    </h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      In a closely watched primary election in central Florida, incumbent Republican Representative Cory Mills lost his bid for a third term in the House of Representatives. According to race calls by The Associated Press, Mills was defeated by his primary challenger, former local television news anchor Ryan Elijah, who secured a decisive victory by about 12 percentage points. 
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      Mills entered the race carrying substantial political baggage. The House Ethics Committee launched an investigation earlier this year into serious allegations, including domestic abuse claims from a former girlfriend and potential campaign finance violations. While Mills has consistently denied any wrongdoing and maintained his innocence, the controversy clearly shifted the dynamics of the race among local voters.
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      Even though President Donald Trump had endorsed Mills earlier in the year, high-profile support began to fray as the primary approached, with Florida Governor Ron DeSantis publicly stating he neither endorsed nor supported the incumbent. Meanwhile, voters like Tiffany Davis expressed concerns that Mills had accumulated too much controversy to survive a general election against Democratic challenger Bale Dalton, a 24-year Navy veteran.
    </p>
  </section>

  <div style="background-color: #fffaf0; border-left: 4px solid #dd6b20; padding: 20px; margin: 30px 0; border-radius: 4px;" class="ncg-quote-box">
    <p style="font-size: 18px; font-style: italic; color: #7b341e; margin: 0; font-weight: 600;">
      "Political power and platform endorsements can open doors, but sustained public trust requires personal integrity and accountability before God and the community."
    </p>
  </div>

  <section id="the-weight-of-stewardship" style="margin-bottom: 40px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; color: #2d3748; margin-bottom: 15px; border-left: 4px solid #3182ce; padding-left: 12px;">
      The Biblical Lens: Stewardship, Trust, and Accountability
    </h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      When we examine political upheavals through the lens of Scripture, we are reminded that positions of leadership are not merely career milestones or entitlements; they are sacred trusts. In ancient times and modern republics alike, authority carries an inherent responsibility to act with justice, moral clarity, and upright character.
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      Voter sentiment in Florida's 7th District reflected a profound tension between tangible legislative delivery and personal integrity. Some constituents, like Patrick Austin, focused heavily on the federal funds Mills brought home and the practical issues he resolved. Others, however, felt that moral weight and personal conduct could no longer be overlooked. This mirrors a timeless biblical principle found in Luke 16:10 (ESV): <em>"One who is faithful in a very little is also faithful in much, and one who is dishonest in a very little is also dishonest in much."</em>
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      Scripture consistently teaches that leadership is tested not just by the grand achievements a person claims, but by the private and public integrity they maintain when trials arise. When allegations accumulate and public confidence fractures, the foundation of authority begins to crumble, regardless of partisan alliances or high-level endorsements.
    </p>

    <h2 style="font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; color: #2d3748; margin-bottom: 15px; border-left: 4px solid #3182ce; padding-left: 12px;">
      How Should Christians Respond to Political Scandals?
    </h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
      It is easy for believers to become cynical, disillusioned, or overly partisan when political leaders stumble. However, events like the primary defeat in Florida offer an opportunity to recalibrate our expectations and focus on constructive responses:
    </p>
    <ul style="margin-bottom: 15px; padding-left: 20px;">
      <li style="margin-bottom: 10px;"><strong>Prioritize Character Over Partisanship:</strong> Support leaders not merely based on party labels or convenient alliances, but on demonstrated honesty, humility, and respect for others.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 10px;"><strong>Practice Discernment Without Pride:</strong> Avoid moral superiority when public figures fall. Instead, recognize human frailty and pray for genuine repentance and healing for all involved parties.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 10px;"><strong>Engage Constructively in Communities:</strong> Real change often starts locally, where ordinary citizens foster accountability, civic health, and neighborly love in their own neighborhoods.</li>
