<section id="introduction">
<p>Florida voters are heading to the polls today in a pivotal primary that will shape the state's leadership for years to come. With term-limited Governor Ron DeSantis stepping down, a crowded field of 17 major-party candidates is vying for the nomination. Beyond the political rhetoric, this race offers a unique window into the professional backgrounds and career trajectories that define modern political leadership in the Sunshine State.</p>
</section>

<section id="what-happened">
<h2>The State of the Race</h2>
<p>The primary features 11 Republicans and six Democrats, though polling data from the James Madison Institute suggests two frontrunners have emerged: Republican U.S. Rep. Byron Donalds and former U.S. Rep. David Jolly. Donalds, heavily backed by former President Donald Trump, leads the GOP field with 49% support, while Jolly, who transitioned from Republican to Democrat in 2025, leads the Democratic primary with 47%.</p>
</section>

<h3>The Pivot Path: David Jolly</h3>
<p>David Jolly’s professional journey is vastly different. Having served as a Republican congressman before moving to the Democratic Party in 2025, his career demonstrates high levels of ideological flexibility. In the private sector, such a pivot might be viewed as "rebranding" or a "strategic shift in mission." In politics, it presents a unique challenge: proving authenticity to a new base while managing the professional baggage of his past affiliations.</p>

<h2>How Should Readers Respond?</h2>
<ul>
