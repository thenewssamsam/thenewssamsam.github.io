  <section class="lincoln-news-facts">
    <h2>What Happened on the USS Abraham Lincoln?</h2>
    <p>According to USNI News, the USS <em>Abraham Lincoln</em> carrier strike group, alongside its escorts and Carrier Air Wing 9, is currently making its long journey back to the West Coast from the Indian Ocean. Hitting 295 days at sea on September 12, the deployment trails only the monumental 326-day mission of the USS <em>Gerald R. Ford</em> earlier this year.</p>
    
    <p>Operating without traditional access to Middle East logistics hubs, the strike group spent nearly seven months anchored in the Arabian Sea supporting critical operations. The immense strain of stretched supply lines and unexpected extensions prompted understandable concerns and complaints from families back home regarding conditions aboard the vessel. Addressing these hardships, Acting Secretary of the Navy Hung Cao acknowledged the brutal reality of the mission, stating: <em>&ldquo;Deployments are hard. Combat operations make them harder. War is hell and normal routines are disrupted.&rdquo;</em></p>
