<p>According to reports from NBC News, the fresh incident unfolded just after 4 a.m. on a Tuesday at 32nd Street and Seventh Avenue. Local environmental protection officials inspected the nearby sewers, confirming there was no infrastructure damage, but issued stark warnings that entering storm drains and manholes is both illegal and extremely perilous due to noxious gases, rapid flooding, and unstable footing.</p>

The excitement captured in a ten-second TikTok clip bypasses the grim, toxic reality of what it actually means to wander through New York City's wastewater management networks.</p>
