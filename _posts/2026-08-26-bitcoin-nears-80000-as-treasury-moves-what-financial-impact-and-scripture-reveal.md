    <h2>What Happened: The $80,000 Breakthrough</h2>
    <p>Financial markets experienced a sharp jolt of volatility and optimism over the past week, sending shockwaves through Wall Street and digital asset exchanges alike. Bitcoin surged past the $80,000 threshold for the first time since late May, lifting the broader cryptocurrency market by roughly $500 billion in a matter of days. This sudden price explosion was not driven by retail hype alone, but by a major policy signal emanating directly from Washington.</p>
    
    <p>According to reports from CNBC and financial commentators, U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is preparing a dramatic intervention to tame soaring U.S. Treasury bond yields. Wall Street executives told Fox Business that Bessent intends to do "whatever it takes" to "put the fear of God" into bond vigilantes, potentially utilizing the Treasury’s near-$1 trillion General Account to fund aggressive bond purchases. For decentralized finance markets, this policy maneuver was interpreted as an effective return of quantitative easing, breathing fiery life into what analysts are officially declaring a brand-new crypto bull market.</p>
