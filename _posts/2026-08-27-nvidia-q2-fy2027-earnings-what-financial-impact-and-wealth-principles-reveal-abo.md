  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened: The Record-Breaking Numbers</h2>
    <p>In a report that sent ripples across global financial markets, NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) announced its financial results for the second quarter of fiscal 2027, posting a jaw-dropping revenue of $96.2 billion. This figure represents an 18% increase from the previous quarter and a staggering 106% surge compared to the same period a year ago.</p>
    
    <p>The primary driver behind this monumental financial leap remains NVIDIA’s Data Center segment, which brought in $89.0 billion—up 117% year-over-year. GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins held strong at an impressive 75.0%, while GAAP earnings per diluted share came in at $2.46, with non-GAAP earnings at $2.22 per share.</p>

    <div class="ncg-quote-box ncg-accent-box" style="border-left: 4px solid #10b981; background: #f9fafb; padding: 1.25rem; margin: 1.5rem 0; border-radius: 0.50rem;">
      <p style="margin: 0; font-style: italic; font-size: 1.1rem; color: #1f2937;">“AI has reached its inflection point. It’s doing useful work. Its tokens are productive and profitable. Now, compute is revenue.”</p>
      <p style="margin: 0.5rem 0 0 0; font-size: 0.9rem; font-weight: bold; color: #4b5563;">— Jensen Huang, Founder and CEO of NVIDIA</p>
    </div>

    <p>Beyond operating income surging to $63.7 billion under GAAP, NVIDIA demonstrated massive capital return to its investors, distributing approximately $26.0 billion through share repurchases and cash dividends during the quarter alone. The company also announced its upcoming quarterly cash dividend of $0.25 per share, scheduled for October 1, 2026.</p>
