<h2>What Actually Happened in Wisconsin?</h2>
<p>The legal saga centered around two specific social media posts published on March 27, 2025, during a heated election cycle for the Wisconsin Supreme Court. In his initial post, Elon Musk announced an upcoming appearance in the state, noting that attendance would be restricted to individuals who had voted in the judicial election. Crucially, the post added that he would "personally hand over two checks for a million dollars each in appreciation for you taking the time to vote."</p>

In statutory interpretation, prosecutors cannot rely on a general feeling of impropriety; they must prove every specific element of a crime beyond a reasonable doubt.</p>
