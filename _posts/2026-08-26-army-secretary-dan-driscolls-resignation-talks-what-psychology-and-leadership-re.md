    <h2 class="ncg-h2">The Psychology of High-Stakes Burnout</h2>
    <p class="ncg-p">When leaders at the level of Army Secretary contemplate abrupt departures, observers often look for deep political conspiracies or dramatic policy clashes. From a psychological standpoint, however, the reality is frequently far more mundane yet equally powerful: severe cognitive overload and burnout.</p>
    
    <div class="ncg-quote-box">
        "High-stakes leadership extracts a profound psychological toll, where the margin for error is zero and the pressure is relentless."
    </div>

    <p class="ncg-p">Psychologists often point to chronic decision fatigue as a primary driver behind abrupt exits in demanding roles. When every choice carries monumental national security implications, the human brain experiences a depletion of cognitive resources. Over time, the gap between the demands of the office and the individual's psychological recovery capacity widens until a breaking point is reached.</p>
