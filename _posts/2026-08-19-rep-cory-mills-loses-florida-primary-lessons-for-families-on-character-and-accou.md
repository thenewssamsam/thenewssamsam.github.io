    <section id="what-happened">
        <h2>What Happened?</h2>
        <p>Republican Representative Cory Mills has been defeated in Florida’s 7th District primary, according to NBC News projections. Despite receiving an endorsement from former President Donald Trump, Mills fell to challenger Ryan Elijah, a former television journalist. The loss comes as Mills faces ongoing scrutiny, including a House Ethics Committee probe into allegations of sexual misconduct and campaign finance violations, as well as a separate Justice Department investigation into his finances. Mills has consistently denied any wrongdoing.</p>
    </section>

When a public servant is dogged by investigations into their personal conduct and financial integrity, it forces a community—and by extension, families—to grapple with the definition of leadership.</p>
