Despite these operational wins, the company's stock experienced a downward correction on Thursday post-earnings. Analysts and market observers focused heavily on a deceleration in same-store sales growth for Walmart US compared to the explosive first quarter, creating an immediate friction point between actual performance and market valuation.</p>

For more than two years, Walmart operated at an elite level, consistently beating consensus estimates and driving elevated sales and profits. When a corporation performs exceptionally well for an extended period, Wall Street responds by raising estimates higher and higher until they reach an unsustainable threshold.</p>
