      What Happened in South Phoenix
    </h2>
    <p>

Fired and arrested following what Phoenix Police Chief Matt Giordano described as "egregious criminal conduct," both men now face multiple felony charges.

        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>Deliberate Collision:</strong> Striking the rear bumper of a Dodge Charger to force a stop.</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>Physical Assault and Tasing:</strong> Breaking windows, using a Taser on a passenger, and repeatedly punching the driver while demanding compliance.</li>
        <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>Concealment:</strong> Failing to turn on body cameras, file use-of-force reports, or notify supervisors.</li>
