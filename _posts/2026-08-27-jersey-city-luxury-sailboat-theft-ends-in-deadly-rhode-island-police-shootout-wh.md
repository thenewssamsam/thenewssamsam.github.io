    <h2>What Happened: From Jersey City to Newport</h2>
    <p>The sequence of events unfolded over several frantic days across the Northeast coastline. On Friday, August 22, around 11 p.m., a prized luxury sailboat vanished from the Liberty Harbor Marina in Jersey City, New Jersey. The vessel was no ordinary craft; it was a deeply cherished, custom-built passion project that its owner, Jim Bonomo, had spent ten meticulous years refining and caring for.</p>
    <p>According to investigators, the suspect—later identified as 27-year-old Brendan Norris—had been spotted acting unusually at a nearby marina business, Jersey Jet Ski, just prior to the theft. Once aboard the luxury vessel, Norris managed to disable the boat's transponder as he navigated past the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge, effectively blinding authorities and temporarily severing tracking capabilities. The disappearance triggered a massive alert stretching all the way up the East Coast to the United States Coast Guard.</p>
