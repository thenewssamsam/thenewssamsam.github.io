<h2>What Happened in the New Pennsylvania Poll?</h2>
<p>More than half of likely voters in Pennsylvania’s upcoming midterm elections disapprove of President Donald Trump's job performance, according to a striking new poll released Tuesday by The Inquirer, The New York Times, and Siena University. Trump, who narrowly carried the crucial swing state in November 2024 by nearly two percentage points, now finds himself drastically underwater with an approval rating of 39% and a disapproval rating of 58% among likely voters.</p>

<p>The survey of 760 likely Pennsylvania voters, conducted between August 17 and 21, highlights significant vulnerability for Republicans heading into November. Voters are casting doubt not only on the administration's handling of the economy—where 22% of Pennsylvanians cite it as their top issue—but also on foreign policy decisions. Notably, Trump's handling of the war in Iran draws a 66% disapproval rating, with roughly a third of his own 2024 supporters expressing dissatisfaction over rising oil and gas prices and military entanglement.</p>
