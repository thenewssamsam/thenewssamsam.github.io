<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: The White House Construction Dispute</h2>
<p>The controversy centers on above-ground and below-ground construction connected to the new East Wing of the White House, replacing a structure originally built in 1902 and substantially expanded under President Franklin D. Roosevelt in 1942. The National Trust for Historic Preservation previously challenged the construction plans, leading to lower court rulings—including an order from U.S. District Judge Richard Leon and a subsequent decision by the U.S. Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit—that imposed strict scheduling and design limitations on the project.</p>
