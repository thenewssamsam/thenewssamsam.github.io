<h2>What Happened: The Washington Pressure Campaign</h2>
<p>The geopolitical chessboard in the Middle East has shifted toward high-stakes economic warfare. Following a renewed bombing campaign at the end of February by US and Israeli forces, Washington has largely pivoted away from pure military objectives toward a strategy officially dubbed "Operation economic outcast."</p>

<p>US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced that nations continuing to conduct business with Iran face impending economic penalties from the Trump administration. With President Trump signaling personal phone calls to world leaders to clarify expectations, Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan sits squarely in the administrative crosshairs. Turkey shares a crucial border with Iran and maintains extensive commercial ties, making it a critical test case for Washington's maximum-pressure campaign.</p>
