<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: The Anthropic S-1 Filing</h2>
<p>On June 1st, Anthropic filed its initial registration documents with the SEC, signaling that its transition from a private research lab to a publicly traded entity is officially underway. While the exact date for the initial public offering has not been locked in, prediction markets and industry insiders suggest a window opening as early as October 2026. According to data from Polymarket, market participants price an 83% probability that Anthropic goes public by the end of October and a 93% chance of a debut before the close of 2026.</p>
