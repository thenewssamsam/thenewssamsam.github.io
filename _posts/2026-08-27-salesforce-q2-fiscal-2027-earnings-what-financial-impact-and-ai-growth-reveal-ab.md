<section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
  <h2>What Happened in Q2 FY27?</h2>
  <p>On August 26, 2026, Salesforce (NYSE: CRM) released its earnings report for the second quarter of fiscal 2027, covering the period ending July 31, 2026. Led by Chair and CEO Marc Benioff, the company reported total revenue of $11.3 billion, marking an 11% year-over-year increase. More importantly, the report highlighted accelerating momentum across current remaining performance obligations (cRPO), which climbed 14% year-over-year to hit $33.5 billion.</p>
  
  <p>Beyond traditional customer relationship management (CRM) metrics, the story of the quarter is the commercial maturation of enterprise AI. Salesforce announced that annualized recurring revenue (ARR) for Agentforce and Data 360 has approached $3.9 billion, surging over 210% year-over-year. With guidance raised for the full year and massive operating cash flows reported, Wall Street and corporate technology buyers alike are taking a hard look at what these numbers mean for the broader digital economy.</p>
</section>
