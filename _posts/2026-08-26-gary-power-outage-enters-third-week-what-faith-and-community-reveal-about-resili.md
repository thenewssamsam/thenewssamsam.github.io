  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened in Gary?</h2>
    <p>Severe summer storms battered Northwest Indiana on August 11, leaving widespread structural and electrical devastation in their wake. Weeks later, roughly 5,000 residences in and around Gary remain in the dark, struggling with spoiled food, mounting heat stress, and disrupted daily lives.</p>
    <p>Public outrage has largely centered on the Northern Indiana Public Service Company (NIPSCO), with local officials and residents criticizing the utility provider for what they describe as a profound lack of urgency and transparency. Pastor Dyke Lee of the First Tabernacle Missionary Baptist Church voiced a sentiment shared by many in the city, pointing to deferred infrastructure maintenance—such as clearing overgrown trees away from power lines—as a preventable contributor to the prolonged blackout.</p>

Inside the Tree of Life Missionary Baptist Church, members did not merely sit in despair; they surrounded Mayor Melton, laying hands on him in prayer and lifting their voices in song. This gathering highlights a profound spiritual truth: human institutions can crack, but human community rooted in faith provides an enduring anchor.</p>
