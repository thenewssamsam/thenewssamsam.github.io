    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; color: #2c3e50; margin-bottom: 15px; border-bottom: 2px solid #eaeaea; padding-bottom: 8px;">What Happened: The Hum Across the Street</h2>
    <p>For 52-year-old Krystal Polk, life on her family's two-acre property in Southaven, Mississippi, was defined by simplicity. Generations of her family raised chickens and pigs, grew herbs and shared peaches and pears with neighbors. But that quiet rhythm shattered roughly a year ago when heavy industry arrived directly across the street: a sprawling power plant designed to feed Elon Musk's SpaceXAI data centers in the Memphis area.</p>

Despite investments in sound walls and proposed equipment upgrades by the tech venture, local residents report little relief. Over decades, the land became a gathering space for family milestones, holidays, and celebrations.</p>
