    <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in Palm Beach County</h2>
    <p class="ncg-p">According to official reports from state authorities, Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier announced the arrest of Palm Beach County Clerk of the Circuit Court and Comptroller Michael Caruso following a coordinated effort involving the Florida Department of Law Enforcement and local law enforcement agencies. Charging documents outline severe allegations against Caruso, including kidnapping, lewd or lascivious molestation, lewd or lascivious exhibition, luring or enticing a child, and child abuse causing mental injury.</p>
    <p class="ncg-p">Within hours of the announcement, Governor Ron DeSantis formally suspended Caruso from office. The Governor’s office issued a brief statement confirming the suspension, while representatives for the Palm Beach County Clerk’s office moved quickly to reassure the public. The office emphasized that its 650 employees remain committed to maintaining seamless operations and serving community customers with professionalism, despite the unexpected leadership crisis.</p>

    <p class="ncg-p">The specific nature of the charges against Caruso touches upon one of the most serious moral transgressions addressed in both civic law and sacred text: the harm or exploitation of children. Throughout the teachings of Scripture, protecting children and the defenseless is framed as a sacred duty.</p>
    <p class="ncg-p">In the New Testament, Jesus expresses a severe warning regarding those who cause harm to the vulnerable, emphasizing the immense weight of protecting innocence:</p>
    <blockquote class="ncg-p" style="margin: 20px 0; padding-left: 20px; border-left: 3px solid #ccc; font-style: italic;">
      "If anyone causes one of these little ones—those who believe in me—to stumble, it would be better for them to have a large millstone hung around their neck and to be drowned in the depths of the sea." — Matthew 18:6 (NIV)
    </blockquote>
    <p class="ncg-p">This sobering reminder underscores that justice is not merely an institutional concept but a divine mandate. When legal systems act swiftly to investigate, suspend, and hold individuals accountable, it serves as an earthly echo of the moral order that demands protection for the innocent.</p>
