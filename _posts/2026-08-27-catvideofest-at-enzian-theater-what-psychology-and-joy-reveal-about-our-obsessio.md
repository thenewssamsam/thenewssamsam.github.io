Featuring acrobatic cats, heartwarming affection, and genuine animal antics entirely free of AI generation, the event offers more than just a communal laugh—it provides a fascinating window into human psychology and our deep-seated need for simple, shared joy.</p>

    <p>From the perspective of behavioral psychology, our collective obsession with cat videos is far from a trivial waste of time. Psychologists studying media consumption have noted that viewing cute animal clips triggers measurable drops in stress hormones and boosts dopamine and oxytocin—the brain's natural mood-elevating and bonding chemicals.</p>
