    <h2>What Happened at Penn State?</h2>
    <p>Pennsylvania Attorney General Dave Sunday recently announced that 14 individuals have been charged in connection with an alleged cocaine-trafficking operation centered at Penn State University. The investigation reveals a troubling web involving current and former students, with allegations that senior members of two fraternities—Delta Upsilon and Sigma Chi—orchestrated the distribution of narcotics. Perhaps most distressing is the claim that packaging these substances was used as a form of "indoctrination" for new pledges.</p>
