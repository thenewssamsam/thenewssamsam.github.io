    <section id="the-mechanics-of-control">
        <h2>The Health Costs of Constant Vigilance</h2>
        <p>Ossoff is described as being deeply involved in every word of his messaging. For any high-performer, maintaining this level of "always-on" control requires significant cognitive energy. In wellness terms, this is often referred to as managing "decision fatigue." When a candidate is personally involved in every detail—from letters to the Department of the Interior to the specific phrasing of a rally speech—the mental load is immense.</p>
        
        <p>While the goal is to maximize impact and avoid the "complacency" feared by his campaign team, the body and mind have limits. Chronic, high-intensity focus without adequate downtime can lead to burnout, even for those who appear most composed on camera.</p>
