    <h2 style="font-size: 1.6rem; font-weight: 700; color: #1a1a1a; border-left: 4px solid #8B4513; padding-left: 12px; margin-bottom: 20px;">What Happened in the US-Canada Tariff Standoff</h2>
    <p>President Donald Trump late Tuesday stepped back from the precipice of a sweeping economic collision, postponing a threatened 50% tariff on approximately $20 billion worth of Canadian goods. The levies, which would have hit products ranging from dairy and alcohol to industrial equipment, furniture, and plastics, were slated to take effect at midnight.</p>
    <p>Following intense phone negotiations between Trump and Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney, the administration announced a three-day pause, pushing the deadline to the end of the day on August 21. Speaking to reporters from the White House lawn on Wednesday morning, Trump declared that the two sides had come to a "deal" that is still awaiting formal finalization of documents.</p>

In the face of this immense pressure, Canadian officials, including Trade Minister Dominic LeBlanc, have continued collaborative efforts to reach a permanent resolution.</p>
