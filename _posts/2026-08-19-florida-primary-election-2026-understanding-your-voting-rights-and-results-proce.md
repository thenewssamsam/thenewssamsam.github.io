    <h3>Key Voter Protections</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Right to a Provisional Ballot:</strong> If your eligibility is challenged at the polls, you have the legal right to cast a provisional ballot, which will be reviewed by the canvassing board.</li>
      <li><strong>Assistance at the Polls:</strong> Voters who have a disability, are unable to read or write, or are blind have the right to request assistance from a person of their choice, provided that person is not their employer or union representative.</li>
      <li><strong>Privacy and Secrecy:</strong> Once you enter the voting booth, you are entitled to cast your vote in private, free from intimidation or interference.</li>
    </ul>
