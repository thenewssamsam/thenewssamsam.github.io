<h2>What Happened in Ohio</h2>
<p>Just as the San Francisco 49ers were gearing up for a high-stakes NFL season—celebrating the return of key players like George Kittle and Christian McCaffrey—off-field drama took center stage. Team owner Jed York found himself at the center of national sports news following an arrest in Ohio connected to an undercover prostitution sting.</p>

<p>According to court records, York contacted a fictitious online advertisement multiple times before traveling to a mobile home community where he arranged for sexual activity in exchange for $140. Law enforcement officers intervened, leading to York pleading no contest to two misdemeanor charges. While legal proceedings have largely concluded with the no-contest plea, the fallout is far from over. The NFL has announced that York will face a league review, casting a long shadow over the front office just as the team prepares for an international season opener in Australia.</p>
