    <h2>What Happened in the Appeal for Imran Khan?</h2>
    <p>In a rare and striking display of global solidarity, 21 former international cricket captains—including Indian icons Sunil Gavaskar, Kapil Dev, and Dilip Vengsarkar—have written a formal letter to Pakistan Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif. Initiated by former Australia captain Greg Chappell, the letter urges the Pakistan government to ensure proper medical treatment and welfare for the 73-year-old jailed former Prime Minister and World Cup-winning captain, Imran Khan.</p>

    <p>This marks the second major appeal by former international players seeking humane care for Khan, who has been imprisoned since 2023 and recently faced complications, including reported vision loss in his right eye. Just recently, Khan was briefly transferred to a private hospital in Islamabad for Supreme Court-ordered checks before being rushed back to jail hours later. Signatories to the letter read like a historic roll call of cricket royalty, featuring England's Michael Atherton and Nasser Hussain, Australia's Allan Border and Adam Gilchrist, and the West Indies' Clive Lloyd.</p>
    <p>The letter explicitly notes that the signatories are not attempting to interfere with Pakistan's domestic legal proceedings. Instead, they frame their plea around a fundamental standard of human decency: ensuring that court-ordered medical assessments and basic family visits are honored without administrative obstruction.</p>
