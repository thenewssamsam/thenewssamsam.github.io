<h2>What Happened: Malone's Departure and the OpenAI Exit Wave</h2>
<p>Chris Malone joined OpenAI in March 2025 as head of data centers, bringing with him a prestigious engineering background honed at both Meta and Google. In his role, Malone was tasked with helping oversee OpenAI's staggering infrastructure targets, which include plans to spend roughly $600 billion on compute power by 2030. However, after less than a year and a half, his tenure has come to an end.</p>

<h2>A Reflection on Stewardship and Ambition</h2>
<p>Whenever human organizations grow to immense proportions, striving to master the elements and reshape the world through sheer technological might, it prompts a timeless question about the limits of human planning. Proverbs 16:9 reminds us, <em>"In their hearts humans plan their course, but the Lord establishes their steps."</em></p>

<p>The frantic pace of modern tech development—building vast servers and chasing monumental valuations—often encourages a belief that sheer will and capital can conquer any logistical boundary. Yet, leadership changes and societal pushback remind us that human systems are inherently fragile. True wisdom lies not just in how fast we can scale, but in practicing genuine stewardship over the resources, communities, and people affected by our ambitions.</p>
