    <p>Florida Republican Rep. Cory Mills has been defeated in his bid for a third term, losing the 7th Congressional District primary to challenger Ryan Elijah. In an election cycle marked by scandal, Mills' loss serves as a modern case study on the limits of incumbency when faced with mounting ethical allegations and eroding party support.</p>

  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Primary Results</h2>
    <p>The Associated Press called the race for Ryan Elijah, a former local television news anchor, who secured a 12-percentage-point victory over the incumbent. The loss follows a tumultuous period for Mills, who has been the subject of a House Ethics Committee investigation regarding allegations of domestic abuse and campaign finance violations. Despite his initial standing in the district, the accumulation of "baggage"—a term used by local voters—proved too heavy for the incumbent to overcome.</p>
