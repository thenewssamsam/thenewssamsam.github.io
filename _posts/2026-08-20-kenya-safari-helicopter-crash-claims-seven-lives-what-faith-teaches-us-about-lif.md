<section id="what-happened">
  <h2>What Happened in Northern Kenya</h2>
  <p>According to the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority and local police reports, the Eurocopter EC130 B4 was operating a charter flight for travel company &Beyond, traveling from the Loisaba Conservancy toward the Ewaso Nyiro area in Samburu County. At approximately 9:13 a.m., the aircraft went down in a rugged, remote landscape near Mount Ololokwe—a popular destination for tourists seeking the breathtaking vistas of East Africa.</p>
  
  <p>Local emergency response teams, including the Kenya Red Cross and Tropic Air Kenya, immediately dispatched rescue helicopters to the site. However, efforts were severely hampered by difficult terrain access and an active fire at the wreckage. Tragically, authorities confirmed that none of the seven occupants survived.</p>
