  <p>For a time, according to authorities, <strong>Agostino Abbatiello</strong> wasn't just another face in the lecture halls at Penn State. The accounting major and fraternity brother is accused of operating what investigators allege was the largest drug distribution scheme on the university's campus. The case has sent shockwaves through the university community, transforming a familiar picture of college life—fraternities, majors, and campus landmarks like Old Main—into a sobering headline about illicit drug networks.</p>
  <p>While law enforcement details continue to emerge regarding the scale of the alleged operation, the rapid descent from a typical undergraduate routine to a federal or state-level criminal case raises difficult questions.
