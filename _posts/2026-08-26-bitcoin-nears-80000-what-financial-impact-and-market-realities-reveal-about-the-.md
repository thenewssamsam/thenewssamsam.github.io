<h2>What Happened: Inside the Bitcoin Breakout</h2>
<p>The cryptocurrency market experienced a dramatic shift when Bitcoin briefly surged past the $81,000 threshold, extending a powerful rally that began with a 20% gain in just three days. While the flagship asset settled slightly lower—hovering near $79,287—the momentum shattered weeks of sluggish trading and a prolonged market slump that had persisted since October.</p>
<p>This wasn't merely a casual walk upward. CNBC reports that the explosive leg higher was initially catalyzed by a massive short squeeze, wiping out over $4 billion in bearish crypto positions as prices climbed. Yet, unlike previous flash-in-the-pan bounces that quickly evaporated, this movement has caught the attention of institutional analysts due to the underlying liquidity and capital architecture supporting it.</p>
