  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened in the Oil Markets</h2>
    <p>Global energy markets experienced a notable downward correction as geopolitical tensions in the Middle East shifted away from active military engagement toward economic statecraft. International benchmark Brent futures dropped 3.9% to close at $88.58 per barrel, while U.S. West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude declined 3.1% to settle at $82.36 a barrel. This cumulative drop of more than 5% over the week followed the White House unveiling a fresh wave of sanctions targeting Iran and international entities facilitating its petroleum trade.</p>
