  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened at Dar Es Salam?</h2>
    <p>In the early hours of Sunday, the Dar Es Salam landfill in Guinea's capital city of Conakry suffered a catastrophic structural failure. Months of seasonal downpours culminated in an overwhelming surge of moisture, destabilizing a massive, towering mound of accumulated garbage. Without warning, the artificial mountain gave way, surging directly into adjacent informal residential areas while families slept.</p>
    
    <p>Emergency responders and military engineering units rushed to the scene, deploying heavy excavators to dig through tons of putrid waste in a desperate search for survivors. Alongside the 30 confirmed fatalities, at least six individuals sustained serious injuries. Local authorities confirmed that the site had long exceeded safe operational limits and was actually slated for permanent closure on the very day the disaster struck. Government officials noted that prior efforts had been made to clear residents from informal housing surrounding the perimeter due to known collapse risks, but the rapid growth of urban poverty often outpaces relocation initiatives.</p>
