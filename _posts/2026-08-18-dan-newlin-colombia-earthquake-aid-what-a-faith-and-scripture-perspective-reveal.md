    <h1>Dan Newlin Sends Aid from Central Florida to Colombia After Deadly Earthquake</h1>
    <div class="ncg-meta">

  <p>When a staggering 7.4-magnitude earthquake struck Colombia, leaving nearly 300 people dead and overwhelming local hospitals, the devastation crossed borders and touched hearts thousands of miles away in Central Florida. Disasters on this scale naturally leave us searching for hope amidst the rubble—and occasionally, they draw an immediate, practical response from unexpected places.</p>

  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in Colombia?</h2>
  <p>Rescuers in Colombia are continuing their painstaking search through rubble one week after a powerful 7.4-magnitude earthquake rocked the country. As the death toll approaches 300 and countless others remain injured, the nation's healthcare infrastructure has been pushed to a breaking point.</p>

  <p>Amidst the chaos, local Central Florida attorney Dan Newlin stepped in with a massive relief effort. Just 12 hours before the earthquake hit, Newlin happened to be in Cali, Colombia, attending the country's presidential inauguration, where he connected with the new president and his wife. When disaster struck, Colombia's first lady personally reached out to Newlin for assistance.</p>
  <p>Answering the call without hesitation, Newlin coordinated with AdventHealth, which quickly delivered a truck filled with supplies. On Monday, two planes chartered by Newlin transported over 5,000 pounds of critical medical supplies directly to Bogotá. According to Newlin, local hospitals were desperately "starving for medication" and unable to perform basic surgeries due to a lack of proper equipment. Now, those critical items have reached the frontline medical workers who need them most.</p>

  <h2 id="a-faith-perspective">A Faith and Scripture Perspective: The Promptness of Compassion</h2>
  <p>When we witness widespread suffering, the human heart naturally asks: How should we respond when tragedy strikes our global neighbors? Scripture repeatedly calls believers to look beyond their immediate circles and extend grace and practical help to the vulnerable.</p>

  <div class="ncg-verse-box">
    <p>&ldquo;Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due, when it is in your power to act. Do not say to your neighbor, 'Come back tomorrow and I'll give it to you'—when you already have it with you.&rdquo;</p>
    <cite>Proverbs 3:27-28 (NIV)</cite>
  </div>

  <p>Newlin’s immediate reaction—telling the first lady, <em>"Just give us a list, and I'll make it happen"</em>—mirrors the biblical encouragement toward active stewardship. True compassion is rarely passive; it looks for a tangible need and moves quickly to fill it. In a broken world prone to disaster and sudden sorrow, acts of swift solidarity remind us of our shared human dignity and the profound responsibility we have to care for one another across geographic divides.</p>

  <div class="ncg-quote-box">
    &ldquo;True compassion does not wait for convenience; it listens to the cry of a neighbor in crisis and moves immediately to restore hope.&rdquo;
  </div>

  <h2 id="how-should-christians-respond">How Should Christians Respond?</h2>
  <p>While most of us may not have private planes or the immediate ability to ship thousands of pounds of medical cargo across continents, every person can participate in the work of relief and comfort during global crises:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Offer Focused Prayer:</strong> Lift up the grieving families, the injured, and the tireless rescue workers operating in harrowing conditions in Colombia.</li>
    <li><strong>Support Vetted Relief Organizations:</strong> Look for reputable medical and humanitarian organizations operating on the ground that specialize in delivering rapid emergency aid.</li>
    <li><strong>Practice Readiness in Our Own Communities:</strong> Cultivate a lifestyle of preparedness and generosity so that when local or global needs arise, you are ready to give of your time, resources, or finances without hesitation.</li>
  </ul>
