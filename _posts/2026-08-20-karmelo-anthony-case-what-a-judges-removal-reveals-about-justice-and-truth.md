    <section id="what-happened">
        <h2 style="font-size: 1.6rem; color: #222; margin-top: 30px; border-bottom: 1px solid #eee; padding-bottom: 8px;">What Happened: The Recusal Ruling</h2>
        <p>In a dramatic courtroom development in Collin County, Texas, Judge Sid Harle granted a defense motion to remove Judge John Roach from any further matters concerning 19-year-old Karmelo Anthony. Anthony, who was convicted and sentenced to 35 years in prison in June for the fatal stabbing of 17-year-old Austin Metcalf, is currently seeking a new trial.</p>
        
        <p>The defense argued successfully that Judge Roach's post-trial public comments and media interviews—in which he defended his strict courtroom rules and appeared to endorse the jury's guilty verdict while a motion for a new trial was still pending—created a reasonable question regarding his judicial impartiality. When Judge Harle delivered his ruling, supporters in the courtroom reacted with audible cheers and tears, highlighting the deep emotional currents surrounding the case.</p>
