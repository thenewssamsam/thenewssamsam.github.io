    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; color: #2d3748; border-left: 4px solid #b7791f; padding-left: 12px; margin-bottom: 20px;">What Happened in the Himalayas?</h2>
    <p>When the mountains themselves give way, the speed of the disaster leaves little room for warning. A catastrophic flash flood, triggered by a sudden glacial collapse along the Nepal-Tibet border, has unleashed unimaginable devastation across the Himalayan region. More than 1,000 people have now been declared missing, according to rescue coordinators and disaster response agencies working across international lines.</p>
    
    <p>Nepal's disaster agency reported that at least 823 individuals remained out of contact more than 24 hours after the initial wall of water tore through valleys. Among those missing are at least 579 tourists—both domestic travelers and foreign visitors drawn to the majestic scenery of the region. Across the border in China, authorities confirmed at least 165 deaths, with a staggering 558 additional people reported missing. Officials continue to cross-reference data to ensure counts do not overlap, but the sheer scale of the emergency has overwhelmed local infrastructure.</p>
