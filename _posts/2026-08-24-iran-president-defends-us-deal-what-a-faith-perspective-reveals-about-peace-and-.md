<h2>What Happened: The Stalled US-Iran Agreement</h2>
<p>Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian publicly defended an expired Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) struck with the United States, describing the ongoing hostility between Washington and Tehran as an exhausting state of being "neither war nor peace." According to state-run reports, the country's Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) endorsed the assessment through the lens of national interest and dignity, noting that the text contains no provisions amounting to capitulation.</p>
