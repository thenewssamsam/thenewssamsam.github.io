<section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened: Central Magnet Takes the Crown</h2>
    <p>The latest education rankings released by <em>U.S. News & World Report</em> evaluate thousands of public high schools across the nation based on metrics including state assessment performance, graduation rates, and college-readiness. Rising above formidable competition, Central Magnet School in Rutherford County captured the coveted No. 1 spot.</p>
    <p>Operating since 2010, Central Magnet has a well-documented history of academic achievement, frequently appearing near the top of state and national lists. District officials, including Rutherford County Schools Director Jimmy Sullivan and Principal Dr. Clark Blair, quickly praised the collective effort of students, faculty, and parents. Beyond Central's success, the broader Rutherford County district also showed strong overall performance, with eight local high schools placing in the top 25% statewide out of 440 institutions.</p>
