<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened: The 2028 Trial Date</h2>
<p>On Wednesday, US Air Force judge Lt Col Michael A Schrama ruled that the delayed military tribunal for Khalid Sheikh Mohammed and three co-accused—Walid bin Attash, Ali Abdul Aziz Ali, and Mustafa al-Hawsawi—will finally commence on June 5, 2028. The timeline allows prosecutors, defense attorneys, and the military court system additional months to resolve complex evidentiary disputes and motions.</p>

<p>Captured in Pakistan in March 2003 and held at the Guantanamo Bay detention facility since September 2006, Mohammed has spent over two decades in US custody. The path to this trial date has been fraught with legal detours, most notably the unraveling of a negotiated plea agreement last summer. That deal would have traded a guilty plea for a life sentence instead of the death penalty, but a federal appeals court ultimately threw it out after high-level friction within the Department of Defense.</p>
