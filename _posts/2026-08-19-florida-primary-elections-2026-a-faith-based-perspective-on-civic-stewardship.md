        <p>The 2026 primary election in Florida has finalized the candidate lists for key positions, including seats in the U.S. Senate and House of Representatives, as well as the Florida Governor's office. According to the Florida Division of Elections and Associated Press reporting, voters in counties ranging from Orange to Volusia have cast their ballots, narrowing the field for the upcoming general election. This process is a fundamental aspect of the American democratic system, providing an opportunity for citizens to select the representatives who will advocate for their values and policies in Tallahassee and Washington, D.C.</p>
