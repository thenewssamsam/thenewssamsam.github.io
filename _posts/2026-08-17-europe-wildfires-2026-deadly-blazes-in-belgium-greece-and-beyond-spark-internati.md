    <h1>Europe Wildfires 2026: Deadly Blazes in Belgium, Greece, and Beyond Spark International Response</h1>
    <div class="na-meta-bar">
      <span>By <strong>Editorial Team</strong></span>
      <span><time datetime="2026-08-09">Published: August 9, 2026</time></span>
      <span>Estimated reading time: 6 minutes</span>

  <section>
    <p>A severe wave of summer heat and prolonged dry conditions has ignited historic wildfires across Europe, prompting two fatalities on a Greek island, massive evacuations in Belgium, and a coordinated cross-border emergency response as of August 2026.</p>

    <div class="na-quick-take">
      <h3>Quick Take</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Belgium:</strong> The High Fens nature reserve suffered its worst recorded wildfire, burning roughly 12 square miles and forcing hundreds to evacuate near the German border.</li>
        <li><strong>Greece:</strong> Two people died in Peristeria on the island of Salamina as strong winds rapidly accelerated a wildfire toward residential areas.</li>
        <li><strong>International Aid:</strong> Belgium activated the European Union civil protection mechanism, drawing firefighting aircraft and crews from Sweden, the Netherlands, the Czech Republic, and Germany.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: Destructive Fires Strike Belgium and Greece</h2>
    <p>Firefighters faced relentless conditions over the weekend as extreme heat and drought fueled fast-moving blazes in multiple European nations <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — NBC News]</strong>.</p>
    <p>In eastern Belgium, a massive wildfire scorched approximately 12 square miles of the High Fens, a prominent nature reserve <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — European Forest Fire Information System]</strong>. Approximately 600 residents in Liège province near the German border received evacuation orders as shifting winds pushed thick smoke across the municipalities of Waimes and Bütgenbach <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — Local Authorities]</strong>.</p>
    <p>Simultaneously, tragedy struck in Greece, where two individuals died during a wildfire on the island of Salamina, west of Athens <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — Greek Firefighters]</strong>. First responders recovered the bodies in Peristeria, while authorities evacuated beachgoers from Selini using firefighting and coast guard vessels <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — Hellenic Coast Guard]</strong>.</p>

    <blockquote class="na-pullquote">
      "We’re dealing with something much deeper because we have very dry soil." — Nicolas Yernaux, Public Service of Wallonia
    </blockquote>

    <h2 id="background">Background: How Dry Soil and Heat Waves Fueled the Crisis</h2>
    <p>The severity of the current blazes stems from cumulative environmental factors. Weeks of unusually high temperatures—reaching up to 37°C (98.6°F) in parts of Belgium—dried out soil and vegetation <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — Meteo Reports]</strong>. In the U.K., temperatures climbed to 38.1°C (100.6°F), triggering widespread drought alerts <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — UK Met Office]</strong>.</p>
    <p>In Belgium, the High Fens fire easily surpassed a previous record set in 2011, which burned nearly 5½ square miles <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — European Forest Fire Information System]</strong>. Officials noted that the unique composition of heathland and peat bogs in the reserve created deep subterranean burning conditions that are exceptionally difficult for ground crews to tackle directly <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — Department of Nature and Forest]</strong>.</p>

    <h2 id="international-response">Cross-Border Cooperation and EU Civil Protection</h2>
    <p>With domestic resources stretched thin, European nations turned to cooperative defense mechanisms. Belgium activated the European Union’s civil protection mechanism, according to E.U. Crisis Management Commissioner Hadja Lahbib <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — Hadja Lahbib via X]</strong>.</p>
    <p>The international deployment included:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Sweden:</strong> Dispatched two specialized water-bombing aircraft <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — EU Civil Protection]</strong>.</li>
      <li><strong>The Netherlands & Czech Republic:</strong> Deployed a combined total of three firefighting helicopters <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — Emergency Services]</strong>.</li>
      <li><strong>Germany:</strong> Contributed over 100 firefighters to assist Belgian crews along the shared border, while local farmers utilized tanker trucks to support suppression efforts <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — Liège Provincial Governor Hervé Jamar]</strong>.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 id="regional-impacts">Wider Regional Impact: France and the United Kingdom</h2>
    <p>The crisis was not isolated to Belgium and Greece. In southwestern France, authorities monitored a 6½-square-mile fire in the Landes pine forest region after approximately 650 people evacuated their homes <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — Landes Prefect Gilles Clavreul]</strong>. This followed earlier devastating summer blazes in Gironde that scorched roughly 160 square miles <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — Local Authorities]</strong>.</p>
    <p>Meanwhile, in South Wales, emergency crews combated 14 simultaneous wildfires with military support, while fires in the English West Midlands destroyed more than a dozen homes <strong class="na-claim">[Confirmed — UK Emergency Services]</strong>.</p>

    <h2 id="what-happens-next">What Happens Next: Future Scenarios and Mitigation</h2>
    <p>Meteorological forecasts suggest that while acute heat waves may ease temporarily in certain regions, underlying drought conditions will maintain elevated wildfire risks <strong class="na-claim">[Analysis]</strong>. Plausible near-term developments include:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Continued Regional Bans:</strong> Authorities across Western Europe are expected to maintain strict outdoor burning bans and tourist restrictions in high-risk nature reserves <strong class="na-claim">[Analysis]</strong>.</li>
      <li><strong>Enhanced EU Resource Sharing:</strong> As climate volatility increases the frequency of multi-national drought events, reliance on centralized EU aerial fleets will likely expand <strong class="na-claim">[Analysis]</strong>.</li>
    </ul>
