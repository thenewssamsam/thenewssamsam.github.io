<h2>What Happened: The Mystery Over Vnukovo Airport</h2>
<p>When a heavy transport aircraft touches down in a rival capital while officials offer conflicting accounts, the world naturally pauses to watch. Flight data from Flightradar24 revealed that a Boeing C-17A Globemaster III departed Joint Base Andrews in Maryland, transited through Riga, Latvia, and ultimately landed at Moscow's Vnukovo airport. Yet, despite the undeniable digital breadcrumbs left in the sky, the Kremlin publicly claimed it had no information regarding the flight.</p>

<p>The Department of War offered a characteristic "nothing to provide on this subject," while inquiries to the White House and the U.S. Embassy in Moscow left reporters with more questions than answers. The last time a direct U.S.-registered jet made such a high-profile journey to Russia was in January, when special envoy Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met with Russian President Vladimir Putin to discuss the war in Ukraine. This latest arrival of a military-grade transport plane has sparked intense speculation about what high-level maneuvering might be happening behind closed doors.</p>
