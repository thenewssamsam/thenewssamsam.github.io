    <h3 style="font-size: 1.3rem; font-weight: 600; color: #2d3748; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 15px;">1. The Fatigue of Perpetual Urgency and Normalcy Bias</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 20px;">
      Normalcy bias is a mental state that causes people to underestimate the possibility of a disaster or significant change. Conversely, when political rhetoric maintains a constant pitch of emergency over years, citizens eventually experience adaptation fatigue. Voters begin seeking stability and predictable governance over disruptive loyalty tests. When a candidate's primary pitch relies entirely on external association rather than local problem-solving, the psychological appeal begins to wane.
    </p>
