<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Penn State Drug Probe?</h2>

<p>The unfolding investigation led by the Pennsylvania Office of Attorney General has painted a troubling picture of campus drug distribution and organizational culture. Attorney General Dave Sunday announced charges against 14 suspects, noting that 13 of them were Penn State students during 2023 and 2024. Investigators revealed that the packaging of cocaine frequently took place off-campus in fraternity houses, specifically pointing to an alarming pattern where cutting and bagging drugs served as an indoctrination rite for pledges within the Delta Upsilon and Sigma Chi fraternities.</p>
