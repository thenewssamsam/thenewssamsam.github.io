<section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The Shot Heard Round New York</h2>
    <p>When OG Anunoby’s game-winning tip-in dropped through the net during Game 4 of the 2026 NBA Finals, it didn't just cap off an impossible 29-point comeback against the San Antonio Spurs. It shattered a agonizing 53-year championship drought for the New York Knicks. Overnight, the quiet, unflappable forward found himself at the center of an ecstatic, hysterical sports metropolis, fielding everything from late-night TV appearances to getting the key to the city from Mayor Zohran Mamdani.</p>
    
    <p>Yet, weeks later, lounging in a black hoodie during a promotional circuit in New York, Anunoby gave an interview with CNN Sports that surprised many. When asked about his legendary heroics—a moment so improbable fans jokingly dubbed it the "Hand of God"—Anunoby admitted he was already "over" it. Not because he doesn't appreciate the victory, but because he is firmly focused on the upcoming season and defending their hard-earned crown. In a culture that loves to marinate indefinitely in fleeting applause, Anunoby's refusal to be consumed by his own hype offers a fascinating lens through which to examine human ambition, success, and spiritual grounding.</p>
</section>

<section id="how-should-christians-respond">
    <h2>How Should Christians Respond?</h2>
    <p>We do not need to play in Madison Square Garden or win an NBA title to face the temptations of success and recognition. Every day, believers experience micro-victories in the workplace, at home, or in ministry. How should we handle those moments when the spotlight turns our way?</p>
    
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Practice Radical Gratitude Without Entitlement:</strong> Recognize that talents, opportunities, and successes are temporary stewardships, not inherent birthrights.</li>
        <li><strong>Keep Moving Forward:</strong> Do not let past successes become spiritual or professional parking lots. Celebrate the milestone, thank God for His provision, and refocus on the duties of today.</li>
        <li><strong>Cultivate Quiet Humility:</strong> Guard your heart against the intoxicating nature of public praise. Surround yourself with community that keeps you grounded, rather than feeding your ego.</li>
    </ul>
