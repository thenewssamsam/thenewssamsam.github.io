    <h2>The Latest Twist in the Lakers Legacy</h2>
    <p>For the millions of fans who call the Los Angeles Lakers a cornerstone of local identity, the past week has been a whirlwind of contradictory reports. Jeanie Buss, the long-time governor of the franchise, has officially denied that her family intends to sell their remaining 17.8% stake in the team. This statement came in response to widespread reports—initially cited by ESPN—suggesting that the Buss siblings were preparing to exit their ownership position entirely, following a move by majority shareholder Mark Walter to sell his portion to investor Josh Kushner and former Disney CEO Bob Iger.</p>
