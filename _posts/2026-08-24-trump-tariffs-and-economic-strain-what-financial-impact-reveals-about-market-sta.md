As consumers face ongoing cost-of-living pressures, understanding the underlying financial mechanics of these trade proposals is more critical than ever.</p>

<h2 id="financial-perspective">The Financial Impact: Who Actually Pays for Tariffs?</h2>

<p>From a strict financial and market perspective, trade policy is often misunderstood. A common misconception is that foreign governments pay the tariff. In reality, domestic importers remit the tariff tax to customs authorities upon entry. To protect profit margins, businesses routinely pass those added expenses on to the end consumer.</p>
