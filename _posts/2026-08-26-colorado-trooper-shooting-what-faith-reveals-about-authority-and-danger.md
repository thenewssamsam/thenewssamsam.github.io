    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; color: #2c3e50; border-bottom: 1px solid #eaeaea; padding-bottom: 5px;">What Happened on Highway 24</h2>
    <p>On the morning of August 25, 2026, a routine morning commute in Teller County, Colorado, transformed into a critical incident. According to the Colorado State Patrol (CSP), a trooper conducted a traffic stop around 7:15 a.m. along Highway 24, just west of Woodland Park. Without warning, the driver of the vehicle opened fire on the officer.</p>
    
    <p>Within minutes, a second trooper arrived in the area to assist, entering a nearby residential subdivision where the suspect had fled. Tragically, the suspect fired from a moving vehicle in the opposite direction, striking the second officer while he sat in his patrol car. Despite serious injuries, both troopers—veterans with 15 and 10 years of service respectively—managed to self-transport to a local hospital.</p>
