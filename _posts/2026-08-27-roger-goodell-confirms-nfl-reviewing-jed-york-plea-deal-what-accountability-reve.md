<section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened</h2>
    <p>San Francisco 49ers owner Jed York recently found himself at the center of unwanted headlines after entering a no-contest plea to charges of disorderly conduct and possession of criminal tools. The situation drew intense public scrutiny following the release of police body camera footage from a prostitution sting in East Palestine, Ohio. While York was notably absent from the NFL owners' meeting in Atlanta, his absence did little to quiet the discussion.</p>
    <p>When pressed by reporters, NFL Commissioner Roger Goodell addressed the unfolding situation directly, confirming that the league is reviewing the incident under its personal conduct policy. As Goodell stated via the Associated Press, the evaluation is ongoing, with further announcements expected once the review concludes. For a league that continually polices the behavior of its players and coaches, examining the conduct of a franchise owner places the spotlight squarely on the very top of the organizational hierarchy.</p>
