    <h3>The Erosion of Reality</h3>
    <p>In cases of severe psychosis, the brain’s ability to process sensory input and maintain a stable internal narrative can collapse. Individuals may experience delusions or hallucinations that feel entirely real, overriding their natural instincts and moral compass. Unlike typical criminal behavior, this state is characterized by a loss of agency—the person is not "choosing" their actions in a rational sense; they are responding to a distorted reality.</p>
