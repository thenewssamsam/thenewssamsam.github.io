  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened in the Courtroom?</h2>
    <p>In a courtroom setting charged with profound grief and complex legal arguments, the trial surrounding Lindsay Clancy has brought the harrowing realities of severe maternal mental illness back into the public eye. Testimonies from family members—including her former mother-in-law, Susan Clancy, and her mother, Paula Musgrove—paint a picture of a devoted mother whose mental health deteriorated rapidly following the birth of her youngest son, Callan, in 2022.</p>
    
    <p>According to court testimony, Clancy experienced severe insomnia, profound loss of appetite, anxiety, and distressing thoughts of self-harm and harm toward her children. Defence attorneys argue that she was suffering from acute postpartum psychosis at the time of the 2023 tragedy in Massachusetts, a condition characterized by a severe break from reality. Meanwhile, prosecutors have maintained that the actions were calculated. As the legal proceedings unfold, healthcare advocates emphasize that looking past the courtroom theatrics requires examining the systemic gaps in how modern medicine identifies and treats extreme psychiatric crises in new mothers.</p>
  </section>
