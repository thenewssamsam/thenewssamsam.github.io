<section id="what-happened-in-northwest-indiana">
<h2>What Happened in Northwest Indiana?</h2>
<p>When severe weather packing wind gusts up to 100 mph slammed into northwest Indiana on August 11, 2026, it left a trail of destruction that local infrastructure was ill-equipped to handle immediately. Nearly two weeks later, the physical scars of the storm remain visible on streets lined with downed trees, shattered utility poles, and homes sitting in darkness.</p>

<p>As of Sunday morning, more than 20,000 people across the region still lacked electricity, according to local power company NIPSCO. Hard-hit municipalities like Gary, Portage, Lake Station, and Munster have bore the brunt of the prolonged outage. In Gary alone, Mayor Eddie Melton reported that at least 17,000 citizens remained without power, forcing city schools to stay closed and prompting residents to seek shelter at relief centers and food distribution hubs.</p>
