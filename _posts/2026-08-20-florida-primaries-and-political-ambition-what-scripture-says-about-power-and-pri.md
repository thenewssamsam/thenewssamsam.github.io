    <h2>What Happened in the Primaries?</h2>
    <p>The political landscape across the United States shifted further toward a dramatic autumn showdown this week as voters in Florida, Alaska, Wyoming, and California cast their primary ballots. In Florida, U.S. Rep. Byron Donalds secured the Republican nomination for governor, moving a step closer to potentially becoming the state's first Black governor. He is set to face David Jolly, who captured the Democratic nomination after traversing a winding political path from Republican to independent to Democrat.</p>
    
    <p>Meanwhile, the primaries delivered notable surprises down the ballot. In a major upset, progressive state lawmaker Angie Nixon defeated retired Army Lt. Col. Alex Vindman in a Democratic Senate primary, overcoming a massive fundraising deficit. Other races featured dramatic twists, from redrawn congressional districts that pitted veteran lawmakers against each other—such as Debbie Wasserman Schultz securing a win in South Florida—to crowded ballots marked by celebrity candidates, former lawmakers seeking redemption after past scandals, and chaotic candidate fields.</p>
