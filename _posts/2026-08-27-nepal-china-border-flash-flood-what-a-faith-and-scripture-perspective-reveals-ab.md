<section id="what-happened">
  <h2>What Happened in the Himalayas</h2>
  <p>The disaster struck suddenly on Wednesday when a powerful landslide—potentially triggered by a glacier breaking off and crashing into the valley—blocked the Bhote Koshi river. The resulting surge of crushing floodwaters overwhelmed the mountainous border region, submerging villages like Timure in Nepal and hitting China’s Gyirong port. Vital transport links, guest houses, and tourist routes were instantly swallowed by the torrent.</p>
  
  <p>Among the missing are hundreds of foreign nationals, including travelers on spiritual pilgrimages to Mount Kailash, hikers, and local guides. Families are sharing heartbreaking final messages: a brother telling his sister "see you in the morning" before his guesthouse vanished; relatives frantically calling embassies for news of loved ones. As search and rescue operations continue in grueling conditions, the full human toll is only beginning to come to light.</p>
