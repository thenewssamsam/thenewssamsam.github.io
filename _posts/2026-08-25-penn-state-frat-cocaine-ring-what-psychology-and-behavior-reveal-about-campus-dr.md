    <h2>What Happened: The Anatomy of the Penn State Drug Ring</h2>
    <p>According to the Office of the Attorney General, the operation was far from a disorganized handful of college kids experimenting with illicit substances. Court documents describe a surprisingly formal hierarchy. At the top of the alleged food chain sat 24-year-old accounting major Agostino Abbatiello, labeled the kingpin who coordinated supply lines stretching down from New York City. Downstream logistics allegedly flowed through figures like Thomas Robinson and Lars Zeepvat, while younger fraternity pledges and members—spanning Delta Upsilon, Sigma Chi, and Acacia—were tasked with the unglamorous labor of weighing, packaging, and delivering cocaine.</p>
