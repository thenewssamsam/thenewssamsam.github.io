  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>The Results at a Glance</h2>
    <p>Appointed Senator Ashley Moody secured a dominant victory in the Republican primary, capturing roughly 79.6% of the vote. On the Democratic side, Angie Nixon emerged as the projected winner over challenger Alex Vindman, securing 56% of the vote. These outcomes set the stage for a high-stakes general election for the remaining two years of the term originally won by Marco Rubio in 2022.</p>

Historically, this mirrors the 19th-century political machines where established figures maintained control through party infrastructure, a phenomenon that remains highly relevant today.</p>
