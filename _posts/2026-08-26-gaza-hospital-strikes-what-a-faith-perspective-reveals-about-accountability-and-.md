  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section">
    <h2>What Happened at Nasser Hospital?</h2>
    <p>It has been one year since a series of Israeli tank shell strikes hit the Nasser Hospital complex in Gaza on August 25, 2025. The attack resulted in the deaths of 22 Palestinians, including rescue workers and prominent journalists who were documenting the conflict from one of the enclave's last functioning medical facilities. Among those killed were Reuters cameraman Hussam al-Masri and Mariam Dagga, a 33-year-old visual journalist who reported for The Associated Press and other international outlets.</p>
    
    <p>According to original reports, the initial strike killed al-Masri near a camera setup on the hospital roof. As colleagues and emergency personnel rushed to the scene to assist the wounded, a subsequent strike hit the area, compounding the casualties in what rights groups have questioned as a potential "double-tap" maneuver. In the wake of the tragedy, Israeli officials asserted that the target was a suspected militant observation point and later claimed six of those killed had ties to Hamas—though without providing verifying evidence at the time.</p>
