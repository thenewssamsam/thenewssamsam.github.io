    <h2>What Happened</h2>
    <p>Legal proceedings often hinge not just on the evidence presented in a courtroom, but on the individuals entrusted with weighing that evidence. Recently, the legal landscape surrounding Karmelo Anthony shifted when District Judge John Roach Jr. was recused from presiding over an upcoming hearing to determine whether the teen will receive a new trial. Stepping into this vacancy is retired Judge Michael Chitty.</p>
    <p>Chitty, who formerly served as the senior judge of the 422nd District Court in Kaufman County, Texas, is coming out of retirement to handle the motion. Bringing in a retired judge who has no prior connection to the specific trial decisions is a standard procedural mechanism designed to offer a fresh, impartial set of eyes on a complex situation. While the transition marks a procedural reset, it also brings renewed public attention to how legal systems attempt to guarantee fairness.</p>
