<section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened: The 2026 Near-Total Lunar Eclipse</h2>
    <p>Skywatchers across North and South America are preparing for a breathtaking celestial show this Thursday night as a near-total lunar eclipse graces the skies. Coming just two weeks after a total solar eclipse dazzled observers in southern Europe, this upcoming astronomical event offers millions an unobstructed view of Earth's shadow sweeping across the lunar surface.</p>
    
    <p>According to recent reports by NBC News science journalist Denise Chow, nearly the entire moon—specifically 96.2% at its peak—will be shrouded in darkness. Weather permitting, viewers throughout the continental United States, as well as parts of Europe and Africa, will have prime viewing conditions to witness the unfolding spectacle.</p>
