<p class="ncg-lead">When the Orlando Boat Show docks at the Orange County Convention Center from August 28 to 30, 2026, it brings more than 600 vessels and 85 brands under one roof for Central Florida families. But beyond the fiberglass hulls, bowriders, and pontoon boats lies a timeless lesson in how families make major financial and lifestyle decisions together.</p>

<section>
  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened at the Orlando Boat Show</h2>
  <p>The Marine Industry Association of Central Florida is hosting its annual showcase, offering aspiring and veteran boaters a massive display of watercraft just minutes away from pristine local lakes and the St. Johns River. Jeff Husby, president of the association, noted that the event is specifically designed with family participation in mind, featuring everything from children's fishing clinics to interactive stilt walkers.</p>
  
  <p>Florida remains America's leading state for recreational boating, boasting an economic impact of $31.3 billion and supporting over 100,000 jobs across more than 1.1 million registered vessels. With manufacturers eager to clear out remaining 2025 and 2026 inventory to make room for 2027 models, show organizers emphasize that attendees can secure valuable incentives, extended warranties, and exclusive pricing.</p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2 id="family-perspective">A Parenting &amp; Family Perspective on Shared Decisions</h2>
  <p>Buying a boat is rarely just about horsepower or hull design; it is an exercise in household communication and shared values. Husby explicitly encouraged entire families to engage in the purchasing process, emphasizing that seating arrangements, intended uses, and collective preferences matter just as much as the sticker price.</p>
