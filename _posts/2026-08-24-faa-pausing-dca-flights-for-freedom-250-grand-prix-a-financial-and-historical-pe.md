  <section id="what-happened" style="margin-bottom: 40px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.6rem; color: #24292e; border-left: 4px solid #0366d6; padding-left: 12px; margin-top: 0;">What Happened: The DCA Flight Pause Explained</h2>
    <p>The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) has officially announced a temporary suspension of flight operations at Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA) to accommodate the upcoming <strong>Freedom 250 Grand Prix</strong>. Flights are expected to pause on Sunday, August 23, from 10:15 a.m. to 1:15 p.m. local time.</p>
    
    <p>This unprecedented shutdown is directly tied to the logistics of the inaugural motorsports event, which celebrates America's 250th anniversary. The 1.7-mile IndyCar street circuit cuts through downtown Washington, D.C., running close to major federal landmarks including the Washington Monument, the U.S. Capitol, and the Smithsonian museums. Because the racing circuit and safety run-off zones occupy critical airspace corridors and ground areas adjacent to flight paths, aviation authorities deemed a temporary operational pause necessary for public safety.</p>

    <div style="background-color: #f6f8fa; border-left: 4px solid #0366d6; padding: 15px 20px; margin: 20px 0; border-radius: 4px;">
      <h3 style="margin-top: 0; color: #24292e; font-size: 1.1rem;">Quick Take for Travelers</h3>
      <p style="margin-bottom: 0;">If you are booked on a flight arriving at or departing from DCA during the late morning or early afternoon of August 23, expect schedule adjustments. Passengers are strongly advised to verify flight statuses directly with their respective airlines well in advance of heading to the terminal.</p>
