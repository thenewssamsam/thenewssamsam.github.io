    <h2>What Happened in Ukraine's Defense Strategy?</h2>
    <p>According to reports from the Kyiv Independent, President Zelensky disclosed on August 22, 2026, that Ukrainian forces successfully struck an Iskander ballistic missile launcher near the border. This operation represents a formalized pivot to a new tactical doctrine: rather than relying solely on Patriot surface-to-air systems—which are currently at or near empty—Ukraine is taking the fight directly to the mobile erector-launcher systems stationed inside Russian territory, such as in the Bryansk and Kursk oblasts.</p>
