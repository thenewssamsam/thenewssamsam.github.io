SpaceX has officially announced plans for "Starbase, Louisiana," a high-cadence launch facility backed by a staggering $100 billion capital investment commitment from Louisiana Governor Jeff Landry's administration. While bullish Wall Street analysts like JPMorgan's Doug Anmuth project SpaceX pursuing orbital compute toward roughly 75GW by late 2031 at reduced costs compared to Earth-bound facilities, the massive capital outlay introduces significant financial mechanics, execution risks, and broader market dependencies that deserve a closer financial look.</p>
