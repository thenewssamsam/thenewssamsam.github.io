  <section class="ncg-intro">

In recent high-level phone calls between Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty and his Iranian counterpart, Abbas Araghchi, both nations have signaled an urgent push to de-escalate tensions between Iran and the United States. While Washington and Tehran navigate a stalled diplomatic memorandum, Egypt is stepping into a traditional mediating role—not merely out of diplomatic altruism, but because the economic fallout of a choked Strait of Hormuz is battering regional supply chains.</p>
    <p>To understand why this unexpected diplomatic channel matters right now, we have to look past the immediate headlines of phone calls and mediation tracks.
