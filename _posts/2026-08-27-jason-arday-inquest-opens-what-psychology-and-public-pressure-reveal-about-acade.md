    <h2>What Happened: The Inquest Opens</h2>
    <p>The London inner south coroner’s court has formally opened an inquest into the death of Jason Arday, the former University of Cambridge professor whose sudden passing on August 14 sent shockwaves through the academic community. Arday, 41, was found unresponsive at his home in Battersea, south London, just nine days after resigning from his post amid a high-profile controversy involving allegations of plagiarism and career inconsistencies.</p>
    
    <p>During the brief 10-minute hearing attended by family members via video link, senior coroner Dr Julian Morris detailed that emergency services attended the scene and confirmed the death was not suspicious. Dr Morris also used the moment to urge the media to exercise mindfulness and respect for a grieving family. The coroner has adjourned the proceedings to a later date as investigations continue.</p>

    <p>The case has sparked fierce debate regarding the ferocious speed and intensity of modern public commentary. In his resignation statement, Arday expressed that the relentless accusations and public speculation had taken a profound toll on his well-being. His family has since condemned what they termed a relentless campaign of harassment and misinformation, highlighting the immense human cost behind a digital headline.</p>
