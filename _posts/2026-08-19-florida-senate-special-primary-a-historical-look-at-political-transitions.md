    <p>In the Republican primary, Ashley Moody, who was appointed to the seat, maintained a strong lead throughout the reporting, ultimately capturing approximately 80% of the vote. Her primary challengers, Chris Gleason and Ernest Rivera, trailed significantly. On the Democratic side, the race between Angie Nixon and military veteran Alex Vindman concluded with Nixon securing the nomination. These results clarify the candidates who will face off for the final two years of the Senate term.</p>
