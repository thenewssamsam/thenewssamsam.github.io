    <h1>Kyiv Missile Attack Kills 16: What Faith and Scripture Reveal About Endurance in Endless War</h1>
  </header>

  <p>When the night sky over Kyiv fractured under the glare of scores of incoming ballistic missiles and roaring drones, thousands of families once again fled into the deep subterranean safety of the city's subway stations. For millions watching the grinding conflict enter its fourth year, the sheer scale of the devastation—leaving at least 16 dead and dozens wounded across Ukraine—brings a heavy, soul-wearying question: How much longer can ordinary human beings endure this unrelenting storm of destruction?</p>

  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened in the Overnight Barrage</h2>

  <p>According to reports from Ukrainian officials and international news agencies, the nighttime aerial assault was among the heaviest seen this summer. Air raid alerts stretched deep into the morning hours Thursday as explosions echoed across Kyiv and surrounding regions. The Ukrainian air force reported that hits were recorded at 28 locations nationwide, involving a complex mix of ballistic missiles, cruise missiles, and jet-powered drones designed to overwhelm air defense capabilities.</p>
