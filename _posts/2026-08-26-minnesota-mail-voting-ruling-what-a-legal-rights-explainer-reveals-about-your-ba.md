While Congress and federal agencies hold certain oversight powers regarding federal elections, states traditionally retain primary authority over the "times, places, and manner" of holding elections under Article I, Section 4 of the U.S. Constitution. This tension between federal executive authority and state election administration is precisely why state attorneys general are pushing back.</p>
