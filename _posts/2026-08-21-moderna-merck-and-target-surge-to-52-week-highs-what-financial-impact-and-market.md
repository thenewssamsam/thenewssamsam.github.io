  <section id="what-happened" class="ncg-section" style="margin-bottom: 32px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; color: #1a365d; border-bottom: 2px solid #2b6cb0; padding-bottom: 6px; margin-top: 24px;">What Happened on Wall Street?</h2>
    <p>
      Stock markets experienced a wave of dramatic surges as three major companies—Moderna (MRNA), Merck & Co. (MRK), and Target (TGT)—hit new 52-week highs. The catalyst wasn't a broad macroeconomic policy shift or a general market rally, but rather fundamental, company-specific breakthroughs that instantly altered investor valuations.
    </p>
    <p>
      Moderna shares rocketed nearly 177% at the close on Wednesday, posting their steepest climb on record, after the biotech pioneer announced that its personalized mRNA cancer treatment, developed in partnership with Merck, met its primary goal in a pivotal late-stage melanoma study. Merck stock jumped more than 12%, reaching an annual high not seen since March 2009. Meanwhile, retail giant Target closed up more than 4% following a strong second-quarter earnings report and subsequent Wall Street price target upgrades.
    </p>
  </section>
