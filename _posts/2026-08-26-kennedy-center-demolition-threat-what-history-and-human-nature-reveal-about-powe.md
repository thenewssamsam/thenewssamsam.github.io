  <h2 id="what-happened">What Happened: The Ultimatum Over the Kennedy Center</h2>
  <p>According to reports from NBC News, the Department of Justice submitted a court filing on Monday introducing a dramatic twist to the ongoing saga surrounding the Kennedy Center renovations. DOJ attorney Brantley T. Mayers argued that without the administration's proposed overhaul—which includes adding President Donald J. Trump's name to the front of the building—the structure will deteriorate into an "unsafe, decrepit structure that will be required to be taken down."</p>
  
  <p>The filing suggests that if the current renovation and renaming plans are blocked by judicial intervention, an outdoor amphitheater overlooking the Potomac River might eventually replace the iconic building. The administration maintains that Trump's direct involvement, real estate expertise, and donor-marshalling capabilities represent the "sole hope for the Center’s financial survival." This legal maneuver comes amidst a heated battle featuring members of the Kennedy family, congressional Democrats, musicians, and federal judges who previously ruled that the board unlawfully added Trump's name to a memorial site dedicated exclusively to the slain 35th president.</p>
