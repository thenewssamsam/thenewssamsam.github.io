  <section id="what-happened">
    <h2>What Happened in Krasnodar?</h2>
    <p>In the early hours of August 24, a powerful strike shook the Russian city of Krasnodar, triggering a massive fire across a sprawling warehouse facility spanning approximately 9,000 square metres. According to preliminary reports from regional sources, the targeted property is closely linked to «CMM-K», a prominent wholesale supplier specializing in construction fastenings, tools, building materials, and heavy consumables like adhesives and chemical anchors.</p>
    
    <p>Simultaneously, confusion and conflicting reports emerged from local Russian channels, with some residents and social media channels claiming the affected complex housed operations connected to e-commerce giant Ozon. While independent verification remains limited—and exact ownership boundaries are still being sorted out by investigators—the incident fits into a broader, escalating pattern of disruption hitting Russian commercial infrastructure. Logistics hubs, distribution depots, and storage facilities have faced a wave of recent long-range drone operations, raising pressing questions about the vulnerability of civilian-commercial supply networks during wartime.</p>
