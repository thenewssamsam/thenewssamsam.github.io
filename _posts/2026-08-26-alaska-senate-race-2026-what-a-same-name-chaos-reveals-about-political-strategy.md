    <h2>What Happened in Alaska</h2>
    <p>The August 26, 2026, NBC News report details how Alaska's all-party primary system yielded a crowded and uniquely confusing ballot for one of the nation's most closely watched Senate seats. Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan, first elected in 2014, is fighting to retain his seat. Standing across the aisle is Democrat Mary Peltola, the historic former representative who narrowly lost her seat in 2024 after previously winning a statewide special election.</p>
    
    <p>However, the storyline consuming the state is the presence of Dan J. Sullivan, a former teacher who recently registered as a Republican after his previous affiliation with the Alaska Independence Party dissolved. Critics, including the incumbent senator and his Republican allies, have labeled the second Sullivan a potential "plant" engineered to confuse voters and dilute conservative support in a razor-thin battleground. State and federal prosecutors have taken notice, with a grand jury issuing subpoenas to investigate potential conspiracies.</p>
