<section id="what-happened">
<h2>What Happened: The Anatomy of a Polling Hoax</h2>
<p>The political information ecosystem moves at a breakneck speed, hungry for numbers that validate narratives. Median Strategies fed that appetite by releasing surveys that entered local TV newscasts, social media feeds, and digital reporting platforms without undergoing rigorous independent verification. The fabricated survey claimed Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass held a commanding 12-point lead over City Council member Nithya Raman, while another survey predicted a 20-point blowout for Wisconsin Democratic gubernatorial candidate Francesca Hong.</p>

<p>In reality, Bass edged Raman by a much narrower five points, and Hong was defeated in a razor-thin contest by David Crowley. When the truth finally surfaced, Median Strategies posted a brief statement on its website claiming the entire operation was designed to study "how purported polling information could enter and spread through the political information ecosystem without independent verification."</p>
