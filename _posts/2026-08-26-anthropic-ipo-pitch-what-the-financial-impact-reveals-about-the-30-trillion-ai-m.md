<p>From a financial perspective, pitching a massive Total Addressable Market (TAM) is a standard playbook for high-growth technology startups entering the public markets. However, the sheer size of a $30 trillion projection invites careful scrutiny regarding how those numbers are calculated and what they imply for everyday market participants.</p>

<p>When companies project multi-trillion-dollar opportunities, they are typically calculating the cumulative value of every industry their technology could theoretically disrupt or augment—from customer service and legal analysis to software engineering, healthcare diagnostics, and automated financial trading. The core financial assumption is that artificial intelligence will capture a slice of almost every human labor and digital workflow expenditure globally.</p>

<p>Yet, financial literacy teaches us to separate a total addressable market from actual captured revenue or sustainable net profit.
