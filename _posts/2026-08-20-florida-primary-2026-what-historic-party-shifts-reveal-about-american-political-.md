    <h2>What Happened in the 2026 Primaries</h2>
    <p>The 2026 primary election cycle delivered a series of sharp political jolts across the United States, with Florida taking center stage. In a closely watched Democratic Senate primary, state Representative and democratic socialist Angie Nixon secured a projected victory over Alex Vindman, positioning herself to face GOP Senator Ashley Moody in the upcoming November general election. Meanwhile, in the high-stakes race to succeed outgoing Governor Ron DeSantis, Republican Representative Byron Donalds claimed a projected primary win, setting up a contest against Democrat David Jolly.</p>

    <p>Beyond the headline gubernatorial and Senate contests, the ballots revealed deeper fractures within both major political parties. In Florida’s 7th District, Representative Cory Mills lost his GOP primary amid ongoing House Ethics Committee investigations. Simultaneously, across the country in Wyoming, State Senator Eric Barlow defeated the gubernatorial candidate preferred by former President Donald Trump, marking yet another primary setback for presidential endorsements this cycle. These outcomes suggest that contemporary American politics remains deeply fluid, driven less by top-down party discipline and more by localized insurgencies and shifting voter alignments.</p>
