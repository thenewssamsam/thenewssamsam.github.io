    <h2>A Financial and Economic Perspective on Defense</h2>
    <p>From a financial standpoint, modern air defense has long suffered from a devastating asymmetry. Firing a Patriot or a Tamir missile to destroy a drone costing a few thousand dollars is a financial trap. It is the equivalent of using a gold bar to swat a fly. Over time, wealthy nations and their allies risk bleeding their treasuries dry simply by defending themselves against inexpensive saturation attacks.</p>
